# Lion: required adventures (DRAFT 1, 40-minute den time)

Researched 2026-09-30 by the cubmaster-program agent, using the format of Wolf draft 3 and Bear draft 1. Every plan here is in our own words. Requirements are only summarized, so use the official page for the full text. Anything marked "(check)" still needs confirming. Nothing is in the app yet.

How these plans fit a Pack 569 den night:
- The pack does the opening ceremony together: flag, Pledge, Oath and Law. Each den then splits off for about 40 minutes.
- Den meetings here have no gathering or opening steps. Each one is den steps plus one short den closing. The minutes add up to 35–40 and never go over 40, so the timer can use them as they are.
- If the pack opening already covers a requirement, the meeting doesn't get a step for it. Instead it has a "Done at the pack opening:" line, and a leader confirms each scout took part. **No Lion requirement is completed at the pack opening.** Lion Bobcat has no Oath, Law, salute or handshake requirement. Lions still stand with their den and adult partners for the opening.
- Outings (the Mountain Lion walk) aren't normal den nights. They keep their own length and are marked "(outing, about N min)".
- "Reqs: N" means the step does real work on that requirement. "Reqs: N (set-up)" means the step only sets up or reinforces it and does not complete it.
- The guides don't tick requirements. Leaders record progress on the app's Advancement board afterward.
- Lions are in kindergarten, age 5–6. **Every Lion comes with an adult partner, who joins every activity, and there are no drop-offs.** Steps are written for Lion + adult-partner pairs, and the How lines give the partner a job. Activity chunks are 5–12 minutes. Scouts never have to read: leaders say everything, cards use pictures, and partners do any writing.
- Every den room needs two registered adults 21 or over, and there is no one-on-one contact. **An adult partner doesn't count as one of the two registered adults unless they are registered as a leader** (https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/yp-faqs/). If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
- The closing "handoff" for Lions means checking out each Lion with their adult partner by name. The pair leaves together.
- On 2026-09-30 I read all six official Lion adventure pages and the activity pages linked below. Plain WebFetch truncates scouting.org, so I used the r.jina.ai reader. I cross-checked everything against the Michigan Crossroads Council's Oct 2024 Lion requirements PDF (https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Lion-Adventure-Requirements.pdf). The six names and their category order match the app's `ADVENTURES['Lion'].required`.

## Bobcat (Lion)
- Character & Leadership · Meetings: 2 · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/bobcat-lion/ · Other sources: https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Lion-Adventure-Requirements.pdf, https://www.scoutingatl.org/LionAdvancement, https://www.scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/leader-resources/den-meeting-resources/lion-resources/ · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: This is the first adventure of the year, and scouting.org recommends starting with it. Lions and their adult partners meet the den, hear the Scout Law, practice being friendly, and share a "Do Your Best" moment with their partner. Lion Bobcat has only four requirements. Unlike Wolf and Bear, it has no Oath, Law, sign, salute or handshake requirement. The Cub Scout sign appears only as a Req 1 activity idea on the official page.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Get to know the other scouts in your den. (meeting)
  2. Adult partner or den leader reads you the Scout Law; show friendliness. (meeting or at home)
  3. Share a "Do Your Best" moment with your adult partner. (meeting or at home)
  4. Do the Parent's Guide youth-protection activities with a parent. (at home)
- Safety notes:
  - Req 4 is for families only. Leaders send the link and never run it themselves.
  - Two registered adults in the den room. Adult partners don't count unless they are registered. No one-on-one. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
  - Every Lion attends with an adult partner, so there are no drop-offs.
  - Beads for the zipper pull are small. Keep them on the table, keep an adult partner at every Lion's elbow, and don't let anything go in mouths.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Meet the Lion Den · 40 min
- Done at the pack opening: nothing toward Lion Bobcat. Lions stand with their den leader and adult partners for the Oath and Law, which is good practice, but no Lion requirement asks for it. Req 2 needs the Law read to the scout by their partner or the den leader, so that happens at Meeting 2.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, email families:
  - A welcome, and the adult-partner rule: every Lion comes with an adult partner who joins in, and there are no drop-offs.
  - The Parent's Guide link for Req 4.
  - The Lion Handbook & Adult Partner Guide.
  - The uniform basics: Lion T-shirt, navy belt and Lion cap. The neckerchief and slide are optional (see open questions).

  Confirm your second registered adult. Cut a poster-board den flag. Make name tags with each Lion's first name and a sticker picture, so Lions can find their own without reading.
- Supplies: sticker name tags for Lions and partners, soft ball or beanbag, poster-board den flag, crayons, markers, tape
- Tell parents before they leave: Please do the Parent's Guide activities with your Lion at home (Req 4), and tell the den leader when you're done. This week, help your Lion notice one time they did their best. They'll tell you about it at the next meeting.
1. **Roar Your Name** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "When it's your turn, stand up with your grown-up, give us your biggest lion roar, and tell us your name!"
   - How: 1) Pairs sit in a **circle**, with each Lion in front of their adult partner. 2) Each Lion **roars and says their name**, and the partner says their own name. 3) In round two, the Lion **rolls the ball** to someone and says that Lion's name. Partners may whisper hints. 4) Go until every Lion has **been named**.
   - Tip: **Shy Lion?** A wave counts. The partner says the name while the Lion does a tiny roar.
2. **Den Flag Handprints** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "This is our den flag, and every Lion gets a paw on it. Draw one thing you love inside your paw."
   - How: 1) Adult partners **trace their Lion's hand** on the flag. 2) Lions **color it** and draw one favorite thing, like a pet, a food or a toy. 3) Partners **write the first name** under the paw. 4) Each Lion **points to their paw** and tells the den what they drew.
   - Tip: **Crayons, not paint:** They're faster to clean up. The flag comes to every den meeting.
3. **The Cub Scout Sign: Quiet Lions** · den · 6 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "When a grown-up raises this sign, mouths close and ears open. Let's see how fast the Lion den can get quiet!"
   - How: 1) Show the **Cub Scout sign**: right arm straight up with two fingers raised (check the hand details against the Lion handbook). 2) Everyone **talks and wiggles**, and partners join in. 3) A leader raises the sign, and everyone **copies it and freezes**. 4) Count how fast the den gets quiet, and **beat the record**.
   - Tip: **Tie it to the opening:** Lions can hold up the sign during the Oath at the next pack opening.
4. **Find a Friend Who…** · den · 6 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Find a Lion who likes pizza, then hold their hand and roar!"
   - How: 1) The leader calls **"Find a friend who…"** with things like likes dogs, has a sister, or likes the color blue. 2) Lions and partners **walk quickly** to a match. 3) The pair **says the other Lion's name**. 4) Do **five or six rounds**.
   - Tip: **No match?** A Lion with no match joins the leader, and the leader's pair counts.
5. **Do Your Best Preview** · den · 3 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "Our motto is 'Do Your Best.' This week, look for a time you tried really hard, and tell your grown-up about it next time."
   - How: 1) Give **one quick example**, like trying a new food or zipping a coat. 2) Partners **agree to help** their Lion notice one.
   - Tip: **Tiny counts:** Putting on shoes all by yourself is a great one.
6. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Lions keep their den tidy. Every Lion picks up three things with their grown-up!"
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up** in pairs. 2) Give a one-minute **leader thought**. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their adult partner by name.
   - Tip: **Pickup:** If someone other than the adult partner is collecting a Lion, confirm it before that Lion leaves.
   - (at home) Req 4: families do the Parent's Guide activities.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Friendly Lions · 38 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, make a Scout Law poster with one simple picture beside each point. Buy large pony beads, chenille stems and zipper-pull clips. Make a pair list so every Lion makes a zipper pull for another Lion. On the night, put the den flag on the wall before the pack opening.
- Supplies: Scout Law picture poster, soft ball, large pony beads, chenille stems, clips, bead trays, den flag
- Tell parents before they leave: If you haven't finished the Parent's Guide activities (Req 4), please do them this week. At home, read the Scout Law to your Lion anytime; the poster picture is in the handbook (check the page).
1. **Scout Law Story Time** · den · 6 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "The Scout Law is a list of ways a Scout tries to be. I'll read it, and you say each word back to me like an echo."
   - How: 1) Lions sit **on the floor** in front of their partners. 2) The den leader **reads each point** slowly and points to its picture. 3) Lions **echo** each point. 4) Stop on **Friendly**: "What does a friendly Lion do?"
   - Tip: **Wiggly den?** Give each point a quick motion, like a thumbs-up for Helpful or a wave for Friendly.
2. **Compliment Toss** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Being friendly means making someone feel good. When you catch the ball, say something nice to the Lion who threw it."
   - How: 1) Everyone makes a **circle**. 2) The leader tosses first and gives a **compliment**. 3) The catcher gives a **compliment back** to the thrower. 4) Partners may **whisper ideas**, but the Lion says it.
   - Tip: **Model it first:** "I like how you shared the crayons" is better than "Nice shirt."
3. **Friendship Zipper Pull** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Friendly Lions make gifts for friends. Make a zipper pull for the Lion on your card, and they're making one for you!"
   - How: 1) Each Lion learns **who they're making it for**. 2) The adult partner **holds the chenille stem**, and the Lion picks and **threads beads**. 3) The partner **twists on the clip**. 4) Lions **give it to their friend** and say "Thank you" when they get theirs.
   - Tip: **Choking check:** Beads stay in the trays, and every partner watches. Wipe up spills right away.
4. **Do Your Best Whisper** · den · 7 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Whisper to your grown-up about a time you did your best. Grown-ups, listen, then draw a little picture of it together."
   - How: 1) Pairs **turn toward each other**. 2) The Lion **tells the story** to their partner. 3) Together they **draw it** in the handbook or on paper. 4) Volunteers **share** with the den.
   - Tip: **The requirement is telling the partner.** Sharing with the whole den is optional. Note who shared with their partner.
   - (at home) Req 3 can be finished at home with the adult partner.
5. **Friendly Goodbye & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Friendly Lions say goodbye nicely. Give a friend a high five, then let's clean up."
   - How: 1) Everyone gives **high fives**. 2) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their adult partner by name.
   - Tip: **Beads:** Count the trays back in and sweep the floor for strays.

## King of the Jungle (Lion)
- Citizenship · Meetings: 2 · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/king-of-the-jungle/ · Other sources: https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Lion-Adventure-Requirements.pdf, https://scoutermom.com/19193/lion-king-of-the-jungle-adventure/, https://www.scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/2025-holiday-letters-for-military/, https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/healthsafety/pdf/680-027.pdf, https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/healthsafety/pdf/680-028.pdf · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: For a Lion, citizenship starts at home. Lions draw the people they live with, pick a family job with their partner, hear an older adult talk about being a citizen, and help with a service project. All four requirements are required. Scouter Mom estimates 1–2 meetings plus the home job and the older-adult talk.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Draw or photograph the people you live with. (meeting or at home)
  2. With your adult partner, pick a family job; do it once. (chosen in the meeting; done at home)
  3. Talk with a grandparent or older adult about citizenship. (meeting with a guest, or at home)
  4. Take part in a service project. (meeting or outing)
- Safety notes:
  - The official page points to the Service Project Planning Checklist and SAFE Project Tool Use (links above). It notes that the tool-use guide doesn't let Lions and Tigers use paint brushes on service projects, though painting in arts and crafts is fine.
  - Off-site service needs two registered adults, permission, transport planning and the council's outing process (check). If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
  - A guest speaker is never alone with scouts. Both registered adults stay in the room.
  - Family drawings: every kind of household counts. Don't ask about who isn't in the picture.
  - Cards for the military: first names only.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · My Family Team · 36 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, book the Meeting 2 guest. It could be a Lion's grandparent, another older adult, or a veteran; the official page lists all three. Ask for a 5-minute talk, in kid words, on what being a good citizen means to them. Print picture job cards: feed a pet, set the table, pick up toys, water plants, match socks, carry in groceries. Make a take-home job chart for each Lion with seven boxes for stickers.
- Supplies: paper, crayons, picture job cards, take-home job charts, stickers, sticky notes
- Tell parents before they leave: Your Lion picked a family job tonight. Please help them do it at least once this week (Req 2). The chart has boxes for stickers. If you'd rather use a family photo for Req 1, that's fine too. Next time we have a guest and make cards for service members.
1. **Who Lives at My House** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Draw everybody who lives in your home with you. Pets can be in it too!"
   - How: 1) Lions **draw** with crayons. 2) The adult partner **writes names** under each person as the Lion says them. 3) Each Lion **holds up the picture** and says one person's name.
   - Tip: **Every family counts.** Households look different, so never ask who's missing. Lions who'd rather not draw can bring a family photo next time.
2. **Helper Charades** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 2 (set-up)
   - Say: "Families are teams, and team members help. Act out a job with your grown-up, and let the den guess it!"
   - How: 1) Each pair **draws a picture card**. 2) The pair **acts it out** together with no talking. 3) The den **guesses** the job.
   - Tip: **Big and silly:** Partners who ham it up get the Lions laughing.
3. **Pick My Family Job** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 2 (set-up)
   - Say: "Now pick the job you'll really do at home to help your family team."
   - How: 1) Pairs **choose one job** together from the cards. 2) The Lion **glues or tapes the picture** onto their job chart. 3) The partner **writes the job** underneath.
   - Tip: **Keep it doable:** Pick something a 5-year-old can do this week without help.
4. **Question for Our Guest** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "A citizen is someone who belongs to a community and helps it. Next time a special guest is coming. What would you like to ask them?"
   - How: 1) Take **quick ideas**, like "How do you help our town?" 2) Partners **write their Lion's question** on a sticky note. 3) The leader **collects them**.
   - Tip: **Keep them:** Hand the notes to the guest ahead of time.
5. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Tonight you practiced being a helper. Let's help clean up this room!"
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up** in pairs. 2) Make sure each pair has its **drawing and job chart**. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Home note:** Point partners to the job chart.
   - (at home) Req 2: do the family job at least once. Req 1 can be a photo instead of the drawing.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Citizens Help Out · 40 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, confirm the guest and send them the Lions' questions.
  - In the meeting (this plan): holiday cards for military personnel, the nationwide project listed on the official page. The page says to mail them to the Scouting America National Service Center by the ship-by date, **[date]** (2026: Nov 6), so this meeting must come before then. The 2025 project page gives the address as 10,000 for the Troops, Scouting America, 1325 West Walnut Hill Lane, Irving, TX 75015-2079. The date is a ship-by deadline. Each card carries the scout's first name only, plus the town, state and pack number, and is no bigger than a letter sheet folded in half. **The Lion den mails its own cards.** Check the address for 2026.
  - Off-site option: a council Scouting for Food event (check the NEGA dates). Complete the Service Project Planning Checklist. Collect permission slips and health forms, confirm two registered adults, and follow the council's outing process (check). The off-site version is an outing: change the header to "(outing, about 60 min)".
- Supplies: cardstock folded into cards, crayons, markers, stickers, envelope or box for shipping, the Lions' questions
- Tell parents before they leave: Adults will mail the cards this week. If your Lion hasn't talked with a grandparent or other older adult about being a citizen (Req 3), you can do it at home in person or on a call. Have you done the family job yet (Req 2)?
1. **Meet Our Guest** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Our guest is going to tell us what being a good citizen means to them. Listen hard, then ask your question!"
   - How: 1) The **guest talks** for about 5 minutes. 2) Lions **ask their questions**, with partners helping as needed. 3) Each Lion tells their partner **one thing they heard**. 4) Everyone thanks the guest with a **big lion roar**.
   - Tip: **No guest?** Lions do Req 3 at home with a grandparent or older neighbor. The den goes straight to the cards and adds a longer wiggle break.
2. **Jungle Wiggle** · den · 3 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Stand up! Stomp like an elephant, swing like a monkey, and pounce like a lion!"
   - How: 1) Call out **animal moves**. 2) Partners **join in**. 3) Finish with a **quiet sit-down**.
   - Tip: **Use it anytime** Lions start to melt.
3. **Cards for Service Members** · den · 15 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Some grown-ups serve our country far from home, even on holidays. Let's make them cards so they know we're thinking of them."
   - How: 1) Lions **draw a picture** on the front. 2) The adult partner **writes the message** the Lion says. 3) The Lion **writes or traces their first name**. 4) Partners add the **pack number, city and state**.
   - Tip: **First names only,** and no photos of scouts' faces inside the cards. Use markers and crayons; no paint brushes on a service project.
4. **Why We Help** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Who are our cards for, and how will they feel when they open them?"
   - How: 1) **Circle up**. 2) Each Lion gives a **one-word feeling**. 3) Note who **took part** for the Advancement board.
   - Tip: **Prompt:** "They'll feel ____."
5. **Den Roar & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Loudest den roar for the people we helped! Then clean up."
   - How: 1) Give the **den roar**. 2) **Collect** the cards for mailing. 3) Clean up and **check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Off-site:** Lions thank the host, everyone uses **hand sanitizer**, and you do a **headcount** before check-out.

## Lion's Pride (Lion)
- Family & Reverence · Meetings: 1 · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/lions-pride/ · Other sources: https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Lion-Adventure-Requirements.pdf, https://www.scouting.org/awards/religious-awards/ · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: Families lead the faith parts. The den makes the cheerful card (Req 3) and gives Lions time for the Req 1 drawing after their family talk. The official page says Req 1 may be done at a den meeting if parents know ahead and can opt out. A Lion can also earn this adventure by completing a religious emblem of the family's choosing.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Talk about family faith traditions with a parent; draw a favorite celebration. (at home; the drawing can be made in the meeting)
  2. Attend a religious service or other family reverence gathering. (at home)
  3. Make and give a cheerful card or drawing to someone you love. (made in the meeting; given at home)
- Safety notes:
  - Leaders do not teach or lead religious content. The official page calls the program nonsectarian.
  - Every family's traditions count, including non-religious ones. No Lion has to share beliefs aloud.
  - Families can opt out of the in-den drawing and do it at home.
  - The official page lists attending a Veterans Day event with the den as one Req 2 activity (see open questions).

### Meeting 1 of 1 · Cards and Celebrations · 38 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, send families a note:
  - Reqs 1 and 2 are family-led at home.
  - Before this meeting, please talk with your Lion about your family's traditions and a favorite holiday or celebration. The drawing at the meeting is based on that talk.
  - If you'd rather your Lion draw it at home, tell the den leader, and your Lion will make a second card instead.
  - A religious emblem is another way to earn the adventure (link above).
  - Our chartered organization is a local church. Going to one of its services is one option for Req 2, never a requirement. Any faith's service or reverence gathering counts, and each family chooses its own.

  Fold cardstock into cards and set out sticker sheets.
- Supplies: folded cardstock, crayons, markers, stickers, drawing paper, envelopes or bags for taking cards home
- Tell parents before they leave: Your Lion made a cheerful card for someone they love. Please help them give it this week, because giving it finishes Req 3. Reqs 1 and 2 happen at home and are led by your family.
1. **Someone I Love** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "Think of someone you love. It could be a grandma, a friend, a neighbor, anyone! You're going to make them a happy card."
   - How: 1) Each Lion **tells their partner** who the card is for. 2) A few volunteers **tell the den**.
   - Tip: **Any person counts.** Lions can pick a pet sitter, a teacher or a cousin.
2. **Cheerful Card** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Make it happy! Bright colors, stickers, and a picture that will make them smile."
   - How: 1) Lions **decorate the front**. 2) The adult partner **writes the message** the Lion says inside. 3) The Lion **writes or traces their name**. 4) Put it in a **bag with the Lion's name**.
   - Tip: **The words are the Lion's.** Partners write them down but don't change them.
3. **Happy Dance** · den · 3 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Cards done! Everybody do a happy dance with your grown-up!"
   - How: 1) Play a **short song**. 2) Everyone **dances**. 3) Everyone **freezes** when the music stops.
   - Tip: **Freeze-dance** helps wiggly Lions settle for the next step.
4. **My Family Celebration** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Draw your favorite holiday or celebration that your family talked about at home."
   - How: 1) Lions **draw**. 2) The partner **labels it** if the Lion wants. 3) Opt-out Lions **make a second card** instead.
   - Tip: **Leaders don't suggest which celebration to pick.** If a family missed the home talk, the Lion can draw now and finish the talk at home.
   - (at home) The rest of Req 1 is the family talk.
5. **Gallery Walk (optional)** · den · 3 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "If you want to, hold up your picture and tell us one thing about it."
   - How: 1) Ask for **volunteers only**. 2) Everyone gives a **silent cheer**. 3) Thank every **presenter**.
   - Tip: **No one has to share beliefs.** Don't call on anyone.
6. **Kind Words & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Families celebrate different things, and that's great. Let's clean up with kind hands."
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 2) Check that every card is **in its bag**. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Home note:** Remind partners that giving the card finishes Req 3.
   - (at home) Req 2 with family, plus giving the card.

## Mountain Lion (Lion)
- Outdoors · Meetings: 2 (one den meeting plus one outing) · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/mountain-lion/ · Other sources: https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Lion-Adventure-Requirements.pdf, https://scoutermom.com/19077/mountain-lion-adventure-cub-scout/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/does-my-whistle-work/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/reverse-hide-and-seek/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/grab-the-six-essentials/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/safety-moments/weather-related-safety/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/safety-moments/anaphylaxis/ · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: The den night covers the Six Essentials, S.A.W. and wild versus domesticated animals. On the outing, each Lion and adult partner walk for 20 minutes or more, spotting natural and man-made things. S.A.W. means **Stay, Answer, Whistle**: stay put, answer when your name is called, and blow three short whistle blasts (official activity pages). The walk can be done with the den, the pack or family, so a Lion who misses it can make it up with family.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Name the Six Essentials and show what each is for. (meeting)
  2. Walk 20+ minutes with your Essentials; spot natural and man-made things. (outing; with den, pack or family)
  3. Learn what S.A.W. means. (meeting; practiced on the outing)
  4. Name local animals; sort them into domesticated and wild. (meeting)
- Safety notes:
  - The walk is an outing: two registered adults, permission, and the council outing process (check). If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
  - The official page asks leaders to:
    - use the SAFE Checklist
    - take Hazardous Weather training
    - review every scout's health record
    - use the buddy system and active supervision
  - If a scout carries an EpiPen, at least one other adult must know how to use it (official page).
  - Each Lion's buddy is their adult partner, and pairs stay together the whole walk.
  - Postpone for lightning. Plan for Georgia heat and water. Do a tick check afterward. Don't touch wildlife.
  - Restrooms: a parent takes their own Lion. Otherwise, buddies go together and an adult waits outside. There is no one-on-one.
  - Trail food: check allergies.
  - Whistles are for practice and emergencies only.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Ready to Explore · 38 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before:
  - Build a demo Six Essentials kit (first-aid kit, water bottle, flashlight, trail food, sun protection, whistle). This list comes from Scouter Mom and the "Grab the Six Essentials" page; see wolf.md question 17 (check).
  - Get one whistle per Lion, or ask families to bring the whistle from their kit.
  - Print animal picture cards: dog, cat, horse, cow, chicken, goat, deer, squirrel, raccoon, rabbit, opossum, fox, turtle, owl.
  - Make two hula-hoop "homes," one labeled with a house picture and one with a tree picture.
  - Set the walk date and place, and have permission slips ready.
- Supplies: demo kit, a towel to cover it, whistles, animal picture cards, 2 hula hoops with picture labels, permission slips, walk info sheets with a packing list
- Tell parents before they leave: The Lion walk is [date/place]. Please return the permission slip and health form. Help your Lion pack their own Six Essentials, and wear closed-toe shoes and weather-right clothes.
1. **Six Essentials Charades** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "These six things go on every Cub Scout hike. I'll show you what each one does, then you and your grown-up act one out and we'll guess!"
   - How: 1) **Hold up each item** and show its use. 2) Each pair **picks one** secretly. 3) The pair **acts it out**, for example drinking water or putting on sunscreen. 4) The den **guesses**, and the Lion **holds it up** and says what it's for.
   - Tip: **Note Req 1** once each Lion has named all six and shown one use.
2. **What's Missing?** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Look hard at all six. Close your eyes… Now, which one is missing?"
   - How: 1) Lions **study the kit**. 2) Eyes shut while a leader **hides one** under the towel. 3) Lions **name the missing item** and say its use. 4) Play **four rounds**.
   - Tip: **Partners stay quiet** and let the Lion answer.
3. **S.A.W. Whistle Practice** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "If you ever get lost, remember S.A.W.: Stay right where you are. Answer when you hear your name. Whistle three short blasts."
   - How: 1) Teach a motion for each: **Stay** (hug a tree), **Answer** (hand to your ear), **Whistle** (three fingers up). 2) Every Lion **says S.A.W.** with the motions. 3) One Lion at a time blows **three short blasts**. 4) Remind Lions that the whistle is **only for emergencies**.
   - Tip: **Loud room:** Partners may cover their Lion's ears between turns. Note who can say what S.A.W. means.
4. **Wild or Home?** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Some animals live with people and some live wild. Grab an animal card and run it to the right home: the house or the tree!"
   - How: 1) Spread the **cards face down**. 2) Each Lion and partner **flip one** and name the animal. 3) The Lion **runs it to a hoop**. 4) The den **checks** each one and asks, "Have you seen one near your house?"
   - Tip: **Tricky ones:** A rabbit or turtle can be a pet or wild. Say both answers are OK and ask why.
5. **Walk Briefing & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Next time we explore outside! Bring your Six Essentials and your whistle."
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 2) Hand each **partner** the info sheet and permission slip. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Collect the whistles** if they're den-owned.
   - (at home) Lions pack their own Six Essentials with their partner.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Lion Explorer Walk (outing, about 45 min)

Collect permission slips and health forms. Confirm two registered adults. Follow the council outing process (check). The adult in charge completes Hazardous Weather training and the SAFE Checklist. Check the forecast the day before and that morning. Postpone for lightning.

- Tell parents before they leave: Please do a tick check tonight. If your Lion missed the walk, you can do a 20-minute family walk with your Six Essentials instead, and tell the den leader what you spotted.
1. **Check-In & Essentials Check** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 2 (set-up)
   - Say: "Show me your Six Essentials and your shoes. Are you ready to explore?"
   - How: 1) **Sign in** each pair and collect any missing forms. 2) Each Lion **shows** their Essentials. 3) **Check shoes and clothes**. 4) Everyone takes a **restroom stop**, following the restroom rule.
   - Tip: **Missed Meeting 1?** Have that Lion name each Essential and show its use here, which covers Req 1.
2. **S.A.W. Reminder** · den · 3 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "What do we do if we get lost? Show me the motions!"
   - How: 1) Everyone does **Stay, Answer, Whistle**. 2) The rule is **one partner, one Lion**, and they stay together. 3) One adult leads at the **front**, and one sweeps at the **back**.
   - Tip: **Needs a second registered adult.** The sweep never lets a pair fall behind.
3. **I Spy: Natural or Man-Made, Part 1** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Natural things come from nature. Man-made things were built by people. With your grown-up, spy as many of each as you can!"
   - How: 1) **Walk** at Lion pace. 2) Pairs play **I Spy**: the Lion spots something and says "natural" or "man-made." 3) The partner **colors a box** on the sheet. 4) Stop to **look and listen** for 30 seconds.
   - Tip: **Start the clock here:** Req 2 needs 20 or more minutes of walking and exploring.
4. **I Spy: Natural or Man-Made, Part 2** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Head back, explorers. Can you find something natural and man-made right next to each other?"
   - How: 1) **Finish the loop**. 2) Take a **water break**. 3) Pairs **keep spotting**. 4) Everyone **packs out** their own trash.
   - Tip: **Don't cut it short:** Parts 1 and 2 must add up to 20 or more minutes.
5. **Share Circle** · den · 4 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Tell us one natural thing and one man-made thing you found."
   - How: 1) **Circle up** at the start point. 2) Each Lion **names two things**. 3) Note who finished **Req 2**.
   - Tip: **Shy Lion?** They can point to the pictures on their sheet.
6. **Headcount & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Last headcount! Great exploring, Lions."
   - How: 1) Count **every pair**. 2) Do a **trash sweep**. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Tick reminder:** Say it to every partner at check-out.

## Fun on the Run (Lion)
- Personal Fitness · Meetings: 2 · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/fun-on-the-run/ · Other sources: https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Lion-Adventure-Requirements.pdf, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/bubbles-good-clean-fun/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/snack-time/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/lion-freeze-tag/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/animals-sleep-just-like-me/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/safety-moments/food-allergies/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/safety-moments/anaphylaxis/ · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: Lions learn food groups, hand-washing, 20 minutes of being active, and ways to rest. The official page places every activity in the den or at home. Two meetings are needed because 20 active minutes plus rest fill one night.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Name the five food groups. (meeting)
  2. Practice hand-washing and say when to wash. (meeting)
  3. Be active for 20 minutes. (meeting)
  4. Practice ways to rest. (meeting)
- Safety notes:
  - Collect food allergies from health forms and families before the snack, and ask about religious or cultural food needs (official page).
  - If a scout carries an EpiPen, at least one other adult must know how to use it (official page).
  - Follow food safety: keep cold food in a cooler and wash hands first.
  - Adults do all cutting. Cut food small for 5-year-olds, and skip whole grapes and hard round foods.
  - Freeze tag uses light tags from the waist up only (official activity page).
  - Bubbles make floors slippery, so do them outside or over a towel.
  - Take water breaks, and check the heat if you're outdoors.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Clean Hands, Happy Plate · 38 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, collect allergy and diet information and choose safe foods, one from each group. Examples: banana or clementine (fruit), cucumber rounds (vegetable), whole-grain crackers (grain), cheese cubes (dairy), and a protein such as turkey slices. Confirm a sink you can use. Print food picture cards and five group signs (fruits, vegetables, grains, protein, dairy). On the day, adults pre-cut food and pack cold items in a cooler.
- Supplies: bubbles and wands (one per pair), hand soap, paper towels, a hand-washing picture poster, food picture cards, 5 hoops or mats with group signs, plates, napkins, wipes, cooler and ice packs, water
- Tell parents before they leave: Ask your Lion to show you how to wash hands while singing "Happy Birthday" twice. Next meeting is very active, so send sneakers and a water bottle.
1. **Bubble Pop** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 2 (set-up)
   - Say: "Grown-ups, blow bubbles! Lions, pop as many as you can. Now look at your hands: what's on them?"
   - How: 1) **Partners blow bubbles**, and Lions pop them. 2) Lions **rub their fingers** and feel the slippery soap. 3) Explain that soap is what **lifts germs off**.
   - Tip: **Outside or over a towel,** because soapy floors are slippery.
2. **Scrub Like a Lion** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Wet, soap, scrub palms, between fingers, thumbs and fingertips, rinse, dry. Scrub while you sing 'Happy Birthday' two times!"
   - How: 1) Show the **steps** on the poster. 2) Pairs take turns at the **sink**, and the partner sings along. 3) Back in the circle, hold up picture cards: **"Wash or not?"** (after the bathroom, before eating, after petting a dog, after coughing). 4) Each Lion **names one time to wash**.
   - Tip: **Note Req 2** once each Lion has washed and named a time to wash.
3. **Wrong Group!** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Five food groups, five homes. Run each food to its home. If a grown-up puts one in the wrong home, shout 'Wrong group!'"
   - How: 1) Name the **five groups** at their signs. 2) Lions **run picture cards** to the right hoop. 3) A leader sneaks in **wrong ones** on purpose. 4) Lions **fix them**.
   - Tip: **Partners cheer,** and Lions run.
4. **Happy Plate Snack** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Build a plate with one food from each group. Say the group before you take a bite!"
   - How: 1) Hands are **already clean**, so go straight to the snack table. 2) The partner **holds the plate** while the Lion picks. 3) The Lion **names each group** and tastes. 4) Ask, "**Which group was missing** from your plate?"
   - Tip: **A tiny bite counts.** Never force it. Only serve foods cleared for allergies.
5. **Wipe Down & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Tell your grown-up your favorite food group, then let's clean up."
   - How: 1) **Wipe tables** in pairs. 2) Throw away **leftovers**. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Leftovers:** Don't send opened food home with other families.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Move and Rest · 38 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, book a gym or open floor, or a field with daylight. Remind families to send sneakers and water. Bring mats or towels, calm music, a speaker and a timer. Set four cones for the tag area.
- Supplies: 4 cones, timer, speaker with a dance song and calm music, mats or towels, water
- Tell parents before they leave: Ask your Lion about owls and dogs and when they sleep. Lions need about 10 hours of sleep a night (official activity page), so try a calm-down song at bedtime this week.
1. **Warm-Up & Lion Says** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Lion says, reach for the sky! Lion says, touch your toes! Jump! Oops, Lion didn't say!"
   - How: 1) Do a **quick stretch**: arm circles, toe touches, big reach. 2) Play **Lion Says**, with partners playing too. 3) A Lion who moves by mistake becomes a **helper** who spots the next mistake. 4) Point out the **boundaries**.
   - Tip: **Start the clock:** Req 3's 20 minutes start here.
2. **Lion Freeze Tag** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "If you're tagged, you freeze like a statue. A friend can unfreeze you with a tap!"
   - How: 1) Pick **two taggers**. 2) Only **light tags, waist up**. 3) Frozen players are **unfrozen** by a friend's tap. 4) **Switch taggers** every 2–3 minutes and take a **water break**.
   - Tip: **Too rough?** A player sits out one round, per the official activity.
3. **Lion Dance Party** · den · 4 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Dance like a lion! When the music stops, freeze!"
   - How: 1) Play **music**. 2) Everyone **dances**. 3) Everyone **freezes** when it stops.
   - Tip: **Note Req 3** now. Steps 1–3 add up to 22 active minutes.
4. **Animals Sleep Too** · den · 6 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Owls sleep in the day and wake up at night. Dogs sleep at night like you. Show me a sleepy owl!"
   - How: 1) Name **night animals and day animals**. 2) Lions **act out** each one waking up and falling asleep. 3) Lions tell their partner **what time they go to bed** and how they feel in the morning.
   - Tip: **Handbook page:** The official activity uses a Lion handbook page; do it at home if time is short (check the page number).
5. **Sleepy Lions** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Lie down like a sleepy lion. Hand on your tummy. Breathe in slow… and out slow."
   - How: 1) **Dim the lights** and play **calm music**. 2) Lions lie on mats next to their partner. 3) Do **belly breathing**, then "melt" toes, legs, arms and face. 4) Ask, "**How does your body feel?**"
   - Tip: **Note Req 4** once each Lion has tried two rest methods.
6. **Slow Wake-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Sleepy lions, wake up slowly and stretch. Let's pick up our mats."
   - How: 1) Everyone **sits up slowly**. 2) **Roll up the mats** and stack the cones. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Water bottles:** Check the water line so no bottle gets left.

- Summary: Parents lead the Protect Yourself Rules video at home. - Requirements (own words):
  1. With a parent's OK, watch the Lion Protect Yourself Rules video. (at home)
  2. Show Shout, Run, Tell with your adult partner. (meeting, when the partner is the parent; otherwise at home)

  - Send the official parent notification (linked above) before anything else.
  - The video is 13 minutes and meant to be watched by the parent with their scout (activity page). Don't show it in the den.

  - The "Five Trusted Adults" page goes home for the family. The den doesn't ask Lions to name trusted adults aloud.
  - 911 practice uses pretend phones only, never a real phone.
  - Street practice happens only indoors or on blacktop closed to cars, with cones across any entrance. No real traffic.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Shout, Run, Tell and Call for Help · 38 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, email families the parent notification and the video link, and ask them to watch it with their Lion before this meeting (Req 1). Leaders review the Protect Yourself Rules and the disclosure steps. Make one cardboard or toy phone per pair, or use the handbook's pretend phone page (check the page). Print picture cards: a house fire, someone who won't wake up, a car crash, someone choking, a lost toy, a scraped knee, spilled juice.
- Supplies: pretend phones, emergency picture cards, cones, crayons
- Tell parents before they leave: If you haven't yet, please watch the Protect Yourself Rules video with your Lion (Req 1). If you weren't your Lion's partner tonight, please practice Shout, Run, Tell at home (Req 2). The handbook's "Five Trusted Adults" page is for you to do together at home. Help your Lion learn your street address. Next meeting may be outdoors, so wear sneakers.
1. **Shout, Run, Tell Relay** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 2 (when the partner is the parent)
   - Say: "If something ever feels wrong, you Shout 'No!', Run away, and Tell a grown-up you trust. Let's practice with your grown-up!"
   - How: 1) Lions line up **about 10 yards** from their partners. 2) On "Go," each Lion **shouts their own words**, like "No!" or "Stay away!" 3) They **run to their partner**. 4) They say, **"I need to tell you something."** Every Lion takes **two turns**.
   - Tip: **Keep it general.** Don't discuss the video's details, because that talk belongs to parents. If a Lion discloses something, follow the disclosure steps.
   - Note who finished Req 2: Lions whose partner tonight is their parent. The rest finish it at home with a parent.
   - (at home) Req 1 (the video) is done at home with a parent.
2. **Is It a 911?** · den · 7 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "911 is for big emergencies, when someone is badly hurt or in danger. I'll show a picture. Jump up if it's a 911, sit down if it isn't!"
   - How: 1) Show each **picture card**. 2) Lions **jump or sit**. 3) For each "sit," ask, **"Who could help instead?"**
   - Tip: **Never a joke:** 911 is only for real emergencies, even on a pretend phone.
3. **Pretend 911 Call** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Your grown-up is the 911 helper. Dial 9-1-1 on your pretend phone and answer their questions."
   - How: 1) Each pair takes a **pretend phone**. 2) The Lion **dials 9-1-1**. 3) The partner, as the **operator**, asks: "What's the emergency? Where are you? Who's hurt?" 4) Do **two different scenarios**.
   - Tip: **Address privacy:** The Lion says their address only to their own partner, not to the den. Note who finished Req 3.
4. **Helper Moves** · den · 4 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Who comes when you call 911? Show me a firefighter spraying a hose! A police officer directing traffic! A paramedic carrying a stretcher!"
   - How: 1) Call out **helpers**. 2) Lions and partners **act them out**.
   - Tip: **Wiggle break** before the closing.
5. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Great job, safe Lions. Let's put the phones away."
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 2) Hand each partner the **home reminder**. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Partner in hand:** Give the note to the adult, not the Lion.
   - (at home) The Five Trusted Adults page with a parent.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Safe Steps · 37 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, choose the space: a gym with painter's tape, or blacktop closed to cars, in daylight (check with the site). Tape or chalk a street with a crosswalk, a curb line and a small parking lot with 4–6 spaces. Make red and green signs and paper-plate steering wheels. Put cones across any entrance.
- Supplies: painter's tape or sidewalk chalk, red and green signs, paper-plate steering wheels, cones, water
- Tell parents before they leave: On your next walk, let your Lion lead the "Stop, look left, right, left" at a real crossing while you hold hands. If you haven't finished Reqs 1–2 at home, please do them this week and tell the den leader.
1. **Red Light, Green Light** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 4 (set-up)
   - Say: "Green means go, red means stop! Walk, don't run, and freeze the second you see red."
   - How: 1) Lions and partners line up **side by side**. 2) The leader flips the **red and green signs**. 3) Anyone who moves on red **goes back three steps**. 4) Play **three rounds**.
   - Tip: **Connect it:** "Cars stop on red. We watch for cars even when it's our turn."
2. **Stop, Look, Listen Crosswalk** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Hold your grown-up's hand. Stop at the curb, look left, right, left, and listen. Then walk across. Keep looking while you cross!"
   - How: 1) Pairs line up at the **curb line**. 2) The Lion **leads the steps** out loud while holding the partner's hand. 3) A leader acts as the **walk signal**. 4) Each pair **crosses three times**, once with a "car" that doesn't stop.
   - Tip: **Official safety point:** Seeing a car doesn't mean the driver sees you, so keep looking.
3. **Make-Believe Parking Lot** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Some of you are cars and some are walkers. Walkers, hold your grown-up's hand and stay to the side. Cars, drive slowly!"
   - How: 1) Half the Lions are **cars** with steering wheels, and their partners walk beside them. 2) The other pairs are **walkers**. 3) Cars **back out of spaces** while walkers watch and wait. 4) **Switch roles**. Note who finished Req 4.
   - Tip: **Needs a second registered adult.** One adult runs the lot while the other watches the crosswalk.
4. **Cone Pick-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Tell your grown-up your favorite safety step, then help pick up the cones."
   - How: 1) Pull up the **tape** and collect the **cones**. 2) Take quick **answers**. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name, and walk them to the car the safe way.
   - Tip: **Practice for real:** Walking to the car is the first real parking-lot practice.
