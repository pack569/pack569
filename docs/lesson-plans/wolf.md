# Wolf: required adventures (DRAFT 3, 40-minute den time)

> **A guide, not the rulebook.** These are ideas to help a den leader run the meeting. They are not a complete or official source. The official requirements on scouting.org and the Guide to Safe Scouting always come first. If something here is unclear or unsettled, use your judgment and check with the Cubmaster.

Researched 2026-09-29 by the cubmaster-program agent and rewritten the same day for 40-minute den time. Every plan here is in our own words. Requirements are only summarized, so use the official page for the full text. Anything marked "(check)" still needs confirming. Nothing is in the app yet.

How these plans fit a Pack 569 den night:
- The pack does the opening ceremony together: flag, Pledge, Oath and Law. Each den then splits off for about 40 minutes.
- Den meetings here have no gathering or opening steps. Each one is den steps plus one short den closing. The minutes add up to 35–40 and never go over 40, so the timer can use them as they are.
- If the pack opening already covers a requirement, the meeting doesn't get a step for it. Instead it has a "Done at the pack opening:" line, and a leader confirms each scout took part.
- Outings (the nature walk and the playground) aren't normal den nights. They keep their own length and are marked "(outing, about N min)".
- "Reqs: N" means the step does real work on that requirement. "Reqs: N (set-up)" means the step only sets up or reinforces it and does not complete it. Some outing steps use a semicolon to list both kinds, for example "Reqs: 3; 1 (set-up)".
- On 2026-09-29 I re-read the official Bobcat (Wolf) and Running With the Pack pages on scouting.org to settle where Bobcat Req 2 and Running With the Pack Reqs 3–5 can be done.

## Bobcat (Wolf)
- Character & Leadership · Meetings: 2 · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/bobcat-wolf/ · Other sources: https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Wolf-Adventure-Requirements.pdf, https://scoutermom.com/45161/wolf-bobcat-adventure/ · Checked: 2026-09-29
- Summary: This is the first adventure of the year. Scouts get to know the den, learn the Scout Law, the sign, the salute and the handshake, and write the den's own Code of Conduct. They say the Oath and Law at the pack opening.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Get to know the other scouts in your den. (meeting)
  2. Say the Scout Oath and Law with your den and den leader. (pack opening, if the den stands together with its den leader)
  3. Learn about the points of the Scout Law. (meeting)
  4. Make a den Code of Conduct together. (meeting)
  5. Show the Cub Scout sign, salute and handshake, and when to use each. (meeting)
  6. Tell the den or family about a time you did your best. (meeting or at home)
  7. Do the Parent's Guide youth-protection activities with a parent. (at home)
- Safety notes:
  - Req 7 is for families only. Leaders send the link and never run it themselves.
  - Two registered adults at the meeting. No one-on-one. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Meet the Den · 40 min
- Done at the pack opening: Req 2. The den stands together with its den leader, and a leader confirms each scout took part.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, print name tags and make 12 Scout Law point cards. Email families the Parent's Guide link for Req 7. Confirm your second registered adult. On the night, hide the Law cards in the den room before the pack opening.
- Supplies: name tags, markers, soft ball, 12 Scout Law point cards, tape, Oath & Law poster
- Tell parents before they leave: Please do the Parent's Guide activities with your scout at home (Req 7). The link is in the email, and please tell the den leader when you've finished. This week, help your scout notice a time they did their best.
1. **Name Toss** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "When you catch the ball, say the name of the person who threw it to you. Then say your own name and your favorite animal."
   - How: 1) Stand in a **circle** wearing name tags. The leader goes first. 2) Use **underhand** tosses only. 3) The catcher says the **thrower's name**, then their own name and animal. 4) In round two, try to name **everyone** who went before you.
   - Tip: **Shy scout?** Let them pass in round one and toss the ball back to the leader.
2. **Sign, Salute & Handshake: Akela Says** · den · 15 min · Reqs: 5
   - Say: "You just used two of our special moves at the pack opening. Wolves have three: a sign, a salute and a handshake. Learn when to use each one, and then see if Akela can trick you!"
   - How: 1) Teach the **sign**: right arm straight up with two fingers raised like wolf ears. Use it for the Oath and to ask for quiet. 2) Teach the **salute**: the same two fingers touch the eyebrow or cap brim. Use it for the flag. 3) Teach the **handshake**: right hand, with two fingers along the other scout's wrist. Use it to greet another Cub Scout. (check the hand details against the Wolf handbook) 4) Play **Akela Says**, calling things like "Akela says, the flag is passing!" or "Greet a Scout!" 5) A scout who moves without "Akela says" becomes a **helper** who spots the next mistake, so no one sits out.
   - Tip: **Sign off as you go:** Have each scout show you all three moves once and tell you when each is used. That completes Req 5.
3. **Scout Law Card Hunt** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "Twelve Scout Law cards are hiding in this room. Find them and we'll put them on the wall in order!"
   - How: 1) Scouts **search in pairs**. 2) Each pair brings its **card to the wall**. 3) The leader helps the den **put them in order** using the poster. 4) **Read the wall** together once.
   - Tip: **Hide some low:** Put a few cards at kid height so everyone finds one. Save the cards for Meeting 2.
4. **Do Your Best Preview** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 6 (set-up)
   - Say: "Our motto is 'Do Your Best.' This week, watch for a time you tried hard at something, even if it didn't turn out perfect."
   - How: 1) Share your own **short example** from this week. 2) Ask for **two or three quick ideas**, like homework, a sport or helping at home. 3) Tell scouts they'll **share their moment** next meeting.
   - Tip: **Keep it small:** Tying shoes or finishing a book counts. The point is trying hard, not winning.
   - (at home) Scouts may share with their family instead of the den.
5. **Clean-Up & Handshake Line** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "You tried a lot of brand-new things tonight, and that's doing your best. Let's leave this room cleaner than we found it."
   - How: 1) **Two-minute clean-up**: every scout picks up five things. 2) Give a one-minute **leader thought**. 3) Scouts go out through a **handshake line** past the leaders. 4) Hand each scout to their **adult** by name.
   - Tip: **Pickup:** Don't let a scout leave until you've seen their adult.
   - (at home) Req 7: families do the Parent's Guide activities.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Our Den's Promise · 40 min
- Done at the pack opening: Req 2 for any scout who missed it at Meeting 1. A leader confirms each of those scouts took part.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, write "Our Den Code" at the top of a poster board and bring a "talking stick." On the night, tape the Scout Law cards from Meeting 1 back on the wall before the pack opening.
- Supplies: Scout Law point cards, tape, poster board, markers, talking stick, Oath & Law poster
- Tell parents before they leave: If you haven't finished the Parent's Guide activities (Req 7), please do them this week and let the den leader know. The den's Code of Conduct poster will be at every meeting.
1. **Scout Law Charades** · den · 15 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "With your partner, pull a card and act out a 20-second scene that shows that point. No talking! The den guesses which point it is."
   - How: 1) **Pair** the scouts. 2) Each pair **draws a card** and plans for one minute. 3) **Act it out** for 20 seconds. 4) After each guess, ask **"What does that point mean?"**
   - Tip: **Stuck pair?** Whisper an idea, like "Helpful: carry groceries for someone."
2. **Write Our Den Code** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Every good team has rules it agrees on. Let's write ours, and say what we *will* do, not just what we won't."
   - How: 1) **Brainstorm** ideas. The leader writes them all down. 2) Turn "don'ts" into **"dos,"** so "no interrupting" becomes "We take turns talking." 3) The den **votes** on 4–5 rules. 4) Every scout **signs** the poster.
   - Tip: **Link to the Law:** Ask which Scout Law point each rule matches.
3. **Talking-Stick Circle: I Did My Best** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 6
   - Say: "Only the person holding the stick talks. Tell us about a time you did your best."
   - How: 1) Sit in a **circle**. 2) **Pass the stick** around. 3) After each story, the den gives a **silent cheer** with wiggling fingers. 4) Record who **shared**.
   - Tip: **Shy scout?** They can pass and tell a parent instead, because Req 6 allows sharing with family.
   - (at home) Req 6 can be finished at home with family.
4. **Read the Code & Handshake Line** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "This is our den's promise to each other. We'll bring it to every meeting."
   - How: 1) **Read the Code** aloud together. 2) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 3) Scouts leave through a **handshake line** and go to their adults.
   - Tip: **Keep it visible:** Roll up the poster and pack it with the den box.

## Council Fire (Wolf)
- Citizenship · Meetings: 3 · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/council-fire/ · Other sources: https://scoutermom.com/19287/wolf-council-fire-duty-to-country-cub-scout/, https://cubscoutideas.com/tag/council-fire/, https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Wolf-Adventure-Requirements.pdf · Checked: 2026-09-29
- Summary: Scouts learn flag care and ceremonies, build a model neighborhood, and serve their real one. Scouts must complete all six requirements.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Learn to care for and fold the U.S. flag, then do a flag ceremony. (meeting; the ceremony can be a pack opening led by the Wolves, or a pack meeting)
  2. Name three Scout Law points that make a good neighbor. (meeting)
  3. Build a model of your home. (meeting)
  4. Use the same materials to model a building in your community. (meeting)
  5. Put the models together into a neighborhood. (meeting)
  6. Do a service project and explain how it helps the neighborhood. (meeting or outing)
- Safety notes:
  - Kid-safe scissors only.
  - Off-site service needs two registered adults, parent permission and transport planning. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over. The chase driver never carries a lone scout who isn't their own child. Call the scout's parent, add a second adult, or carry a buddy too.
  - Tools on an off-site service project follow the SAFE Project Tool Use chart (680-028): Wolves may use rakes, shovels and trowels; wheelbarrows, handsaws, electric screwdrivers and palm sanders are Scouts BSA only, so adults use them.
  - Litter pickup: gloves, and adults handle glass and sharps. Keep scouts away from the road.

### Meeting 1 of 3 · Our Flag, Good Neighbors · 40 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, ask the Cubmaster whether the Wolves can lead the pack opening at Meeting 2. If not, ask for a flag-ceremony slot at the next pack meeting. Find a second U.S. flag for folding, because the pack's flag stays posted from the opening (check that the pack has a spare). Recruit one adult to help with folding and print the folding steps.
- Supplies: spare U.S. flag, a large towel for practice, printed folding steps, butcher paper, crayons, script cards for the ceremony
- Tell parents before they leave: Save clean boxes, tubes and cartons for next meeting. Your scout is helping lead the flag ceremony at [the pack opening on (date) / the pack meeting on (date)], so please arrive 10 minutes early. Early set-up starts only when both registered adults are there; the scout's parent stays until then. If you like, help your scout sketch the front of your home and bring the drawing.
1. **Flag Care & the Triangle Fold** · den · 15 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Our flag gets special care. It stays off the ground, it gets a light if it flies at night, and it's retired with respect when it wears out. Let's learn to fold it."
   - How: 1) **Talk through** the three care rules. 2) Pairs practice the **fold** on the towel, following the printed steps. 3) Fold the **real flag** with an adult on one end. 4) Every scout gets **one turn** handling the flag.
   - Tip: **Needs a second adult.** One adult folds with the pairs while the other keeps the rest of the den practicing on towels.
2. **Ceremony Rehearsal** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "You're leading the flag ceremony for the whole pack! Let's pick jobs and practice twice."
   - How: 1) Assign **roles**: color guard, caller and Pledge leader. 2) Walk through it **slowly**. 3) Run it **full speed** once. 4) Give the caller a **script card** to take home.
   - Tip: **Everyone has a job:** Scouts without a role lead the salute from the front row.
3. **Good-Neighbor Mural** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Draw someone being a great neighbor. Which Scout Law point are they showing?"
   - How: 1) Roll out the **butcher paper**. 2) Each scout draws a **neighbor scene**. 3) Label each scene with a **Law point**, such as Helpful, Friendly, Kind or Courteous. 4) The den **votes** on its top three.
   - Tip: **Check off Req 2:** Each scout says their three points out loud before leaving.
4. **Neighbor Idea & Clean-Up** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Tell me one neighborly thing you'll do this week, then let's clean up like good neighbors."
   - How: 1) Go around for **one idea** from each scout. 2) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 3) **Hand off** each scout to their adult.
   - Tip: **Short on time?** Skip the idea round and ask scouts to tell their adult on the way out.

### Meeting 2 of 3 · Build Our Neighborhood · 40 min
- Done at the pack opening (if the Wolves lead it tonight): the flag ceremony for Req 1. A leader confirms each scout had a job or took part. If the ceremony happens at a pack meeting instead, record it there.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, collect materials, draw streets on a large cardboard base, and have 2–3 service-project options ready for the vote. On the night, arrive 10 minutes early so the Wolves can walk through the ceremony once. Early set-up starts only when both registered adults are there; the scout's parent stays until then. Sort the materials into one tray per scout so building starts fast.
- Supplies: small boxes, paper tubes, construction paper, tape, glue sticks, markers, kid-safe scissors, cardboard base, scouts' home sketches (if they brought them)
- Tell parents before they leave: We picked our service project for next meeting. [If off-site: here's the location, date and permission slip.]
1. **Build My Home** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Build your home using your drawing or your memory. You have 12 minutes, so start with the biggest box."
   - How: 1) Pick a **base box**. 2) Add a **roof, doors and windows**. 3) Put your **name** on the bottom.
   - Tip: **Tape over glue:** Tape holds boxes faster than glue. An apartment or townhome counts, so build the building and mark your door.
2. **Build a Community Building** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Now use the same kinds of materials to build a place in our town, like the library, the fire station or your school."
   - How: 1) Each scout **chooses a building**, trying not to double up. 2) **Build** it with the same materials. 3) Add a **sign** with its name.
   - Tip: **Family places count:** A place of worship, a grocery store or a doctor's office all work.
3. **Put the Neighborhood Together** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 5
   - Say: "Let's put every building on the map. Tell us who works in your building and how they help."
   - How: 1) Each scout **places** their home and building on the base. 2) Add **street names**. 3) Each scout says **who works** in their building.
   - Tip: **Show it off:** Bring the neighborhood to the pack meeting for display.
4. **Pick Our Service Project** · den · 4 min · Reqs: 6 (set-up)
   - Say: "Real neighbors help each other. Which project should our den do?"
   - How: 1) Show **2–3 options** you've already checked with adults. 2) **Vote** by hands up. 3) Tell scouts **what to bring**.
   - Tip: **Only offer options you can deliver.** Don't list a project you haven't confirmed with the site.
5. **Job-Site Clean-Up** · closing · 4 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Great builders clean up their job site. Everybody grab five scraps!"
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 2) Move the neighborhood to a **safe spot**. 3) **Hand off** to adults.
   - Tip: **Wet glue?** If you're allowed, let the models dry flat overnight at the meeting site.

### Meeting 3 of 3 · Service in Action · 40 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, confirm the project.
  - In the meeting (this plan): cards and thank-you signs for veterans, a senior community or local helpers such as firefighters. Arrange delivery with the site first.
  - Off-site option: a food-pantry collection or a park cleanup. Confirm the site contact, address, restrooms and parking. Collect permission slips and health forms, plan transport, confirm two registered adults, and follow the council's outing process (check). The chase driver never carries a lone scout who isn't their own child. Call the scout's parent, add a second adult, or carry a buddy too. The off-site version is an outing, not a den night. Change the header to "(outing, about 60 min)". Add a 10-minute arrival check-in and headcount at the start, make Do the Project 35 minutes, and add the cleanup rules to the briefing.
- Supplies: cardstock, poster board, markers, stickers. Off-site cleanup also needs gloves, trash bags, hand sanitizer and a first-aid kit.
- Tell parents before they leave: Thank you for helping us serve. Adults will deliver the cards this week. [If off-site: pickup is at the site at (time).]
1. **Job Briefing** · den · 4 min · Reqs: 6 (set-up)
   - Say: "The Oath says we help other people at all times. Tonight we prove it. Here's our job and who it's for."
   - How: 1) Explain **what we're making** and **who gets it**. 2) Assign **buddies**. 3) Show **one finished example**.
   - Tip: **Off-site:** Add the cleanup rules here: gloves on, adults pick up glass and sharps, and stay away from the road.
2. **Do the Project** · den · 25 min · Reqs: 6
   - Say: "Let's get to work! See how much good our den can do in 25 minutes."
   - How: 1) Buddies make **cards** and one big **"Thank You, Helpers!" sign**. 2) Write or dictate a **short message** in each card. 3) Sign with **first names only**. 4) Take a **group photo** of the finished work. Photograph only scouts whose parents gave photo permission; post only on the pack's official channel, with no names.
   - Tip: **Losing steam?** Set a mini-goal like "Two more cards each."
3. **Reflection Circle** · den · 6 min · Reqs: 6
   - Say: "Who are we helping, and how does that make our neighborhood better?"
   - How: 1) **Circle up**. 2) Each scout gives **one sentence**. 3) Record each scout as **complete** for Req 6.
   - Tip: **Prompt:** "Because we did this, ______ is better."
4. **Den Cheer & Clean-Up** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Give our loudest den cheer for our neighbors, then let's clean up!"
   - How: 1) Do the **den cheer**. 2) **Collect** the cards for delivery. 3) Clean up and **hand off** to adults.
   - Tip: **Off-site:** Scouts thank the host, everyone uses **hand sanitizer** before snacks, and you do a **headcount** before handoff.

## Footsteps (Wolf)
- Family & Reverence · Meetings: 2 · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/footsteps/ · Other sources: https://scoutermom.com/19049/wolf-duty-to-god-footsteps-of-faith/, https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Wolf-Adventure-Requirements.pdf · Checked: 2026-09-29
- Summary: Families lead the faith and reverence parts at home. The den covers honesty and kindness. Scouts can also earn this adventure by completing a religious emblem of the family's choosing.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Talk about family traditions with a parent, name three celebrations, and make art. (at home; the art can be made in the meeting)
  2. Attend a religious service or reverence gathering with family. (at home)
  3. Do an act of kindness. (meeting or at home)
  4. Hear "The Boy Who Cried Wolf" and discuss telling the truth. (meeting)
- Safety notes:
  - Leaders do not teach or lead religious content.
  - Every family's traditions count, including non-religious ones.
  - No scout has to share beliefs aloud.
  - Check food allergies if the kindness project involves food.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Truth Counts · 40 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, send families a note:
  - Reqs 1 and 2 are family-led.
  - Please talk about three family celebrations before Meeting 2.
  - A religious emblem is another way to earn the adventure.
  - Our chartered organization is a local church. Going to one of its services is one option for Req 2, never a requirement. Any faith's service or reverence gathering counts, and each family chooses its own.

  Get a copy of the fable (it is public domain). Make kindness-jar slips.
- Supplies: fable text, sheep ears or props (optional), jar, paper slips, crayons
- Tell parents before they leave: Reqs 1 and 2 happen at home and are led by your family. Please talk about three family celebrations before next meeting, and attend a service or gathering of your family's choice. Your scout picked a kind act from our jar to do this week.
1. **Story Time: The Boy Who Cried Wolf** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Tonight's Scout Law point is Trustworthy. That means people can count on you to tell the truth. Here's a very old story about a shepherd boy. Listen for the moment the villagers stop believing him."
   - How: 1) Sit scouts **on the floor** facing you. 2) **Read aloud** with big voices. 3) Stop and ask, "**Why did he do that?**"
   - Tip: **Wiggly den?** Let scouts shout "Wolf!" every time the boy does.
2. **Act It Out** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Now it's your turn to be the shepherd, the sheep and the villagers!"
   - How: 1) **Cast** the roles, with plenty of sheep. 2) Act it out **once as the story goes**. 3) Act it out **again with a new ending** where the shepherd tells the truth.
   - Tip: **Everyone on stage:** Extra scouts can be the wolf or trees.
3. **Winning Trust Back** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "If someone stops believing you, how can you earn their trust back?"
   - How: 1) Take **three or four answers**. 2) Connect them to **Trustworthy**. 3) Mark each scout **complete** for Req 4 after they answer.
   - Tip: **Keep it general:** Don't ask scouts to confess a real lie.
4. **Kindness Jar** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "Draw or write a kind act on a slip and drop it in the jar. Then everyone picks one to do this week."
   - How: 1) Each scout makes **two slips**. 2) **Read** a few aloud. 3) Each scout **draws one** to take home.
   - Tip: **Keep it doable:** A 7-year-old should be able to do it without money or a ride.
   - (at home) The kind act is done at home this week.
5. **Leader Minute & Clean-Up** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "When you always tell the truth, people believe you, even when it really matters."
   - How: 1) Give the **leader minute**. 2) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 3) **Hand off** to adults.
   - Tip: **Home note:** Tape the kind-act slip to the scout's hand-out so parents see it.
   - (at home) Reqs 1–2: the family celebrations talk and a service or gathering of the family's choice.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Celebrations and Kindness · 40 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, remind families about the home talk. If delivering cards, arrange it first with a senior community or the school office.
- Supplies: contact paper, tissue-paper squares, construction paper, glue sticks, markers, cardstock
- Tell parents before they leave: Adults will deliver the den's cards this week. If your family hasn't finished Reqs 1–2 at home, please do, and tell the den leader when you're done.
1. **Kindness Jar Report** · den · 4 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Tonight's Scout Law point is Kind. Thumbs up if you did your kind act! Who wants to tell us about it?"
   - How: 1) **Thumbs up or down**. 2) Two or three scouts **share**. 3) **Record** who finished Req 3.
   - Tip: **Didn't do it yet?** No shame. Tonight's cards count as a den kind act.
2. **Celebration Art** · den · 14 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Make art that shows a celebration your family talked about at home."
   - How: 1) Scouts choose a **sun catcher** (tissue on contact paper) or a **paper mosaic**. 2) **Build** it. 3) **Label** it with the celebration's name if the scout wants to.
   - Tip: **Didn't do the home talk?** Let them show a birthday or family day now and finish the talk at home.
   - (at home) The rest of Req 1 is the family talk at home.
3. **Den Kindness Cards** · den · 14 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Think of someone who helps you, like a teacher, a bus driver or a neighbor. Make them a card with a kind message inside. A grown-up will deliver it for us."
   - How: 1) Decorate the **card front**. 2) Write or dictate a **short message** inside. 3) Sign with **first names only**. 4) **Collect** the cards for delivery.
   - Tip: **Non-writers:** Scouts dictate and an adult writes. The scout signs.
4. **Gallery Walk (optional)** · den · 4 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "If you want to, show your art and tell us one thing about it. Different families celebrate different things, and that's great."
   - How: 1) Ask for **volunteers only**. 2) Everyone gives a **silent cheer**. 3) Thank every **presenter**.
   - Tip: **No one has to share beliefs.** Skipping is fine, so don't call on anyone.
5. **Leader Minute & Clean-Up** · closing · 4 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Reverence means respect. Tell me one way you can respect what other people believe."
   - How: 1) Take a few **answers**. 2) **Clean up**. 3) **Hand off** to adults.
   - Tip: **Art is wet?** Send it home flat in a folder.

## Paws on the Path (Wolf)
- Outdoors · Meetings: 2 (one den meeting plus one outing) · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/paws-on-the-path/ · Other sources: https://scoutermom.com/24540/wolf-paws-on-the-path-adventure/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/grab-the-six-essentials/, https://www.scouting.org/outdoor-programs/outdoor-ethics/, https://www.scouting.org/outdoor-programs/leave-no-trace/ · Checked: 2026-09-29
- Summary: Scouts get trail-ready with the Six Essentials, buddies, the right clothes and outdoor ethics. Then they take a nature walk of 30 minutes or more.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Name the Cub Scout Six Essentials and show each one's use. (meeting)
  2. Learn the buddy system and pick a buddy for the walk. (meeting and outing)
  3. Choose weather-right clothes and shoes, and wear them on the walk. (meeting and outing)
  4. Learn the Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace for Kids. (meeting)
  5. Walk at least 30 minutes and describe four animals. (outing)
- Safety notes:
  - The walk is an outing: two registered adults, permission, and the council's outing process (check). If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
  - The official page asks the adult in charge to take Hazardous Weather training, watch the weather safety-moment video, and use the SAFE Checklist.
  - Bring and review health forms. Postpone for lightning. Plan for Georgia heat and water.
  - If a scout carries an EpiPen, at least one other adult must know how to use it (official page).
  - Do a tick check afterward. Don't touch wildlife.
  - Restrooms: buddies go together and an adult waits outside. There is no one-on-one.
  - Trail food: check allergies.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Ready for the Trail · 40 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, build a demo Six Essentials kit (first-aid kit, water bottle, flashlight, trail food, sun protection, whistle). Choose the walk location and date, and have permission slips and walk info sheets ready.
- Supplies: demo kit, bag of clothes (rain jacket, hat, flip-flops, sneakers, heavy coat, sunglasses), weather cards, Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace for Kids posters, walk info sheets with packing lists, permission slips

1. **What Would You Grab?** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Here are the Six Essentials. I'll tell you a trail problem. Run and touch the thing that helps, then tell us how you'd use it!"
   - How: 1) **Name each item** as you place it around the room. 2) Read a **scenario**: a scraped knee, getting dark, lost (three whistle blasts), hot sun, hungry or thirsty. 3) Scouts **run and touch** the right item. 4) One scout **shows how to use it**.
   - Tip: **Check off Req 1:** Each scout names all six and shows one use before the walk.
2. **Dress the Hiker** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "Your team gets a weather card. Dress your hiker for that weather, fast!"
   - How: 1) Make **two teams** with one volunteer "hiker" each. 2) Teams **draw a weather card**. 3) **Dress the hiker** from the clothes bag. 4) The den checks: **closed-toe shoes?**
   - Tip: **Flip-flops trap:** Always slip them in so scouts can catch them. Req 3 is complete when scouts wear the right clothes on the walk.
3. **Buddy Check Drill** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "When I call 'Buddy check!', grab your buddy's hand, raise it high and freeze."
   - How: 1) **Pair** the scouts. 2) Scouts **walk around** the room. 3) Call **"Buddy check!"** 4) **Count off** the pairs.
   - Tip: **Odd number?** Make one group of three. Never leave a scout alone.
4. **Outdoor Code & Leave No Trace for Kids** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Outdoors, we take care of nature. Let's give each rule a hand motion so we remember it."
   - How: 1) Read the **Outdoor Code** together from the poster. 2) Teach a **motion** for each Leave No Trace for Kids principle: Know Before You Go, Choose the Right Path, Trash Your Trash, Leave What You Find, Be Careful with Fire, Respect Wildlife, Be Kind to Other Visitors. 3) **Quiz** the den: you do a motion and they name the principle.
   - Tip: **Scouts make up the motions:** They remember their own better.
5. **Walk Briefing & Handoff** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Next time we're hitting the trail! Pack your own Six Essentials, and wear the right shoes for the weather."
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 2) Hand the **info sheet** and **permission slip** to each **parent**. 3) **Hand off** each scout by name.
   - Tip: **Put the sheet in the parent's hand,** not in a scout's pocket. Collect any slips parents brought.
   - (at home) Scouts pack their own Six Essentials for the walk.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Nature Walk (outing, about 60 min)
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, scout the trail and plan a loop of 30 minutes or more. Confirm restrooms and parking, and send families the trailhead address and meeting time. Collect permission slips and health forms. Confirm two registered adults. Follow the council's outing process (check). The adult in charge completes Hazardous Weather training and the SAFE Checklist. Check the forecast the day before and again that morning, and postpone for lightning.
- Supplies: each scout's Six Essentials, animal-detective sheets, clipboards, pencils, trash bag, leader first-aid kit, health forms, extra water
- Tell parents before they leave: Pickup is at the trailhead at [time]. Please do a tick check tonight.
1. **Trailhead Check-In & Gear Check** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 3; 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Show me your Six Essentials and your shoes. Are you trail-ready?"
   - How: 1) **Sign in** each scout and collect any missing forms. 2) Each scout **shows** their Essentials and says one use. 3) **Check shoes and clothes** for the weather. 4) Everyone takes a **restroom stop** in buddy pairs, with an adult waiting outside.
   - Tip: **Forgot something?** Lend from the leader kit. The scout still names what was missing.
2. **Buddies & Outdoor Code** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 2; 4 (set-up)
   - Say: "Pick your trail buddy. You stay together the whole walk. Now let's say the Outdoor Code."
   - How: 1) Scouts **pick buddies**. 2) Do a **buddy check** count-off. 3) Say the **Outdoor Code** together. 4) One adult leads at the **front** and one sweeps at the **back**.
   - Tip: **Needs a second adult.** The sweep adult never lets a pair fall behind.
3. **Animal Detective Walk, Part 1** · den · 15 min · Reqs: 5
   - Say: "Detectives use their eyes and ears. Look for animals, tracks, nests and droppings, and whisper when you spot one."
   - How: 1) Hand out **clipboards and sheets**. 2) **Walk** at kid pace. 3) Stop to **look and listen** for 30 seconds at a time. 4) Call **"Buddy check!"** at the halfway point.
   - Tip: **Look, don't touch:** Leashed dogs, birds, bugs and squirrels all count. Hands stay off wildlife.
4. **Animal Detective Walk, Part 2** · den · 20 min · Reqs: 5
   - Say: "Head back, detectives. Remember, we pack out everything we brought."
   - How: 1) **Turn back** or finish the loop. 2) Take a **water break**. 3) Scouts **add to their sheets**. 4) Have them **pack out** their own trash.
   - Tip: **Watch the clock:** Parts 1 and 2 together must be at least 30 minutes of walking. Don't cut them short.
5. **Four-Animal Share** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 5
   - Say: "Tell us about four animals you found: what they looked like and where they were."
   - How: 1) **Circle up** at the trailhead. 2) Each scout **describes four**. 3) **Record** Req 5 as complete.
   - Tip: **Short list?** Buddies can help each other remember, but each scout describes their own four.
6. **Final Buddy Check & Handoff** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Last buddy check! Great job on the trail, Wolves."
   - How: 1) Do a **buddy check** count-off. 2) Make a **trash sweep** of the trailhead. 3) Hand each scout to their **adult** by name.
   - Tip: **Tick reminder:** Say it to every parent at pickup.

## Running With the Pack (Wolf)
- Personal Fitness · Meetings: 3 (was 2 in draft 2) · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/running-with-the-pack/ · Other sources: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/scouting-america-annual-health-and-medical-record-wolf/, https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Wolf-Adventure-Requirements.pdf, https://scoutermom.com/24542/running-with-the-pack-wolf-cub-scout/ (lists only 5 requirements; out of date) · Checked: 2026-09-29
- Summary: Healthy food, healthy teeth, exercise and rest, plus a health-form talk at home. Three meetings are needed because Req 3 (30 minutes active) and Req 5 (10 minutes relaxing) together fill a whole 40-minute den time before the closing.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Taste three foods that are naturally three different colors, each from a food group. (meeting)
  2. Show how to brush your teeth properly. (meeting)
  3. Be active 30 minutes with your den or at least one other person, including stretching and moving. (meeting; the official text also allows one other person, such as family)
  4. Be active 10 minutes doing your own exercises. (meeting; the official text doesn't require the den)
  5. Do a relaxing activity for 10 minutes. (meeting; the official text doesn't require the den)
  6. Review your health form with a parent and discuss taking part in den and pack activities. (at home)
- Safety notes:
  - Collect food allergies before the tasting.
  - Hand-washing before food.
  - Don't share toothbrushes. Use a model instead.
  - Water breaks. Check the heat in the Georgia summer and fall.

### Meeting 1 of 3 · Fuel Up, Brush Up · 40 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, ask families about food allergies and choose safe foods. Send the health-form link for Req 6. Make egg-carton "teeth." Print food pictures and rainbow charts.
- Supplies: strawberries, blueberries, bananas, carrots, cucumber, cheese cubes, whole-grain crackers, plates, napkins, wipes, egg-carton "teeth," washable markers, old toothbrush or paintbrush, cup of water, timer, food pictures, rainbow charts
- Tell parents before they leave: Please review your scout's health form with them at home (Req 6). Brushing is two minutes, twice a day. Next meeting is very active, so send sneakers, comfortable clothes and a water bottle.
1. **Food Sort** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "You said 'physically strong' in the Oath at the opening. Food is how you get there! Sort these pictures by color, then by food group."
   - How: 1) Spread the **pictures** on the table. 2) Scouts sort **by color**. 3) Then they re-sort **by food group**: protein, vegetable, fruit, dairy, grain.
   - Tip: **Go straight to hand-washing** when the sort is done.
2. **Rainbow Taste Test** · den · 15 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Wash up, then try at least three foods of different colors. Color each one on your rainbow chart!"
   - How: 1) Everyone **washes hands**. 2) Serve **small tastes** of each food. 3) Scouts **color the chart** for each one they try. 4) Ask, "**Which food group** is that?"
   - Tip: **Picky eater?** A tiny bite counts as a taste. Never force it. Only serve foods cleared for allergies.
3. **Plaque Attack** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "These teeth are covered in plaque! Brush in little circles on every side until the timer says two minutes."
   - How: 1) Scribble washable **"plaque"** on the egg-carton teeth. 2) Show **small circles** on the front, back and top. 3) Scouts **brush for two minutes** on the timer. 4) Each scout **tells you** the steps.
   - Tip: **No real mouths:** Scouts don't share toothbrushes, so everything happens on the model.
4. **Favorite New Food & Clean-Up** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Tell us one food you tried tonight that you'd eat again!"
   - How: 1) Take **quick answers** around the circle. 2) **Wipe tables** and clean up. 3) **Hand off** to adults.
   - Tip: **Leftover food:** Throw it away. Don't send opened food home with other families.
   - (at home) Req 6: review the health form with a parent.

### Meeting 2 of 3 · Pack on the Move · 40 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, book a gym or field, plan the games, and remind families to send sneakers and water. Check the heat index if you're outdoors.
- Supplies: cones, hula hoops, playground ball, water
- Tell parents before they leave: Next meeting your scout builds their own workout, so ask them what moves they like. If you haven't reviewed the health form yet (Req 6), please do it this week.
1. **Warm-Up Stretch** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Muscles work better when they're warm. Stretch high, low and side to side. Tag is a light touch, and you can get water whenever you need it."
   - How: 1) Do **arm circles**. 2) **Touch your toes** and **reach high**. 3) Do **leg swings** holding a buddy's shoulder. 4) Point out any **walls and doors** that are off limits.
   - Tip: **Start the clock:** Req 3's 30 minutes start here and run through the cool-down.
2. **Den Tag** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "The hoops are wolf dens, and you're safe inside. But you can only stay five seconds!"
   - How: 1) Scatter the **hoops**. 2) Pick **one or two taggers**. 3) Scouts are safe for a **five-second count** inside a hoop. 4) **Switch taggers** every two minutes.
   - Tip: **Too rough?** Change to "tag with a high five."
3. **Relay Races** · den · 13 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Teams line up behind the cones. Run, bear-crawl, then crab-walk. Cheer your team on!"
   - How: 1) Make **two even teams**. 2) Each round uses a **new move**. 3) Take a **water break** between rounds.
   - Tip: **Keep it fair:** Mix up the teams after each round.
4. **Cool-Down** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Slow walk, deep breaths, and let's stretch it out."
   - How: 1) Take a **slow lap**. 2) Repeat the **warm-up stretches**. 3) Take a **water break**.
   - Tip: **Record Req 3** now. That's 30 or more active minutes together.
5. **Stack the Cones & Handoff** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Great running, Wolves! Tell me which game got your heart pumping the most."
   - How: 1) Take **quick answers**. 2) **Stack the cones** and hoops. 3) **Hand off** to adults.
   - Tip: **Water bottles:** Check the water line so no bottle gets left behind.

### Meeting 3 of 3 · My Workout, My Calm-Down · 40 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, print simple move cards with pictures (jumping jacks, bear crawls, wall sits, running in place, and so on). Bring mats or towels, calm music and a speaker.
- Supplies: move cards, paper, crayons, timer, water, towels or mats, calm music and a speaker
- Tell parents before they leave: Ask your scout about "melting snowman," a calm-down trick that works at bedtime. If you haven't reviewed the health form yet (Req 6), please do it this week and tell the den leader.
1. **Plan My Mini Workout** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 4 (set-up)
   - Say: "Build your own workout! Pick four or five moves you like and put them in order."
   - How: 1) Spread out the **move cards**. 2) Scouts **pick 4–5 moves**. 3) They **write or draw** the routine on paper.
   - Tip: **Can't decide?** Offer a starter set of jumping jacks, bear crawls and running in place.
2. **My Mini Workout** · den · 13 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Ten minutes on the clock. Do your own routine, over and over, at your own pace!"
   - How: 1) Each scout gets a **spot** with space around it. 2) Run the timer for **10 minutes** while each scout does their **own routine**. 3) Take a **water break**. 4) Ask two or three scouts to **show a favorite move**.
   - Tip: **Energy crash?** Let scouts swap one move for an easier one. The 10 minutes still count.
3. **Why Rest Matters** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 5 (set-up)
   - Say: "Your body grows and heals while you sleep. Rest is part of being physically strong. What helps you calm down?"
   - How: 1) Take **quick answers**, like reading, deep breaths or quiet music. 2) Run a quick **poll**: "What time do you go to bed?" 3) Scouts **lie down** on the mats.
   - Tip: **Dim the lights** if you can.
4. **Melting Snowman** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 5
   - Say: "Lie down and be a snowman. As the sun comes out, melt one body part at a time until you're a puddle."
   - How: 1) Start the **calm music**. 2) Do **belly breathing** with a hand on your tummy. 3) The leader calls out slow **melting**: toes, legs, arms, shoulders, face. 4) Run it for **ten minutes** by the timer.
   - Tip: **Giggles are OK.** Lower your voice and they'll follow.
5. **Slow Stand-Up & Handoff** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Snowmen, freeze back up slowly. Tell me one way you'll rest well tonight."
   - How: 1) Scouts **sit up slowly**. 2) Take **quick answers**. 3) **Roll up the mats** and **hand off** to adults.
   - Tip: **Record Reqs 4 and 5** before scouts leave.

## Safety in Numbers (Wolf)
- Personal Safety · Meetings: 2 (one den meeting plus one playground outing) · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/safety-in-numbers/ · Other sources: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/protect-yourself-video-wolf/, https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/cubscouts/adventure-resources/wolf/Wolf%20Safety%20in%20Numbers%201%20Parent%20Notification.pdf, https://www.pack3087.com/adventure/safety-in-numbers/, https://scoutermom.com/45175/wolf-safety-in-numbers-adventure/ · Checked: 2026-09-29
- Summary: Parents lead the Protect Yourself Rules video and the safe-touch talk at home. The den practices buddy skills and playground safety.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. With a parent's OK, watch the Wolf Protect Yourself Rules video. (at home)
  2. Talk about "safe touch" from the video. (at home; the den only reinforces it)
  3. Learn the buddy system and show how it works. (meeting)
  4. Review safety rules and use playground equipment correctly. (outing)
- Safety notes:
  - Send the official parent notification before anything else.
  - The video (13 min) is meant to be watched by the parent with their scout. Don't show it in the den.
  - If a scout discloses abuse: stay calm, listen, and don't question or investigate. (1) Make sure the child is safe; call 911 for an injury or immediate danger. (2) **You personally** report within 24 hours to Georgia DFCS, 1-855-GACHILD (1-855-422-4453), or to local law enforcement. You can't hand this to another leader. (3) Then notify the NEGA Scout executive; if you can't reach them, call Scouts First, 1-844-SCOUTS1 (1-844-726-8871). Sources: scouting.org/training/safeguarding-youth/, gss01, Georgia DFCS.
  - Playground: check for hot metal slides, keep an adult at each area, and follow the restroom buddy rule.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Buddies and Trusted Adults · 40 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, email the parent notification and the video link. Ask families to watch and talk about it before this meeting. Leaders review the Protect Yourself Rules and the disclosure steps above. Invite parents to stay for den time. Cut picture cards in half and have playground permission slips ready.
- Supplies: picture cards cut in half, neckerchiefs or bandanas, cones, whistle, stopwatch
- Tell parents before they leave: If you haven't yet, please watch the Protect Yourself Rules video with your scout and talk about safe touch (Reqs 1–2). Talk with your scout at home about their trusted adults (part of the Protect Yourself Rules). The playground outing is [date/place], and we need your permission slip.
1. **Why Buddies?** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Find the scout with the other half of your picture. That's your buddy tonight! A buddy keeps you safe. If something goes wrong, your buddy can help you or go get a grown-up."
   - How: 1) Hand out **half cards** and let scouts find their match. 2) Ask, "**When** would a buddy help?" 3) List answers like **restrooms**, **crowds** and **the playground**. 4) Teach the rule: **go together, come back together**.
   - Tip: **Odd number?** Make a set of three cards for one trio. Keep it upbeat, because this is about teamwork, not scary stories.
2. **Buddy Freeze** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Move around, but when you hear the whistle, find your buddy fast, grab hands, raise them and freeze!"
   - How: 1) Scouts **move** around the room. 2) Blow the **whistle**. 3) **Time** how fast every pair freezes. 4) **Beat the record** next round.
   - Tip: **Needs a second adult.** One adult times while the other watches for collisions.
3. **Three-Legged Buddy Race** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Buddies work together! Tie your legs with a neckerchief and race to the cone and back."
   - How: 1) An adult **ties** each pair's ankles loosely. 2) **Walk** a practice lap first. 3) **Race** in heats.
   - Tip: **Falls happen:** Use a soft floor or grass, and walking is allowed.
4. **No, Go, Tell Relay** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 2 (set-up)
   - Say: "Your body belongs to you. If something feels wrong, you say 'No!', get away, and tell a grown-up you trust."
   - How: 1) At the **cone**, the scout shouts **"No!"** 2) They **run** to their parent, or to a leader if the parent isn't there. 3) They say, **"I need to tell you something."** 4) Every scout gets **a turn**.
   - Tip: **Keep it general.** Don't discuss details of the video, because that talk belongs to parents. If a scout discloses something, follow the disclosure steps.
   - (at home) Reqs 1–2 are finished at home with a parent.
5. **Buddy Check Challenge** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Let's show how fast buddies can check in. When I call 'Buddy check!', find your buddy, grab hands and raise them high."
   - How: 1) Scouts **walk around** the room. 2) Call **"Buddy check!"** at random times. 3) Buddies **grab hands and raise** them. 4) **Swap buddies** once and try again.
   - Tip: **Record Req 3:** Note each scout who finds their buddy quickly every time.
6. **Buddy Clean-Up & Handoff** · closing · 4 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Great buddy work, everyone. Let's clean up together, buddies side by side."
   - How: 1) Buddies **clean up in pairs**. 2) Collect **permission slips**. 3) **Hand off** to adults.
   - Tip: **Slip check:** Collect playground permission slips before scouts leave.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Playground Pros (outing, about 55 min)
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, pick a public playground close by and check the equipment and restrooms. Send families the address and times. Collect permission slips and health forms. Confirm two registered adults. Follow the council's outing process (check). Check the weather, and test slides for heat on hot days.
- Supplies: first-aid kit, water, health forms, rule cards, whistle
- Tell parents before they leave: Pickup is at the playground at [time]. Ask your scout for their favorite playground rule.
1. **Arrival, Buddies & Boundaries** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "Find your buddy and check in with me. No one goes on the equipment until everyone's here and knows the boundaries."
   - How: 1) **Sign in** each scout from their adult. 2) Scouts **pair up**. 3) Adults **touch-test** the metal and slides for heat. 4) **Point out** where scouts can and can't go. 5) Everyone takes a **restroom stop** in buddy pairs, with an adult waiting outside.
   - Tip: **Early scouts:** They play a sitting game like I Spy by the bench until everyone arrives. Early set-up starts only when both registered adults are there; the scout's parent stays until then.
2. **Rule Walk-Around** · den · 15 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Every piece of equipment has a safety rule. At each stop, you make up the rule!"
   - How: 1) Walk the den to **each piece**. 2) Scouts **make up the rule**, for example: slide feet first one at a time, walk wide around swings, sit to swing, two hands on climbers, and touch-test for heat. 3) An adult **adds anything missed**. 4) Read the **rule card** aloud.
   - Tip: **Needs a second adult.** One adult leads the walk and one keeps the group together.
3. **Show Me** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Pick three pieces and show us how to use them the safe way. Your buddy is the judge!"
   - How: 1) Each scout picks **three pieces**. 2) Their **buddy watches** and gives a thumbs up. 3) A leader **records** Req 4.
   - Tip: **Adult at each area:** Spread the adults out so no piece goes unwatched.
4. **Free Play with Buddy Checks** · den · 15 min · Reqs: 3, 4
   - Say: "Free play! But every time you hear the whistle, find your buddy and freeze."
   - How: 1) Play **inside the boundaries**. 2) Blow the whistle every **five minutes**. 3) Buddies **grab hands and raise** them. 4) Take a **water break** halfway.
   - Tip: **Record Req 3:** Scouts who do the buddy checks correctly have shown the buddy system.
5. **Favorite Rule & Handoff** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Tell us your favorite playground rule, then one last buddy check!"
   - How: 1) Take **quick answers**. 2) Do a **final buddy check**. 3) Hand each scout to their **adult** by name.
   - Tip: **Don't release a scout** until you see their adult.

    - Meetings total 35–40 minutes, not 60. Outings carry their own length in the header "(outing, about N min)", so the timer shouldn't assume a fixed length.
    - Some meetings have a "Done at the pack opening:" line. It is not a step, but a leader still confirms each scout took part. It needs its own field, and possibly a way to record credit from pack-opening attendance (see question 4).
    - "Reqs: N (set-up)" means the step doesn't complete the requirement. Some outing steps mix both kinds with a semicolon, like "Reqs: 3; 1 (set-up)."
    - A few steps end with an "(at home)" marker.

    The step data model needs to tell apart "this step completes a requirement" from "this step supports it," so that tapping through a step doesn't record credit the scout hasn't earned (see DESIGN-adventures.md on runs).

Dropped from draft 2: the All-dens gathering question. There are no gathering steps any more, and the pack opening is shared by every rank.
