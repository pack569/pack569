# Tiger: required adventures (DRAFT 1, 40-minute den time)

> **A guide, not the rulebook.** These are ideas to help a den leader run the meeting. They are not a complete or official source. The official requirements on scouting.org and the Guide to Safe Scouting always come first. If something here is unclear or unsettled, use your judgment and check with the Cubmaster.

Researched 2026-09-30 by the cubmaster-program agent, using the format of Lion draft 1, Wolf draft 3 and Bear draft 1. Every plan here is in our own words. Requirements are only summarized, so use the official page for the full text. Anything marked "(check)" still needs confirming. Nothing is in the app yet.

How these plans fit a Pack 569 den night:
- The pack does the opening ceremony together: flag, Pledge, Oath and Law. Each den then splits off for about 40 minutes.
- Den meetings here have no gathering or opening steps. Each one is den steps plus one short den closing. The minutes add up to 35–40 and never go over 40, so the timer can use them as they are.
- If the pack opening already covers a requirement, the meeting doesn't get a step for it. Instead it has a "Done at the pack opening:" line, and the den leader confirms who was present. **Two Tiger requirements can be done at the pack opening.** The first is Bobcat Req 2: say the Oath with your den, with your adult partner there. The second is Team Tiger Req 2, on the night a Tiger does a pack-opening job they chose. Tigers take their turn leading the opening flag ceremony in the normal rotation, with adult partners helping.
- Outings (the Tigers in the Wild walk) aren't normal den nights. They keep their own length and are marked "(outing, about N min)".
- "Reqs: N" means the step does real work on that requirement. "Reqs: N (set-up)" means the step only sets up or reinforces it and does not complete it. Some outing steps use a semicolon to list both kinds.
- The guides don't tick requirements. Leaders record progress on the app's Advancement board afterward.
- Tigers are in 1st grade, age 6–7. **Every Tiger comes with an adult partner, who joins every activity, and there are no drop-offs.** Steps are written for Tiger + adult-partner pairs, and the How lines give the partner a job. Activity chunks are 5–12 minutes with lots of movement. Reading is kept to a minimum: leaders say the instructions, and partners do the writing.
- Every den room needs two registered adults 21 or over, and there is no one-on-one contact. **An adult partner doesn't count as one of the two registered adults unless they are registered as a leader** (https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/yp-faqs/). If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
- The closing "handoff" for Tigers means checking out each Tiger with their adult partner by name. The pair leaves together.
- On 2026-09-30 I read all six official Tiger adventure pages and the activity pages linked below. Plain WebFetch truncates scouting.org, so I used the r.jina.ai reader. I cross-checked everything against the Michigan Crossroads Council's Oct 2024 Tiger requirements PDF (https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Tiger-Adventure-Requirements.pdf). The six names and their category order match the app's `ADVENTURES['Tiger'].required`.

## Bobcat (Tiger)
- Character & Leadership · Meetings: 2 · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/bobcat-tiger/ · Other sources: https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Tiger-Adventure-Requirements.pdf, https://scoutermom.com/45159/tiger-bobcat-adventure/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/tiger-oath-and-law-puzzle/, https://www.scoutingatl.org/TigerAdvancement, https://www.scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/cub-scout-uniform/ · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: The official page calls Bobcat the first required adventure on the trail to the Tiger badge. After it, the other adventures can go in any order. Tiger Bobcat sits between Lion and Wolf. Like Wolf, it has the Oath and the sign, salute and handshake. Unlike Wolf, only the Oath is recited (the Law is read to the scout), and there's no den Code of Conduct. Four of the six requirements name the adult partner.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Get to know the other scouts in your den. (meeting)
  2. Say the Scout Oath with your den and adult partner. (pack opening, when the den stands together with its leader and the partners)
  3. Hear the Scout Law read; show trustworthy, helpful or friendly. (meeting)
  4. Show the sign, salute and handshake, and when each is used. (meeting)
  5. Tell your adult partner about a "Do Your Best" moment. (meeting or at home)
  6. Do the Parent's Guide youth-protection activities with a parent. (at home)
- Safety notes:
  - Req 6 is for families only. Leaders send the link and never run it themselves.
  - Two registered adults in the den room. Adult partners don't count unless they are registered. No one-on-one. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
  - Every Tiger attends with an adult partner, so there are no drop-offs.
  - The Req 3 maze is at walking pace on a clear floor, and the partner's eyes are closed, not blindfolded, so they can peek if needed.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Meet the Tiger Den · 39 min
- Done at the pack opening: Req 2 for any Tiger who can already say the Oath with the den while their adult partner stands with them. The den leader confirms who was present. Tigers still learning the words start on them tonight in step 4 and count at a later pack opening.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, email families:
  - A welcome, and the adult-partner rule: every Tiger comes with an adult partner who joins in, and there are no drop-offs.
  - The Parent's Guide link for Req 6.
  - The Tiger Handbook.
  - The uniform basics: blue Cub Scout shirt, navy web belt, Tiger cap, orange Tiger neckerchief and slide (uniform page above). Unlike the Lion uniform, scouting.org doesn't list the Tiger neckerchief as optional. Check the pack's expectations (see open questions).

  Confirm your second registered adult. Cut a poster-board den flag with one stripe per Tiger. Make name tags with each Tiger's first name and a tiger sticker. For the Oath puzzle, write or print the Oath one line per craft stick, 9 sticks per pair, and bundle each set with a rubber band.
- Supplies: name tags for Tigers and partners, soft ball, poster-board den flag, crayons, markers, Oath stick bundles (one per pair), Oath poster
- Tell parents before they leave: Please do the Parent's Guide activities with your Tiger at home (Req 6), and tell the den leader when you're done. Keep the Oath sticks and practice the Oath together this week; your Tiger says it with the den at a pack opening (Req 2). Help your Tiger notice one time they did their best, which they'll tell you about next time.
1. **Roar Your Name** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Stand up with your grown-up, give us your biggest tiger roar, and tell us your name and your favorite animal!"
   - How: 1) Pairs sit in a **circle**, with each Tiger in front of their adult partner. 2) Each Tiger **roars and says their name** and a favorite animal, and the partner says their own name. 3) In round two, the Tiger **rolls the ball** to someone and says that Tiger's name. 4) Go until every Tiger has **been named**.
   - Tip: **Shy Tiger?** A wave and a tiny roar count, and the partner can say the name.
2. **Find a Friend Who…** · den · 6 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Find a Tiger who has a dog, then give them a high five and say their name!"
   - How: 1) The leader calls **"Find a friend who…"** with things like has a brother, likes pizza, or rides a bike. 2) Pairs **walk quickly** to a match. 3) The Tiger **says the other Tiger's name**. 4) Do **five or six rounds**.
   - Tip: **No match?** That Tiger joins the leader's pair for the round.
3. **Den Flag Stripes** · den · 9 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Every Tiger gets a stripe on our den flag. Draw something you like on your stripe."
   - How: 1) Each Tiger **picks a stripe**. 2) Tigers **draw** a favorite thing: a pet, a food or a game. 3) Partners **write the first name** on the stripe. 4) Each Tiger **points to their stripe** and says what they drew.
   - Tip: **Crayons, not paint:** They're faster to clean up. The flag comes to every den meeting and can lead the den into pack openings.
4. **Oath Stick Puzzle** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 2 (set-up)
   - Say: "This is the Scout Oath, our Cub Scout promise. It's in pieces! Drop the sticks, pick them up one at a time, and put the promise back in order."
   - How: 1) The partner **reads each stick** aloud. 2) The Tiger **drops the bundle** from about a foot above the table. 3) Pair members take turns lifting **one stick without moving the others**. 4) Together they **line the sticks up in order** using the poster, then say the Oath once.
   - Tip: **Req 2 counts at a pack opening,** not here. Tonight is practice. The sticks go home.
5. **Do Your Best Preview** · den · 3 min · Reqs: 5 (set-up)
   - Say: "Our motto is 'Do Your Best.' This week, look for a time you tried really hard, and tell your grown-up about it next time."
   - How: 1) Give **one quick example**, like learning a new word or riding without training wheels. 2) Partners **agree to help** their Tiger notice one.
   - Tip: **Tiny counts:** Tying your shoes all by yourself is a great one.
6. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Tigers keep their den tidy. Every Tiger picks up three things with their grown-up!"
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up** in pairs. 2) Give a one-minute **leader thought**. 3) **Check out** each Tiger with their adult partner by name.
   - Tip: **Pickup:** If someone other than the adult partner is collecting a Tiger, confirm it before that Tiger leaves.
   - (at home) Req 6: families do the Parent's Guide activities. Practice the Oath with the sticks.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Tigers Keep Their Word · 38 min
- Done at the pack opening: Req 2 for any Tiger who said the Oath with the den tonight while their adult partner stood with them. The den leader confirms who was present. Tigers still learning it can count at any later pack opening.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, make a Scout Law poster with one simple picture beside each point, and circle Trustworthy, Helpful and Friendly. Bring 8–10 cones or chairs for a simple maze. On the night, put the den flag up before the pack opening.
- Supplies: Scout Law picture poster, cones or chairs, den flag, crayons, paper
- Tell parents before they leave: If you haven't finished the Parent's Guide activities (Req 6), please do them this week. If your Tiger didn't get to tell you their "Do Your Best" story tonight, you can do it at home (Req 5). Keep practicing the Oath for the pack opening.
1. **Scout Law Read-Aloud** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "The Scout Law is a list of ways a Scout tries to be. Grown-ups, read it to your Tiger. Tigers, point to the picture for each one."
   - How: 1) Pairs sit **side by side** at the poster. 2) Each **partner reads the Law** to their Tiger, or the den leader reads it to the whole den. 3) The Tiger **points to each picture**. 4) Stop on the three circled points: **"What does that look like?"**
   - Tip: **Either reader counts:** Req 3 allows the adult partner or the den leader to read it.
2. **Tiger Guide Maze** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Grown-ups, close your eyes. Tigers, you're the guide! Hold their hand and use your words to get them through the maze safely. That's being trustworthy and helpful."
   - How: 1) Set a **cone maze** on a clear floor. 2) The partner **closes their eyes**, and the Tiger holds their hand. 3) The Tiger **guides with words**: "Step left! Stop! Go straight!" 4) After each pair finishes, ask, **"Which Scout Law point did you show?"**
   - Tip: **Note Req 3** when each Tiger names Trustworthy, Helpful or Friendly and shows it. Walking pace only.
3. **If You're Happy and You Know It** · den · 6 min · Reqs: 4 (set-up)
   - Say: "Let's sing it Cub Scout style! If you're happy and you know it, give the sign!"
   - How: 1) Teach the **sign**: right arm straight up with two fingers raised like tiger ears. 2) Teach the **salute**: the same two fingers touch the eyebrow or cap brim. 3) Teach the **handshake**: right hand, with two fingers along the other person's wrist. (check the hand details against the Tiger handbook) 4) **Sing** a verse for each, and partners join in.
   - Tip: **Partners model it:** Tigers copy the grown-up facing them.
4. **Tiger Says: When Do We Use It?** · den · 6 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Tiger says, the flag is going by! What do you do? Tiger says, greet a Cub Scout! Tiger says, time to get quiet!"
   - How: 1) The leader calls a **situation**. 2) Tigers **do the right move**: salute for the flag, handshake to greet, sign for the Oath or quiet. 3) A Tiger who moves without "Tiger says" becomes a **helper**. 4) Each Tiger **shows all three** and says when to use each.
   - Tip: **Note Req 4** as each Tiger gets all three right.
5. **Do Your Best Whisper** · den · 6 min · Reqs: 5
   - Say: "Turn to your grown-up and whisper about a time you did your best. Grown-ups, listen, then draw it together."
   - How: 1) Pairs **turn toward each other**. 2) The Tiger **tells the story**. 3) Together they **draw it** on paper. 4) Volunteers **share** with the den.
   - Tip: **The requirement is telling the partner.** Sharing with the den is optional. Note who told their partner.
   - (at home) Req 5 can be finished at home with the adult partner.
6. **Handshake Line & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Let's use our new Cub Scout handshake on the way out!"
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 2) Tigers leave through a **handshake line** past the leaders. 3) **Check out** each Tiger with their adult partner by name.
   - Tip: **Maze cones:** Stack them before the handshake line, so no one trips.

## Team Tiger (Tiger)
- Citizenship · Meetings: 2 · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/team-tiger/ · Other sources: https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Tiger-Adventure-Requirements.pdf, https://scoutermom.com/19282/team-tiger-adventure-cub-scout/, https://www.scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/2025-holiday-letters-for-military/, https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/healthsafety/pdf/680-027.pdf, https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/healthsafety/pdf/680-028.pdf · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: Tigers learn why games have rules, how to be a good teammate, and how to do a job for their team. Then they serve. All four requirements are required. The official Req 2 ideas are all pack or school jobs: a flag ceremony, greeters at a pack meeting, or set-up and clean-up. So the Tigers' turn leading the pack opening fits this requirement well. Scouter Mom estimates 1–2 meetings.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Play a game with rules; talk about why rules matter. (meeting; family also allowed)
  2. With your adult partner, pick a team job; do it once. (chosen in the meeting; done at a pack opening, pack meeting or den night)
  3. Play a two-team game; talk about being a good teammate. (meeting; family also allowed)
  4. Take part in a service project. (meeting or outing)
- Safety notes:
  - The official page asks leaders to use the Service Project Planning Checklist and SAFE Project Tool Use (links above). SAFE Project Tool Use doesn't let Lions and Tigers use paint brushes on a service project. Painting in arts and crafts is fine.
  - Off-site service needs two registered adults, permission, transport planning and the council outing process (check). If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
  - Use a beach ball, not balloons: broken balloons are a choking risk for children under 8, and some scouts have latex allergies.
  - Beanbags are thrown at the target only, never at people.
  - Cards for the military: first names only.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Rules and Teams · 39 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, ask the Cubmaster when the Tigers' next turn to lead the pack opening flag ceremony is, and which pack-meeting jobs (greeters, set-up, clean-up) the Tigers can take. Make picture job cards: color guard (carry the flag with your partner), caller, greeter at the door, chair set-up crew, clean-up crew, den flag carrier. Set up a cornhole board or a box target, a throw line, and a tape "net" line for volleyball.
- Supplies: beanbags (3 per pair), cornhole board or box target, painter's tape, a light beach ball, picture job cards, a take-home job slip for each pair
- Tell parents before they leave: You and your Tiger picked a team job tonight (Req 2). The slip says when it happens. Please arrive 10 minutes early that night. Next meeting we make holiday cards for service members, so there's nothing to bring.
1. **Beanbag Toss** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Here are the rules: stand behind the line, one thrower at a time, and your grown-up keeps score. Let's play!"
   - How: 1) The leader says **three simple rules**. 2) Pairs **take turns**: the Tiger throws three beanbags and the partner **counts points**. 3) Play **two rounds**.
   - Tip: **Shorter line for small arms:** Let Tigers step closer. Partners fetch the beanbags.
2. **The No-Rules Round** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Now let's play with NO rules. Everybody throw at once! … Hmm. How did that go? Why do games need rules?"
   - How: 1) Give **10 seconds** of everyone throwing at the target at once. 2) **Sit down** and collect the beanbags. 3) Ask, **"What went wrong?"** and "Why do rules help?" 4) Each Tiger gives **one reason**, with their partner helping.
   - Tip: **Keep it short and safe:** Throw at the target only. Note Req 1 when each Tiger gives a reason.
3. **Beach Ball Volleyball** · den · 11 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Two teams, one beach ball. Keep it up and bat it over the line. Everyone on your team gets a turn to hit it!"
   - How: 1) Make **two teams**, with partners on the same team as their Tiger. 2) Tigers **bat the ball** over the tape line. 3) Count how many hits in a row before it lands. 4) **Mix the teams** halfway.
   - Tip: **Tigers hit, partners help:** Partners keep the ball in play but let Tigers take the hits.
4. **Good Teammate Huddle** · den · 3 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "What did a good teammate do in that game?"
   - How: 1) **Huddle up**. 2) Each Tiger gives **one idea**, like cheering, sharing turns, or not getting mad. 3) Note who **answered**.
   - Tip: **Prompt:** "A good teammate ____."
5. **Pick Our Team Jobs** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 2 (set-up)
   - Say: "Our pack is a team, and every team needs helpers. With your grown-up, pick a job you'll really do."
   - How: 1) Hold up the **picture job cards** and say what each job is. 2) Pairs **choose one** together. 3) The partner **writes the date** on the take-home slip.
   - Tip: **Spread the jobs out** so the Tigers can cover a whole flag ceremony on their turn.
6. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Team clean-up! Every Tiger picks up three beanbags or pieces of tape."
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up** in pairs. 2) Make sure each pair has its **job slip**. 3) **Check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Home note:** Point partners to the date on the slip.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Tigers Serve · 36 min
- Done at the pack opening: Req 2 on whatever night a Tiger does a pack-opening job they chose, such as the Tigers' turn to lead the flag ceremony. The den leader confirms who did their job. Jobs done at a pack meeting count there instead.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, confirm the project.
  - In the meeting (this plan): holiday cards for military personnel, the nationwide project on the official page. The official page says to mail them to the Scouting America National Service Center by the ship-by date, **[date]** (2026: Nov 6). Hold this meeting by late October so the den leader can mail the cards in time. **The Tiger den mails its own cards.** The 2025 project page (it now shows the 2026 deadline) gives the address as 10,000 for the Troops, Scouting America, 1325 West Walnut Hill Lane, Irving, TX 75015-2079. It asks for the pack number, city and state on each card, the scout's first name only, all cards shipped in one package, and the pack number and council in the return address. The date is a ship-by deadline (2025 page, checked 2026-09-30). Check the address for 2026.
  - Off-site option: a council Scouting for Food event (check the NEGA dates). Complete the Service Project Planning Checklist. Collect permission slips and health forms, confirm two registered adults, and follow the council outing process (check). The off-site version is an outing: change the header to "(outing, about 60 min)".
- Supplies: cardstock folded into cards, crayons, markers, stickers, one shipping envelope or box, a sample card
- Tell parents before they leave: The den leader will mail the cards this week. If your Tiger hasn't done their team job yet (Req 2), the date is on your slip.
1. **Why We Serve** · den · 3 min · Reqs: 4 (set-up)
   - Say: "Some grown-ups serve our country far from home, even on holidays. Our den is going to make them cards so they know we're thinking of them."
   - How: 1) Show the **sample card**. 2) Say **who gets them**. 3) Say the **first names only** rule.
   - Tip: **Keep it cheerful:** This is about thanking people, not about danger.
2. **Cards for Service Members** · den · 18 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Draw a happy holiday picture on the front. Then tell your grown-up what to write inside."
   - How: 1) Tigers **draw the front**. 2) The partner **writes the message** the Tiger says. 3) The Tiger **writes their first name**. 4) Partners add the **pack number, city and state**. 5) Make **two cards each** if there's time.
   - Tip: **First names only,** and no photos of scouts inside the cards. Use markers and crayons; no paint brushes on a service project.
3. **Tiger Stretch** · den · 3 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Stand up! Stretch like a tiger waking up, prowl, and pounce!"
   - How: 1) Call **tiger moves**. 2) Partners **join in**. 3) Finish with a **quiet sit-down**.
   - Tip: **Use it anytime** Tigers start to melt.
4. **Why We Help** · den · 4 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Who are our cards for, and how will they feel when they open them?"
   - How: 1) **Circle up**. 2) Each Tiger gives **one feeling word**. 3) Note who **took part** for the Advancement board.
   - Tip: **Prompt:** "They'll feel ____."
5. **Team Job Check-In** · den · 3 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Thumbs up if you did your team job! What did you do?"
   - How: 1) **Thumbs up or down**. 2) Two or three Tigers **share**. 3) Note who **finished Req 2**.
   - Tip: **Not yet?** No problem. Their job night is on the slip.
6. **Den Roar & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Loudest den roar for the people we helped! Then clean up."
   - How: 1) Give the **den roar**. 2) **Collect** the cards for mailing. 3) Clean up and **check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Off-site:** Tigers thank the host, everyone uses **hand sanitizer**, and you do a **headcount** before check-out.

## Tiger Circles (Tiger)
- Family & Reverence · Meetings: 1 · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/tiger-circles/ · Other sources: https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Tiger-Adventure-Requirements.pdf, https://www.scouting.org/awards/religious-awards/ · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: Families lead the faith parts. The den does an act of kindness (Req 3) and gives Tigers time to draw for Req 1 after their family talk. The official page says the adventure can be done at home or as a den. If it's done as a den, every parent must know the content ahead of time and may opt out and do it at home. A Tiger can also earn this adventure by completing a religious emblem of the family's choosing.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Talk faith traditions with a parent; name three; draw your favorite. (at home; the drawing can be made in the meeting)
  2. Attend a religious service or reverence gathering with family. (at home)
  3. Do an act of kindness. (meeting or at home)
- Safety notes:
  - Leaders do not teach or lead religious content. The official page calls Scouting America absolutely nonsectarian.
  - Every family's traditions count. No Tiger has to share beliefs aloud.
  - Tell families ahead of time and let them opt out of the in-den drawing (official page).
  - The official page lists attending a Veterans Day event with the den as one Req 2 activity (see open questions).

### Meeting 1 of 1 · Kindness and Celebrations · 39 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, send families a note:
  - Reqs 1 and 2 are family-led at home.
  - Before this meeting, please talk with your Tiger about your family's faith traditions and three holidays or celebrations. The drawing at the meeting is based on that talk.
  - Here is what the den will do: make a kindness card and draw a favorite celebration. If you'd rather your Tiger draw at home, tell the den leader, and your Tiger will make a second kindness card instead.
  - A religious emblem is another way to earn the adventure (link above).
  - Our chartered organization is a local church. Going to one of its services is one option for Req 2, never a requirement. Any faith's service or reverence gathering counts, and each family chooses its own.

  Fold cardstock into cards and set out sticker sheets. Make a named bag for each Tiger.
- Supplies: folded cardstock, crayons, markers, stickers, drawing paper, named bags for taking work home
- Tell parents before they leave: Your Tiger made a kindness card tonight. Please help them give it this week, because giving it finishes Req 3. Reqs 1 and 2 happen at home and are led by your family.
1. **Kindness Detectives** · den · 6 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "A Scout is Kind. I'll act out a moment. Jump up if you can think of a kind thing to do!"
   - How: 1) Two leaders act **quick scenes**: a kid sitting alone at recess, someone dropping their crayons, a friend who is sad. 2) Tigers **jump up** and say the kind fix. 3) Pairs **act out one fix** together, like inviting the lonely kid to play.
   - Tip: **Made-up kids only.** Don't use real classmates' names.
2. **Card of Kindness** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Think of someone special: a grandparent, a teacher, a neighbor. Make them a card that will make them smile."
   - How: 1) The Tiger **tells their partner** who it's for. 2) The Tiger **decorates the front**. 3) The partner **writes the message** the Tiger says inside. 4) The Tiger **writes their first name**, and the card goes in their bag.
   - Tip: **Giving it is the act of kindness.** Note who gave their card at the next meeting.
   - (at home) The Tiger gives the card this week.
3. **Tiger Wiggle** · den · 3 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Cards done! Everybody do a happy dance with your grown-up, and freeze when the music stops!"
   - How: 1) Play a **short song**. 2) Everyone **dances**. 3) Everyone **freezes** when it stops.
   - Tip: **Freeze-dance** settles the den before drawing.
4. **My Family Celebration** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Draw your favorite holiday or celebration that your family talked about at home."
   - How: 1) Tigers **draw**. 2) The partner **labels it** if the Tiger wants. 3) Opt-out Tigers **make a second kindness card** instead.
   - Tip: **Leaders don't suggest which celebration to pick.** If a family missed the home talk, the Tiger can draw now and finish the talk at home.
   - (at home) The rest of Req 1 is the family talk.
5. **Gallery Walk (optional)** · den · 3 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "If you want to, hold up your picture and tell us one thing about it."
   - How: 1) Ask for **volunteers only**. 2) Everyone gives a **silent cheer**. 3) Thank every **presenter**.
   - Tip: **No one has to share beliefs.** Don't call on anyone.
6. **Kind Words & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Families celebrate different things, and that's great. Let's clean up with kind hands."
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 2) Check that every card and drawing is **in its bag**. 3) **Check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Home note:** Remind partners that giving the card finishes Req 3.
   - (at home) Req 2 with family, plus giving the card.

## Tigers in the Wild (Tiger)
- Outdoors · Meetings: 2 (one den meeting plus one outing) · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/tigers-in-the-wild/ · Other sources: https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Tiger-Adventure-Requirements.pdf, https://scoutermom.com/19271/tigers-in-the-wild-adventure-cub-scout/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/the-four-cs-of-the-outdoor-code/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/leaf-and-bark-rubbing/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/pinecone-bird-feeder/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/grab-the-six-essentials/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/safety-moments/weather-related-safety/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/safety-moments/anaphylaxis/ · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: The den night covers the Six Essentials, the Outdoor Code and wild versus domesticated animals. On the outing, each Tiger and adult partner walk for 20 minutes or more, spotting natural and man-made things, and study a tree. The walk can be done with the den, the pack or family, so a Tiger who misses it can make it up with family. Scouter Mom estimates 1–2 meetings. The Six Essentials are a first-aid kit, water bottle, flashlight, trail food, sun protection and a whistle (Scouter Mom; see wolf.md question 17, check).
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Name the Six Essentials and show what each is for. (meeting)
  2. Learn the Outdoor Code with your den leader or partner. (meeting)
  3. Walk 20+ minutes with Essentials; spot natural and man-made things with partner. (outing; with den, pack or family)
  4. Name local animals, sort wild and domesticated, draw your favorite. (meeting)
  5. Find a tree near home; tell how it helps. (outing, or at home)
- Safety notes:
  - The walk is an outing: two registered adults, permission, and the council outing process (check). If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
  - The official page asks leaders to:
    - use the SAFE Checklist
    - know the Guide to Safe Scouting and the Age-Appropriate Guidelines
    - take Hazardous Weather training (my.scouting.org)
    - watch the weather safety-moment video
    - review every scout's health record
    - use the buddy system and active supervision
  - If a scout carries an EpiPen, at least one other adult must know how to use it (official page).
  - Each Tiger's buddy is their adult partner, and pairs stay together the whole walk.
  - Postpone for lightning. Plan for Georgia heat and water. Know poison ivy ("leaves of three"). Do a tick check afterward. Don't touch wildlife.
  - Restrooms: a parent takes their own Tiger. Otherwise, buddies go together and an adult waits outside. There is no one-on-one.
  - Trail food: check allergies.
  - Fire: the Outdoor Code's "careful with fire" is talk only. There is no fire on this walk.
  - If you swap in the official pinecone bird feeder, the official page says to check for peanut allergies and use sunflower butter instead. Plastic knives only.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Ready for the Wild · 37 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before:
  - Build a demo Six Essentials kit and set the six items across the room for a relay.
  - Make four big "C" signs for the Outdoor Code: Clean, Careful, Considerate, Conservation-minded (official Four C's activity page).
  - Print animal picture cards: dog, cat, horse, cow, chicken, goat, deer, squirrel, raccoon, rabbit, opossum, fox, turtle, hawk.
  - Make two hula-hoop "homes," one labeled with a house picture and one with a tree picture.
  - Set the walk date and place, and have permission slips ready.
- Supplies: demo Six Essentials kit, 4 "C" signs, animal picture cards, 2 hula hoops with picture labels, drawing paper, crayons, permission slips, walk info sheets with a packing list
- Tell parents before they leave: The Tiger walk is [date/place]. Please return the permission slip and health form. Help your Tiger pack their own Six Essentials, and wear closed-toe shoes and weather-right clothes.
1. **Six Essentials Relay** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "These six things go on every Cub Scout hike. I'll tell you a trail problem. Run to the thing that helps, hold it up, and tell us what you'd do with it!"
   - How: 1) **Hold up each item** and show its use. 2) Call a **problem**: a scraped knee, getting dark, lost, hot sun, thirsty, hungry. 3) One Tiger at a time **runs to the item**, with their partner walking beside them. 4) The Tiger **says what they'd do** with it.
   - Tip: **Note Req 1** when each Tiger has named all six and shown one use.
2. **The Four C's** · den · 9 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "The Outdoor Code is how Scouts take care of the outdoors. It has four C's. Let's give each one a move!"
   - How: 1) Hold up each **C sign** and explain it in kid words: keep it clean, be careful with fire, be kind to people and animals outside, and save nature for later. 2) Pairs **make up a motion** for each C. 3) The leader does a motion and Tigers **shout the C**. 4) Pairs **tell each other** one way they'll follow the Code on the walk.
   - Tip: **Fire talk only:** Tigers never light fires. Adults handle fire at pack campouts.
3. **Wild or Home?** · den · 13 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Some animals live with people and some live wild. Flip a card, name the animal, and run it to the right home: the house or the tree!"
   - How: 1) Spread the **cards face down**. 2) Each pair **flips one**, and the Tiger names it. 3) The Tiger **runs it to a hoop**. 4) Then each Tiger **draws their favorite animal**, and the partner writes "wild" or "home" under it.
   - Tip: **Tricky ones:** A rabbit or turtle can be a pet or wild. Say both answers are OK and ask why.
4. **Walk Briefing & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Next time we explore outside! Bring your Six Essentials and your whistle."
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 2) Hand each **partner** the info sheet and permission slip. 3) **Check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Put the sheet in the partner's hand,** not a Tiger's pocket.
   - (at home) Tigers pack their own Six Essentials with their partner.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Tiger Explorer Walk (outing, about 55 min)
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, pick a short loop at a park with restrooms and big trees (a local park, check). Walk it yourself first. Send families the address and meeting time. Collect permission slips and health forms. Confirm two registered adults. Follow the council outing process (check). The adult in charge completes Hazardous Weather training and the SAFE Checklist. Check the forecast the day before and that morning. Postpone for lightning.
- Supplies: each Tiger's Six Essentials, "Natural or Man-Made?" picture sheets, crayons with the paper peeled off, plain paper, clipboards, trash bag, leader first-aid kit, health forms, extra water
- Tell parents before they leave: Please do a tick check tonight. If your Tiger missed the walk, you can do a 20-minute family walk with your Six Essentials, spotting natural and man-made things, and find a tree near home. Tell the den leader what you found.
1. **Check-In & Essentials Check** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "Show me your Six Essentials and your shoes. Are you ready to explore?"
   - How: 1) **Sign in** each pair and collect any missing forms. 2) Each Tiger **shows** their Essentials. 3) **Check shoes and clothes**. 4) Everyone takes a **restroom stop**, following the restroom rule.
   - Tip: **Missed Meeting 1?** Have that Tiger name each Essential and show its use here, which covers Req 1.
2. **Walk Rules & the Four C's** · den · 4 min · Reqs: 2 (set-up)
   - Say: "Show me your four C moves! Now, one grown-up and one Tiger stay together the whole walk."
   - How: 1) Do the **Four C motions**. 2) Point out **poison ivy** if you've seen it. 3) One adult leads at the **front**, and one sweeps at the **back**.
   - Tip: **Needs a second registered adult.** The sweep never lets a pair fall behind.
3. **I Spy: Natural or Man-Made, Part 1** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Natural things come from nature. Man-made things were built by people. With your grown-up, spy as many of each as you can!"
   - How: 1) **Walk** at Tiger pace. 2) The Tiger **spots something** and says "natural" or "man-made." 3) The partner **colors a box** on the sheet. 4) Stop to **look and listen** for 30 seconds.
   - Tip: **Start the clock here:** Req 3 needs 20 or more minutes of walking and exploring.
4. **Tree Detectives** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 5
   - Say: "Pick a big tree. Make a rubbing of its bark and one fallen leaf. Then tell your grown-up how this tree helps."
   - How: 1) Pairs **pick a tree** along the path. 2) The partner **holds the paper** on the bark while the Tiger **rubs with a crayon**. 3) The Tiger does a **leaf rubbing** from a fallen leaf. 4) The Tiger names **one way it helps**: shade, homes for animals, clean air or food.
   - Tip: **Look before you touch:** Check the trunk for poison ivy vines and ants first. Use fallen leaves only.
   - (at home) A Tiger who misses the walk can find a tree near home with a parent.
5. **I Spy: Natural or Man-Made, Part 2** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Head back, explorers. Can you find something natural and man-made right next to each other?"
   - How: 1) **Finish the loop**. 2) Take a **water break**. 3) Pairs **keep spotting**. 4) Everyone **packs out** their own trash.
   - Tip: **Don't cut it short:** Parts 1 and 2 must add up to 20 or more minutes of walking.
6. **Share Circle** · den · 4 min · Reqs: 3, 5
   - Say: "Tell us one natural thing, one man-made thing, and how your tree helps."
   - How: 1) **Circle up** at the start point. 2) Each Tiger **shares**, pointing to their sheet and rubbing. 3) Note who finished **Reqs 3 and 5**.
   - Tip: **Shy Tiger?** They can point to the pictures on their sheet.
7. **Headcount & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Last headcount! Great exploring, Tigers."
   - How: 1) Count **every pair**. 2) Do a **trash sweep**. 3) **Check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Tick reminder:** Say it to every partner at check-out.

## Tiger Bites (Tiger)
- Personal Fitness · Meetings: 2 · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/tiger-bites/ · Other sources: https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Tiger-Adventure-Requirements.pdf, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/food-group-tiger-relay/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/scout-oath-and-law-hand-washing/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/when-should-i-wash-my-hands-game/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/tiger-get-ready-for-bed/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/tiger-time-to-sleep/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/tiger-obstacle-course/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/tiger-red-light-green-light/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/my-favorite-snack/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/safety-moments/food-allergies/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/safety-moments/anaphylaxis/ · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: Tigers learn the food groups, hand-washing, 30 minutes of being active, and ways that help them sleep. Tigers need **30** active minutes, not the Lions' 20, so the active games get a meeting to themselves. This plan uses toy food or picture cards, so no food is served. The official "My Favorite Snack" activity uses real food; if the den does it, follow every food rule below.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Name the five food groups. (meeting)
  2. Practice hand-washing and say when to wash. (meeting)
  3. Be active for 30 minutes. (meeting)
  4. Practice ways that help you sleep. (meeting; practiced again at home)
- Safety notes:
  - If any real food is served, collect food allergies from health records and families first, and ask about religious or cultural food needs (official page).
  - If a scout carries an EpiPen, at least one other adult must know how to use it (official page).
  - With real food, keep cold food in a cooler, wash hands first, and have adults do any cutting.
  - The official obstacle course needs a level space with no tripping hazards. Pool noodles and cones are softer than the PVC hurdles on the activity page.
  - Take water breaks, and check the heat if you're outdoors.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Clean Hands, Food Groups, Good Sleep · 38 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, confirm a sink you can use. Gather 15 toy foods or picture cards, three for each group, and label five boxes: Fruits, Vegetables, Grains, Protein, Dairy (official relay page). Print a hand-washing picture poster and bedtime picture cards: bath, pajamas, brush teeth, story, lights out.
- Supplies: hand soap, paper towels, hand-washing poster, Oath and Law poster, 15 toy foods or picture cards, pillowcase, 5 labeled boxes, bedtime picture cards, mats or towels
- Tell parents before they leave: Ask your Tiger to wash their hands while saying the Scout Oath and Law. Tigers this age need about 9–12 hours of sleep (official activity page), so try the bedtime order you made tonight. Next meeting is very active: send sneakers and a water bottle.
1. **Wash or Freeze?** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "I'll call out a time. If you should wash your hands, jump! If you shouldn't, freeze!"
   - How: 1) Tigers and partners **line up**. 2) Call **situations**: before eating, after the bathroom, after playing outside, after sneezing into your hand, talking to a friend, looking at a book. 3) Tigers **jump or freeze**. 4) Each Tiger **names one time** to wash.
   - Tip: **Partners play too,** and they're allowed to get it wrong on purpose.
2. **Oath & Law Scrub** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Wet, soap, scrub palms, between fingers, thumbs and fingertips, rinse, dry. Keep scrubbing until you and your grown-up finish saying the Scout Oath and Law!"
   - How: 1) Show the **steps** on the poster. 2) Pairs take turns at the **sink**, and the partner **says the Oath and Law** with the Tiger while they scrub. 3) Use a paper towel to **turn off the tap**. 4) Waiting pairs **play Wash or Freeze** with a leader.
   - Tip: **Note Req 2** once each Tiger has washed and named a time to wash. One sink? Start the relay setup with the other leader.
3. **Food Group Tiger Relay** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Five food groups, five boxes. Pull a food from the bag, run it to the right box, and run back to tag your team!"
   - How: 1) Name the **five groups** at their boxes. 2) Make **two teams** about 5 yards back. 3) Each Tiger **pulls a food**, says its group, and **runs it to the box**. 4) Partners **cheer and check** each answer, and a wrong box means another try.
   - Tip: **Note Req 1** when each Tiger has named all five groups.
4. **Sleepy Tiger Routine** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Tigers need lots of sleep to grow. Put the bedtime pictures in order, then let's practice a sleepy-tiger breath."
   - How: 1) Pairs **put the cards in order**. 2) Partners **work out a bedtime** from wake-up time. 3) Tigers **lie down** by their partner for **belly breathing**: in slow, out slow, five times. 4) Ask, "**What helps you fall asleep?**"
   - Tip: **Note Req 4** once each Tiger has ordered a routine and tried the breathing.
   - (at home) Try the routine at bedtime this week.
5. **Wipe Down & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Tell your grown-up your favorite food group, then let's clean up."
   - How: 1) **Collect** the foods and cards. 2) **Wipe the sink area**. 3) **Check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Toy food** goes in the pillowcase so no pieces are left behind.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Tigers on the Move · 36 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, book a gym or open floor, or a field with daylight. Remind families to send sneakers and water. Build a soft obstacle course: 3 pool-noodle hurdles on cones, 2 "duck under" noodles, a tape line to balance on, and 6 hula hoops to hop through, with about 10 feet between stations. Bring a soft soccer ball and 4 cones for goals.
- Supplies: pool noodles, cones, painter's tape, 6 hula hoops, stopwatch, soft soccer ball, water
- Tell parents before they leave: Your Tiger was active for 30 minutes tonight. Ask them which obstacle was hardest. Keep up the bedtime routine.
1. **Warm-Up & Tiger Says** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Tiger says, reach for the sky! Tiger says, touch your toes! Jump! Oops, Tiger didn't say!"
   - How: 1) Do a **quick stretch**: arm circles, toe touches, big reach. 2) Play **Tiger Says**, and partners play too. 3) A Tiger who moves by mistake becomes a **helper**. 4) Point out the **boundaries**.
   - Tip: **Start the clock:** Req 3's 30 minutes start here.
2. **Tiger Obstacle Course** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Jump the hurdles, duck under the bars, walk the balance line, and hop through the hoops. Go as fast as you safely can!"
   - How: 1) A leader **shows the course** once. 2) Tigers **go one at a time**, and partners walk alongside at the balance line. 3) Partners **time** their Tiger. 4) Everyone gets **at least two runs** and tries to beat their own time.
   - Tip: **Beat yourself, not each other:** Compare to your own first run.
3. **Red Light, Green Light** · den · 6 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Green light means go, red light means freeze! If you move on red, back to the start!"
   - How: 1) Tigers and partners line up **2 feet apart**. 2) The caller stands **about 20 feet away**. 3) The first one to the caller **becomes the caller**. 4) Take a **water break** after.
   - Tip: **Partners move too,** and they're slow on purpose.
4. **Tiger Soccer** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Two teams, one ball, no hands! Kick it into the other team's goal."
   - How: 1) Make **two teams** with partners on their Tiger's team. 2) **Cone off** two goals. 3) Tigers **kick**, and partners **pass back** to Tigers. 4) **Mix teams** halfway.
   - Tip: **Note Req 3** now. Steps 1–4 add up to 31 active minutes.
5. **Cool-Down Walk & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Slow tiger walk, deep breaths. Now help pick up the course."
   - How: 1) Take a **slow lap**. 2) **Stack the cones** and noodles. 3) **Check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Water bottles:** Check the water line so no bottle gets left.

- Summary: Parents lead the Protect Yourself Rules video at home. Shout, Run, Tell counts at the den relay when the Tiger's adult partner is their parent (Keith, 2026-09-30); a Tiger whose partner isn't a parent finishes it at home. The den covers reaching emergency help and what to do if lost, using S.A.W.: **Stay, Answer, Whistle** (stay put, answer when your name is called, and blow three short whistle blasts; official activity pages). The official page says the adventure may be done at home or as a den, and that parents, guardians and adult partners must be told the content before the meeting.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. With a parent's OK, watch the Tiger Protect Yourself Rules video. (at home)
  2. Show Shout, Run, Tell with your adult partner. (meeting, when the partner is the parent; otherwise at home)
  3. With your adult partner, show how to reach emergency help. (meeting)
  4. With your adult partner, show what to do if lost. (meeting)
- Safety notes:
  - Send the official parent notification (linked above) before anything else.
  - The video is 13 minutes, and a parent or legal guardian watches it with their scout (activity page). Don't show it in the den.
  - If a scout discloses abuse: stay calm, listen, and don't question or investigate. (1) Make sure the child is safe; call 911 for an injury or immediate danger. (2) **You personally** report within 24 hours to Georgia DFCS, 1-855-GACHILD (1-855-422-4453), or to local law enforcement. You can't hand this to another leader. (3) Then notify the NEGA Scout executive; if you can't reach them, call Scouts First, 1-844-SCOUTS1 (1-844-726-8871). Sources: scouting.org/training/safeguarding-youth/, gss01, Georgia DFCS.
  - The official activities have families list five trusted adults and their phone numbers. That list stays at home with the family, and the den doesn't ask Tigers to name trusted adults aloud.
  - 911 practice: Tigers use pretend phones only. Partners may *show* where the emergency call button is on their own phone, but no one presses it. The activity page confirms no real call is placed. If a call goes through by accident, stay on the line and tell the dispatcher it was a mistake.
  - Hide-and-seek happens in a marked area in daylight, or in a gym, free of obstacles (activity page). Each Tiger hides with their own adult partner. Hiding spots stay in sight of a registered leader, so no adult is out of view with a child who isn't their own (see open questions).
  - Whistles are for practice and emergencies only.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Shout, Run, Tell and Get Help · 36 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, email families the parent notification and the video link, and ask them to watch it with their Tiger before this meeting (Req 1). Tell them what the den will practice, as the official page asks. Leaders review the Protect Yourself Rules and the disclosure steps. Make one cardboard or toy phone per pair. Print picture cards: a house on fire, someone who won't wake up, a car crash, someone who can't breathe, a lost toy, a scraped knee, spilled milk, a lost library book.
- Supplies: pretend phones, emergency picture cards, cones, crayons
- Tell parents before they leave: If you haven't yet, please watch the Protect Yourself Rules video with your Tiger (Req 1). If you weren't your Tiger's partner tonight, please practice Shout, Run, Tell at home (Req 2). The handbook's trusted-adults page is for you to do together at home. Next meeting may be outdoors, so wear sneakers and bring the whistle from the Six Essentials.
1. **Shout, Run, Tell Relay** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 2 (when the partner is the parent)
   - Say: "If something ever feels wrong, you Shout, Run away, and Tell a grown-up you trust. Let's practice with your grown-up!"
   - How: 1) Tigers line up **about 10 yards** from their partners. 2) On "Go," each Tiger **shouts their own words**, like "No!" or "Stay away from me!" 3) They **run to their partner**. 4) They say, **"I need to tell you something."** Every Tiger takes **two turns**.
   - Tip: **Keep it general.** Don't discuss the video's details, because that talk belongs to parents. If a Tiger discloses something, follow the disclosure steps.
   - Note who finished Req 2: Tigers whose partner tonight is their parent. The rest finish it at home with a parent.
   - (at home) Req 1 (the video) is done at home with a parent.
2. **Is It a 911?** · den · 7 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "911 is for big emergencies, when someone is badly hurt or in danger. I'll show a picture. Jump up if it's a 911, sit down if it isn't!"
   - How: 1) Show each **picture card**. 2) Tigers **jump or sit**. 3) For each "sit," ask, **"Who could help instead?"**
   - Tip: **Never a joke:** Police, firefighters and paramedics are busy with real emergencies.
3. **Find the Button, Make the Call** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Grown-ups, show your Tiger where the emergency button is on your phone, but don't touch it! Tigers, use your pretend phone to call 9-1-1, and your grown-up will be the helper who answers."
   - How: 1) Each partner **shows the emergency button** on their own lock screen, with fingers off. 2) The Tiger **dials 9-1-1** on the pretend phone. 3) The partner, as the **dispatcher**, asks: "What's the emergency? Where are you? Who's hurt?" 4) Do **two scenarios**, and the Tiger **stays on the line** until the dispatcher says to hang up.
   - Tip: **Address privacy:** The Tiger says their address only to their own partner, not to the den. Note who finished Req 3.
4. **Helper Moves** · den · 4 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Who comes when you call 911? Show me a firefighter spraying a hose! A police officer directing traffic! A paramedic carrying a stretcher!"
   - How: 1) Call out **helpers**. 2) Tigers and partners **act them out**.
   - Tip: **Wiggle break** before the closing.
5. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Great job, safe Tigers. Let's put the phones away."
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 2) Hand each partner the **home reminder**. 3) **Check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Partner in hand:** Give the note to the adult, not the Tiger.
   - (at home) The trusted-adults page with a parent.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Lost? S.A.W.! · 36 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, choose a space where whistles won't bother anyone: a gym, or a fenced field in daylight (check with the site). Mark the play area with cones and walk it for hazards. Remind families to bring whistles, and have spares. Make a blank "My Tiger Info Card" for each pair, with lines for the Tiger's name, a parent's name and phone number, and the pack number.
- Supplies: whistles (plus spares), cones, info cards, pencils, water
- Tell parents before they leave: Keep the info card in your Tiger's backpack or Six Essentials kit, and practice your phone number together. If you haven't finished Reqs 1–2 at home, please do them this week and tell the den leader.
1. **S.A.W. and Three Blasts** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "If you ever get lost, remember S.A.W.: Stay right where you are. Answer when you hear your name. Whistle three short blasts, over and over, until someone finds you."
   - How: 1) Teach a motion for each: **Stay** (hug a tree), **Answer** (hand to your ear), **Whistle** (three fingers up). 2) Every Tiger **says S.A.W.** with the motions. 3) One Tiger at a time blows **three short blasts**. 4) Ask, "**Why stay put?**" (Grown-ups look where they last saw you.)
   - Tip: **Loud room:** Partners may cover their Tiger's ears between turns.
2. **Tiger Hide-and-Seek** · den · 14 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Two Tiger teams hide with their grown-ups. Everyone else looks. If you're not found by the whistle, it's time to show us S.A.W.!"
   - How: 1) **Two pairs hide** inside the cones for 30 seconds while the others cover their eyes. 2) Seekers **look for about 3 minutes**. 3) If a pair isn't found, the leader gives **one whistle blast**, and the hidden pair **stays put, answers** when called, and **blows three blasts** until found. 4) Rotate so **every pair hides once**.
   - Tip: **Note Req 4** as each pair shows S.A.W. with the partner. Hiding spots stay in a leader's view.
3. **My Tiger Info Card** · den · 7 min · Reqs: 4 (set-up)
   - Say: "If you're ever lost, it helps to have your grown-up's phone number with you. Let's make a card for your backpack."
   - How: 1) The Tiger **writes their first name** and decorates the card. 2) The partner **writes the parent's name and phone number**. 3) The Tiger **whispers the number** back to their partner. 4) The card goes **home with the family**.
   - Tip: **Privacy:** Leaders don't collect or photograph the cards.
4. **Whistle Put-Away & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Whistles away until a real emergency! Help pick up the cones."
   - How: 1) **Pack the whistles** into the Six Essentials kits. 2) **Stack the cones**. 3) **Check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Headcount first** if you played outdoors.
