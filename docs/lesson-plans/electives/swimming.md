# Swimming electives, all ranks (DRAFT 2, 40-minute den time)

> **A guide, not the rulebook.** These are ideas to help a den leader run the meeting. They are not a complete or official source. The official requirements on scouting.org and the Guide to Safe Scouting always come first. If something here is unclear or unsettled, use your judgment and check with the Cubmaster.

Researched 2026-09-30 by the cubmaster-program agent, using the format of Fishing (draft 2) and Cycling (draft 2). Draft 2 was revised the same day with the pack's answers. This is the third theme in the elective pass: one adventure that every rank has under its own name. Every plan here is written in our own words. Requirements are only summarized, so use the official page for the full text. Anything marked "(check)" still needs confirming. Nothing is in the app yet.

How these plans fit a Pack 569 den night:
- The pack does the opening ceremony together: flag, Pledge, Oath and Law. Each den then splits off for about 40 minutes.
- Den meetings here have no gathering or opening steps. Each one is den steps plus one short den closing. The minutes add up to 35–40 and never go over 40.
- "Reqs: N" means the step does real work on that requirement. "Reqs: N (set-up)" means the step only sets up or reinforces it and does not complete it.
- The guides don't tick requirements. Leaders record progress on the app's Advancement board afterward.
- **No den or pack swims (pack rule).** Pack 569 never runs a swim. Each rank has:
  - **One den night** (40 minutes or less) in the usual den room, on dry land. It covers water safety, buddy tags and the buddy board, getting in and out, reach-throw-don't-go, sun and pool rules, and (for Webelos) hypothermia. **Nobody goes in the water at a den night.**
  - **A "Done outside the den:" line.** Every in-water requirement is done outside the den, in one of three ways:
    - at a council camp or event with aquatics staff
    - through swim lessons
    - with family at a pool with certified lifeguards, only where the official page allows a family setting
  - Requirements done in the water are marked "(outside the den)".
- **Any Scouting swim, whether a council or anyone else runs it, follows Safe Swim Defense** (Guide to Safe Scouting, Aquatics Safety).
- **Six ranks, one theme.** The official names are Time to Swim (Lion), Tigers in the Water (Tiger), Paws for Water (Wolf), Salmon Run (Bear), Aquanaut (Webelos) and Swimming (AoL). All six match the app exactly, including the lowercase "to", "in the" and "for". The Michigan Crossroads requirement sheets (Oct 2024) agree for Lion, Tiger and Webelos. No rename is needed. None of these slips on the official pages affects a name:
  - The Lion intro says Lions follow "Safety Afloat rules", and the Tiger intro begins partway through a sentence at "Afloat rules". Swimming is governed by **Safe Swim Defense**. Safety Afloat covers boats. These plans follow Safe Swim Defense.
  - The official Lions Go Swimming activity pairs each Lion with their "Tiger Adult Partner". This is a copy slip from the Tiger page.
  - Some activity pages write "Time To Swim" or "Swimming AOL". The adventure pages' own titles match the app.
  - The Michigan Aquanaut sheet says "BSA swimmer test", and the current page says "Scouting America swimmer test". Only the wording differs.
- **Three ways to earn it (official pages):**
  - Complete the requirements, **or**
  - Take swim lessons. The Lion, Tiger, Webelos and AoL pages say "swim lessons". The Wolf and Bear pages say a swimming instruction program from an accredited instructor. **Or**
  - Pass the Scouting America swimmer test. This path is on the Wolf, Bear, Webelos and AoL pages. The Lion and Tiger pages don't list it.
  - **Proof (pack rule):** the Advancement Chair decides what proof counts, such as a lesson card, a certificate or a camp test card. The den leader checks with the Advancement Chair.
- **Family swims and the supervision sentence.** The Lion, Tiger and Bear pages say all swimming activity must be supervised by a mature adult 21 or over trained in Safe Swim Defense (the other three pages: check). Whether a family swim at a pool with certified lifeguards meets that is the Advancement Chair's call (check).
- **The eight points of Safe Swim Defense** (Guide to Safe Scouting, Aquatics Safety). This is what the den teaches on land, and what every Scouting swim follows:
  1. **Qualified supervision:** a mature adult aged 21 or over is in charge, trained in Safe Swim Defense within the last two years.
  2. **Personal health review:** every participant, youth and adult, has an Annual Health and Medical Record. On the day, families tell the supervisor about any recent illness or injury.
  3. **Safe area:** check the area before anyone swims.
     - Nonswimmer area: waist-to-chest deep.
     - Beginner area: from standing depth to just over the head.
     - Swimmer area: no deeper than 12 feet in clear water.
  4. **Response personnel:** lifeguards at a regulated facility fill this point. Without them, the unit needs its own rescuers: at least two (Guide), and one for every 10 swimmers (official adventure pages).
  5. **Lookout:** one person watches the whole area from the deck, stays dry, and calls the buddy checks.
  6. **Ability groups:** everyone is a swimmer, beginner or nonswimmer, based on a classification test renewed every year. **Anyone not tested this year is a nonswimmer, adults included** (official Tiger and Wolf activities).
  7. **Buddy system:** buddies are in the same ability group and check in and out together, using buddy tags on a buddy board. About every 10 minutes the lookout blows a whistle and calls "Buddies!". Pairs join and raise hands within a count of 10, and the lookout counts them.
  8. **Discipline:** go over the rules before any swim, and again at the water's edge.
- **Where scouts finish the swim (outside the den):**
  - **Cub Summer Splash at Camp Rainey Mountain** (July; the 2026 dates were July 17–19): the council page lists lake swimming for rising Tigers, Wolves, Bears, Webelos and AoLs. **Lions can't go.** Scouts may attend with a den, a pack or their family. For Pack 569 that means a family registers on its own (pack rule: no den or pack swims). The 2026 cost was $175 per scout and $50 per adult. The page doesn't say whether aquatics staff give swim tests or run adventure requirements (check). The 2027 dates are unknown (check).
  - **Day or resident camp swimming (check):** the council's 2026 Day, Twilight and STEM Camp page (rising 1st–5th graders) lists no swimming. I found no other council Cub camp with swimming besides Splash (check the 2027 pages).
  - **The council's Aquatics Adventure Weekends at Scoutland** are for Scouts BSA, Venturers and Sea Scouts, not Cub Scouts (check with the council).
  - **The pack's campouts are not swims:** Scoutland (October), Fort Yargo (March) and Rainey Mountain (April).
  - **Swim lessons:** any program the family picks. Wolf and Bear lessons must come from an accredited instructor (official).
  - **A pool with certified lifeguards, with family:** only where the rank's official page allows a family setting (see each rank).
- **The swim classification test:**
  - **Beginner test:** jump in feet first where the water is over your head. Level off and swim 25 feet, turn sharply, and swim back (Guide).
  - **Swimmer test:** jump in feet first where the water is over your head.
    - Swim 75 yards strongly, using sidestroke, breaststroke, trudgen or crawl.
    - Then swim 25 yards of easy resting backstroke.
    - The 100 yards are one swim with no stops and at least one sharp turn.
    - Finish by floating to rest (Guide).
  - **Who gives it (pack rule):** only pool staff or council camp aquatics staff. The pack has no tester. The official sources also allow a trained unit adult to give it, but Pack 569 doesn't use one. Whether a pool's own test is given as the Scouting America test is for the Advancement Chair to judge from the card (check).
  - **How it's given** (Aquatics Supervision, ch. 5): one-on-one, with the tester as the scout's buddy, and a pole, ring buoy or throw bag within reach.
  - **A test is good for one year.**
  - **Trying counts.** Bear, Webelos and AoL only have to *attempt* a test. The official Swimmer Test AOL page says it's fine if a scout doesn't pass.
- **Non-swimmers:**
  - A nonswimmer does every den-night requirement with everyone else.
  - Outside the den, they swim in the nonswimmer area. Swim lessons earn the whole adventure at every rank (official).
  - For a test attempt, the tester (pool or camp staff), the scout and the parent decide together whether the scout is ready to jump into deep water. No scout is ever pushed. Whether a partial try counts as an attempt is the Advancement Chair's call (check).
- **Life jackets:** the official rules only (pack rule). Safe Swim Defense doesn't call for life jackets in a nonswimmer area. The Guide lets a supervisor relax some deeper-water conditions when everyone wears a Coast Guard–approved life jacket. Read that wording before relying on it (check). A life jacket never moves a nonswimmer out of the nonswimmer area. The pack keeps no loaners.
- **Timing:** scouts move up a grade on June 1, so the rank a summer swim counts for is the Advancement Chair's call (check). Council camp swims are in summer. Lessons and indoor pools run all year.
- **Weather, sun and first aid** (facts the den teaches and families use):
  - **Lightning:** at the first thunder or lightning, everyone gets out of the water and into shelter. Wait at least 30 minutes after the last thunder or flash (Guide).
  - **Cold:**
    - About 80°F is ideal. At 70°F or below, keep swims short and watch for chilling (Guide).
    - A scout who is shivering, has blue lips or has clumsy hands gets out, dries off and wraps in a towel.
  - **Heat and sun:** sunscreen goes on at home and again after swimming outdoors (official pages). Bring hats and water, and take shade breaks.
    - Heat exhaustion looks like pale skin, heavy sweating and dizziness. Move the scout to shade and give water.
    - Heatstroke looks like hot, flushed skin and confusion. Call 911.
  - **Drowning is quiet.** A child in trouble often can't call out or wave.
  - **Other rules:** walk on the deck, and no running. No breath-holding contests (check the Guide).
  - **First aid:** a scout who took in water and keeps coughing, or who bumped their head, stops swimming and gets medical advice (check). For anything serious, call 911.
- **Youth Protection:** two registered adults 21 or over at every den night, and no one-on-one contact. For Lions and Tigers, an adult partner doesn't count as one of the two unless they are registered as a leader. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
- **Paperwork:** the den collects no swim forms. Camps collect their own, including the Annual Health and Medical Record (check what each camp asks for).
- **Gear:**
  - **The den makes:** a buddy board and tags for the den night.
  - **Rescue props for den nights:** a light pole (a pool noodle or broom handle), a beach towel, and a ring buoy or throw bag on 10 feet of rope (official supply lists). The pack keeps no loaners except tents, so the den borrows or buys these (see Open questions).
  - **Families supply, for the swim outside the den:** swimsuit, towel, water bottle, flip-flops or water shoes, sunscreen and dry clothes.
- **Food:** none. Water bottles only. The Webelos hypothermia step talks about warm drinks but doesn't serve any.
- **Devices:** scouts don't use them. The leader's watch or phone is the timer.
- **Counting twice:** Cub Summer Splash is one of the council camp choices in summertime-fun.md, so a scout who goes may also log it for Summertime Fun (see summertime-fun.md; check).
- **Pack option:** none. Pack 569 never runs a swim (pack rule).
- **Guest speaker:** a certified lifeguard or a pool's aquatics staff could teach the rules at a den night or a pack guest-speaker night (check). This fits Lion Req 1 (adapted from the official Lions Learn from the Lifeguard) and Bear Req 1, which says "with your den or an adult".
- **Leader reading:** the Guide's Aquatics Safety section, to teach the eight points.

### Meeting 1 of 1 · Swim Rules and Buddy Tags · 40 min
- All-dens option: the Lion and Tiger dens can share this night. Lion Req 1 matches Tiger Req 1, and both color a handbook picture. Each den's leaders run their own pairs.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before:
  - Get Lion handbooks, or print the official activity page. The activity cites handbook page 63 (check your edition).
  - Option B: book the lifeguard guest, and confirm the day before.
  - Make a buddy board: poster board with three columns (Nonswimmer, Beginner, Swimmer) and a hook or Velcro dot for each tag. Make blank index-card tags, one for each Lion and one for each partner.
  - Print a photo of a public pool and 6 picture cards: sunscreen, a hat, a water bottle, someone running on a wet deck, a lightning cloud, and a lifeguard chair.
  - Print the family note.
- Supplies: handbooks or printouts, crayons, markers, buddy board, blank tags, whistle, pool photo, picture cards, a bench or tape line for the "pool edge", family notes
- Tell parents before they leave: The den doesn't swim together. The family note explains how your Lion finishes: swim lessons, or a family swim at a lifeguarded pool if the Advancement Chair allows it.
1. **A Look at the Pool** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Here's a pool! Can you find the lifeguard chair? And the shallow end?"
   - How: 1) Show the **pool photo**. 2) Point out the **lifeguard**, the **shallow end** and the **deep end**. 3) Explain that Lions swim in the **shallow part** with their grown-ups.
   - Tip: **Everyone is a nonswimmer** unless they've been tested this year, grown-ups too (official).
2. **Eight Safe Swim Rules** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Swimmers have eight rules that keep everyone safe. Let's learn them with your grown-up."
   - How: **Option A · Lion Safe Swim Defense:**
     1) Pairs **color** the handbook picture.
     2) As they color, name each **rule** in Lion words: a trained grown-up in charge; tell us if you're sick; a safe, checked place; lifeguards ready; someone always watching; swim where your group swims; always with your buddy; listen and follow the rules.
     3) Each Lion **tells** their partner one rule.

     **Option B · Lifeguard guest:**
     1) The **lifeguard** teaches the eight rules, using the handbook picture.
     2) Pairs **color** the picture as they listen.
     3) Each Lion **asks** one question.
   - Tip: **Note Req 1** for each Lion who can tell their partner a rule.
3. **Buddy Tags** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "At the pool, you and your grown-up are buddies. Your tags go on the board together when you get in, and come off together when you get out."
   - How: 1) Each Lion **decorates** a tag with their first name, and partners make one too. 2) Pairs **hang** both tags side by side in the Nonswimmer column. 3) Practice a **buddy check**: one whistle, "Buddies!", and pairs raise joined hands before the leader counts to 10.
   - Tip: **At a pool,** the lookout calls a buddy check about every 10 minutes (official).
4. **In and Out, Nice and Slow** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 2 and 5 (set-up)
   - Say: "We never run or jump in. Sit on the edge, put your feet in, then slide in slowly to your grown-up."
   - How: 1) A **bench** or tape line is the pool edge. 2) Lions **sit, swing their feet over and slide down** to their partner. 3) Practice **getting out**: walk to the steps with your buddy, climb out, and take your tags off the board together.
   - Tip: **Walk on the deck.** Wet floors are slippery.
5. **Pool Day Thumbs** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Thumbs up if it keeps us safe at the pool, thumbs down if it doesn't."
   - How: 1) Hold up each **picture card**. 2) Lions give a **thumbs up or down**. 3) Ask, "**Why?**" Running makes you slip, thunder means everyone gets out, and sunscreen and water keep us from getting too hot.
   - Tip: **Thunder or lightning** means everyone gets out and waits 30 minutes after the last one (Guide).
6. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Lions, pick up three scraps with your grown-up."
   - How: 1) **Clean up**. 2) Put a **family note** in each partner's hand. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Tags go home** as a reminder. Pools and camps use their own buddy boards.
   - (at home) Blow bubbles at bath time, with your grown-up right there.

### Meeting 1 of 1 · Swim Rules, Buddies and Kicks · 40 min
- All-dens option: Tigers and Lions can share steps 2–4. The Lions do their own den night alongside.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before:
  - Get Tiger handbooks, or print the activity page. The activity cites handbook page 63 (check your edition).
  - Make the buddy board and blank tags, as in the Lion plan. Bring a yoga mat or towel for each pair.
  - Print a photo of a public pool and the family note, with this year's Splash dates filled in (check).
- Supplies: handbooks or printouts, crayons, markers, buddy board, tags, whistle, pool photo, mats or towels, a bench or tape line, family notes
- Tell parents before they leave: The den doesn't swim together. The family note lists the ways to finish: Cub Summer Splash, swim lessons, or a family swim at a lifeguarded pool if the Advancement Chair allows it.
1. **A Look at the Pool** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Here's a pool! Find the lifeguard, the shallow end and the deep end."

## Swimming (Arrow of Light)
- Elective · Meetings: 1 (one dry-land den night; the swim is done outside the den) · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/swimming/ · Other sources: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/swimming-dos-and-donts/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/order-of-rescue-aol/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/bsa-swimmer-test-aol/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/go-swimming-aol/, https://www.nega-bsa.org/cubsplash, https://www.nega-bsa.org/cs-twi-stem-cmp, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/gss/gss02/ · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: This adventure gets AoL scouts ready for Scouts BSA aquatics, where passing the swimmer test matters. At a dry-land den night, the patrol:
  - reviews Safe Swim Defense with a do's-and-don'ts game
  - learns the order of rescue and shows it from land
  - reads the swimmer test and finds where to take it

  Outside the den, scouts try the swimmer test and free swim for 30 minutes with at least three buddy checks. Swim lessons or passing the swimmer test also earn it (official). The AoL pages call the den a patrol.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Review Safe Swim Defense. (den night)
  2. Explain order of rescue; show reach and throw from land. (den night)
  3. Try the swimmer test. (outside the den)
  4. Free swim 30+ minutes with buddies, inside your ability group. (outside the den)
- Done at the pack opening: nothing.
- Done at a pack meeting: nothing. Pack 569 never runs a swim.
- Done outside the den: Reqs 3 and 4, in one of these ways. The den leader checks with the Advancement Chair.
  - **At a council camp or event with aquatics staff:** Cub Summer Splash in July, for rising AoLs (2027 dates: check). Check whether camp aquatics staff give the swimmer test, and whether the camp's free swim has the three buddy checks per half hour that Req 4 asks for. Day or resident camp swimming: the council's 2026 day camp page lists none (check).
  - **Swim lessons,** or **passing the swimmer test** given only by pool staff or council camp aquatics staff, earn Swimming outright (official; pack rule). A scout heading to Scouts BSA will need to pass the test for aquatics there, so lessons are worth it. Proof is whatever the Advancement Chair accepts.
  - **With family at a pool with certified lifeguards:** the Swimming page doesn't name a family setting, and Req 4's buddy checks come from the qualified supervision. This path isn't offered unless the Advancement Chair allows it (check). A swimmer-test try given by pool staff fits Req 3 wherever it happens (check).
- Where Req 2 happens: the page says "from land", so the den night completes it, in the den room with a taped shoreline, or in a gym or on grass.
- Non-swimmer:
  - A nonswimmer does every den-night step.
  - Outside the den, a nonswimmer tries the swimmer test only if the tester (pool or camp staff) and the parent agree (whether a partial try counts: check with the Advancement Chair).
  - Swim lessons earn Swimming (official).

  - Rescuers lie down and anchor themselves, and never throw at anyone's head (official).

### Meeting 1 of 1 · Do's, Don'ts and Rescues · 40 min
- Where: the den room, with a taped shoreline. Step 2 can move to a gym or a grassy area beside the meeting place (with permission; check the light).
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before:
  - Print the official Do's and Don'ts game card, rule strips and key. Tape the card to the wall, and cut the strips.
  - Bring a light pole or pool noodle, a beach towel, and a ring buoy or throw bag on 10 feet of rope (official supply list).
  - Print the swimmer-test steps and the family note, with this year's Splash dates filled in (check).
- Supplies: game card, rule strips, key, tape, AoL handbooks, pole or noodle, towel, ring buoy or throw bag, painter's tape, swimmer-test sheets, family notes
- Tell parents before they leave: The patrol doesn't swim together. The family note lists the ways to finish: Cub Summer Splash, swim lessons, or the swimmer test from pool or camp staff.
1. **Swimming Do's and Don'ts** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Draw a rule strip. Is it a do or a don't, and which of the eight points is it about?"
   - How: 1) Scouts **draw** strips and place each under **Do** or **Don't** (official). 2) Check them with the **key**. 3) Tie each one to a **Safe Swim Defense point**.
   - Tip: **Note Req 1** for each scout who can review the eight points.
2. **Order of Rescue** · den · 15 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Shout for help first. Then reach, then throw. Cub Scouts only reach and throw. They never go in."
   - How: 1) Explain the **order** (official). 2) Tape a **shoreline**. 3) The rescuer **lies down**, anchors, and **reaches** with the pole or towel. 4) The rescuer **throws** the ring past the "swimmer" and **pulls in**. 5) Switch until everyone has done **both**.
   - Tip: **Note Req 2** for each scout who explains the order and shows both.
3. **Ready for the Swimmer Test** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 3 and 4 (set-up)
   - Say: "Here's the swimmer test. Where could you take it this year?"
   - How: 1) Read the **test steps** together. 2) The denner **lists** the places: Cub Summer Splash, a pool whose staff give the test, or swim lessons. 3) Explain the **buddy check**: whistle, and the Cub Scout sign above your head next to your buddy.
   - Tip: **Only pool staff or council camp aquatics staff** give the test. The pack has no tester.
4. **Clean-Up & Handoff** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Denner, call the clean-up. Coil the rope."
   - How: 1) **Clean up**. 2) Put a **family note** in each adult's hand. 3) **Hand off** each scout to their adult by name.
   - Tip: **Coil the rope** so it throws clean next time.
   - (at home) Practice floating and resting backstroke if you can get to a pool with your family.
