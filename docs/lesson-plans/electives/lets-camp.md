# Let's Camp!: elective, all ranks (DRAFT 2, 40-minute den time)

Researched 2026-09-30 by the cubmaster-program agent, using the format of Race Time (draft 2) and Champions for Nature (draft 1). This is the third elective in the pass. Every plan here is written in our own words. Requirements are only summarized, so use the official page for the full text. Anything marked "(check)" still needs confirming. Nothing is in the app yet.

How these plans fit a Pack 569 den night:
- The pack does the opening ceremony together: flag, Pledge, Oath and Law. Each den then splits off for about 40 minutes.
- Den meetings here have no gathering or opening steps. Each one is den steps plus one short den closing. The minutes add up to 35–40 and never go over 40.
- No Let's Camp! requirement is done at the pack opening or at a pack meeting. The overnight is done at a pack campout, so each rank has a "Done at a pack campout:" line, and the den leader confirms who stayed the night.
- "Reqs: N" means the step does real work on that requirement. "Reqs: N (set-up)" means the step only sets up or reinforces it and does not complete it.
- The guides don't tick requirements. Leaders record progress on the app's Advancement board afterward.
- **Five ranks, not six.** Lion, Tiger, Wolf, Bear and Webelos each have a Let's Camp! elective. The official names carry the rank ("Let's Camp Lion"), and the app drops the suffix. **Arrow of Light has no Let's Camp!** The AoL adventure list on scouting.org doesn't include it, the app's AoL electives don't either, and the AoL URL returned only a maintenance page on 2026-09-30. AoL camping sits inside the required Outdoor Adventurer adventure, which covers a den or troop campout, campsite layout and food safety. That adventure isn't planned here.
- **Six Essentials, not Scout Basic Essentials.** All five Let's Camp! pages use the **Cub Scout Six Essentials**: a filled water bottle, a first-aid kit, a whistle, a flashlight, sun protection and trail food. The eleven-item **Scout Basic Essentials** list, which adds a pocketknife and matches, belongs to AoL Outdoor Adventurer Req 1 (official Scout Basic Essentials activity). It isn't taught here.
- **Which campouts count.** Lion to Bear: every official page accepts a pack campout or a council or district Cub Scout overnight camp. Webelos: a campout with the den, the pack or the family. Pack 569 has three campouts on the app's Camping tab:
  - **Fort Yargo State Park, Winder.** The pack's own campout, in late March. 2026 was Mar 27–29, and the 2027 date needs checking. The pack runs it, so the pack provides the BALOO-trained adult. Keith: it's council-approved, because the pack camped there last year.
  - **Scoutland, on Lake Lanier.** The Apalachee District fall family weekend, in early October (2026: Oct 2–4). The district runs it.
  - **Camp Rainey Mountain, Clayton.** The council's spring family weekend, in late April. The 2027 dates aren't posted yet.
  - **This year:** Scoutland is two days away, so the dens aim at Fort Yargo, or at Rainey Mountain as a second chance.
- **Timing.** Hold each rank's den meetings in the 4 weeks before the campout. The Lion and Tiger weather talk (Req 2) has to come "before" the campout, so hold that meeting 1–2 weeks out, once there's a forecast. The Webelos have a short follow-up after the campout.
- **Who camps, and who sleeps where** (the Guide to Safe Scouting camping section and the Age Appropriate Guidelines, 08/24):
  - Lions, Tigers, Wolves and Bears camp only as a pack or at council and district events, never as a den. The official Tiger and Wolf pack-camping activities say so in plain words.
  - A Webelos den may also camp as a den, at a council-designated site with a BALOO-trained adult. The app has a den campout template for this.
  - A pack overnight needs a council-designated or council-appraised site and at least one BALOO-trained adult on it. Every official Let's Camp! page also asks for Hazardous Weather training and the Weather-Related Safety Moment.
  - Lions and Tigers need their adult partner there overnight. The official Pack Camping Tiger activity goes further and says a parent or legal guardian (check; see Open questions).
  - A Cub Scout tents with their own parent or guardian and siblings. No youth shares a tent with an adult who isn't their parent or guardian.
  - If a parent can't come, a Wolf or older may come under another registered adult who is the parent of a Cub Scout on the trip. This must be agreed beforehand, and they don't share a tent. The app's pack rule allows one outside youth per adult, and never a Lion or Tiger. A Webelos without a parent at a den campout needs two registered leaders supervising. A scout without a parent tents alone or with a same-gender scout within two years of age, never with an adult.
  - Everyone brings an Annual Health and Medical Record, Parts A and B.
- **Fire** (Age Appropriate Guidelines, 08/24):
  - Fire building and outdoor cooking are Webelos and AoL only. Lions, Tigers, Wolves and Bears watch from outside the fire ring, and adults build, light, tend and put out the fire.
  - Fueled devices (stoves and lanterns) are marked for Scouts BSA and up. Adults run every stove and fuel lantern at the campout.
  - **The Firem'n Chit is a Scouts BSA award only.** The official page says Scouts BSA members earn it, so no Cub Scout holds one or needs it. Adults carry the matches, and adults light every fire for Lions to Bears.
  - Webelos (and AoL) fire lighting is the den leader's choice (Keith): Option A, an adult lights it; Option B, the scout lights it with an adult at their elbow. Webelos Req 7 lets a Webelos light the fire lay under adult supervision "if circumstances permit."
  - S'mores follow the official Behavior Around Campfires safety moment: stay outside the ring, never pull anything out of the fire, use a skewer long enough to keep your body outside the ring, and never flick hot food.
- **Knives.** Nothing in this adventure needs a knife.
  - Keith's rule: a scout who has earned their rank's knife adventure this year (Bear Whittling, AoL Knife Safety) may use a knife under supervision at home, campouts and outings, but never at den meetings, where adults do the cutting. Here that means a Bear who has earned Whittling may use a pocketknife at the campout under supervision.
  - Webelos have no pocketknife adventure. Chef's Knife covers kitchen knives. So Webelos don't carry pocketknives, and tinder for the fire lay is bought or gathered by hand, not carved.
- **Food.** No food at den meetings. Trail food in the Six Essentials games stays sealed, or is a picture. Keith's cooking rule: meals are cooked at home, at a pack campout, or at a Webelos/AoL den or patrol campout, with adults running the stoves.
  - Families cook. Lions to Bears help with jobs that don't involve heat.
  - Webelos may cook outdoors with an adult beside them, and the Webelos plan one den meal as part of Req 1.
- **Allergies and EpiPens.** The official Webelos page says to review every scout's health record for food allergies and restrictions. It also says that if any youth or adult carries an EpiPen, at least one other adult must know how to give it. The official Cub Scout camping page says to plan for food allergies and EpiPens at every pack campout. Pack 569 applies both rules to every rank. Make the s'mores and trail food allergy-safe (no nuts unless the den is clear).
- **Weather, water, ticks and heat:**
  - **Forecast:** the official camping page says to check it 10, 5, 3 and 1 days before.
  - **Thunder:** everyone gets into hard-topped cars or a solid building (the app's campout pages).
  - **Water:** Fort Yargo sits on Lake Marbury, and Rainey Mountain has Lake Toccoa. No swimming or boating unless it's planned separately under Safe Swim Defense or Safety Afloat. Scouts stay back from the shore, with a buddy and an adult.
  - **Ticks:** they are active in Georgia by late March (the app's Fort Yargo page). Adults put on repellent, and everyone gets a tick check each night and after the trip.
  - **Heat:** early-October afternoons at Scoutland can still feel like summer, so plan water and shade.
  - **Cold:** March and April nights can drop into the 30s. Bring sleeping pads and cold-rated bags.
- **Tents at den meetings.** Tiger (Option A), Bear and Webelos pitch tents at a den meeting. The pack has loaner tents (Keith, 2026-09-30). Borrow from the pack's gear and return them the same night. No stakes indoors. Keep the shock-corded poles pointed away from faces.
- Two registered adults 21 or over in every den room, and no one-on-one contact. For Lions and Tigers, an adult partner doesn't count as one of the two unless they are registered as a leader. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.

## Let's Camp! (Lion)
- Elective · Meetings: 3 (two den meetings and the pack campout) · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/lets-camp-lion/ · Other sources: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/buddy-or-no-buddy/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/adult-partner-dress-up/, https://www.scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/leader-resources/pack-meeting-resources/camping/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/gss/gss03/, https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/HealthSafety/pdf/680-685.pdf, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/safety-moments/camp-fire-behavior/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/safety-moments/weather-related-safety/, https://michiganscouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Lion-Adventure-Requirements.pdf · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: Lions and their adult partners get ready for their first night in a tent. Meeting 1 covers the buddy system and the Six Essentials. Meeting 2 comes 1–2 weeks before the campout: the den dresses for the forecast, checks the Six Essentials bags packed at home, and previews a night at camp. The Lion then camps overnight with their partner at the pack campout. The official Req 3 activity is done at home, with the partner helping the Lion pack.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Learn how the buddy system works outdoors. (meeting, with the partner there)
  2. Before the campout, talk about expected weather and what to wear. (meeting, with the partner there)
  3. Pack your Cub Scout Six Essentials for the campout. (at home with the partner; checked at Meeting 2)
  4. Camp overnight with the pack, or at a council or district camp. (pack campout)
- Done at the pack opening: nothing.
- Done at a pack meeting: nothing. If the pack holds a gear check at the pack meeting before the campout (the official camping page suggests one), it reinforces Req 3 (set-up).
- Done at a pack campout: Req 4. The Lion camps overnight with their adult partner at Fort Yargo, or at a council family weekend, which the official requirement also accepts. The den leader confirms each Lion who stayed the night.
- Leader's choice for Req 2: Option A · Adult Partner Dress Up: partners arrive dressed for a season or a kind of weather, and Lions guess which (official activity). / Option B · Clothes Pile Pick: the leader brings a pile of clothes, and pairs pick an outfit for the weather card they draw (also an official activity). It's the same step with the same minutes.
- Safety notes:
  - An adult partner stays at every Lion's elbow. Partners don't count as one of the two registered adults unless they are registered. No drop-offs. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
  - Whistles: one soft toot at the meeting to try it. Teach that three blasts means "I need help."
  - Trail food stays sealed at the meeting, with no eating. Check for nut allergies before choosing what goes in the sample bag.
  - First-aid kit show-and-tell: only bandages, wipes and gauze. No medicines or sharp items.
  - At the campout, Lions never build, light or tend a fire. They sit outside the ring, and an adult stands between the Lions and the fire.
  - A Lion tents only with their own parent or guardian and siblings.
  - The campout has its own list under Meeting 3.
- Family campout note (send home at Meeting 1):
  - The campout is [Fort Yargo, date] (check). The full packing list, the weather and the rules are on the Camping tab.
  - Your Lion camps with you in your family's tent. Only a Lion's own parent or guardian and siblings share their tent (see Open questions if the adult partner isn't a parent).
  - Fill in the health form (Parts A and B) for everyone who's coming. Tell the den leader about any food allergy or EpiPen.
  - Help your Lion pack their own Six Essentials in a small backpack before Meeting 2 (Req 3). Pack a water bottle, a small first-aid kit, a whistle, a flashlight, a hat and sunscreen, and a sealed bag of trail food.
  - Put the tent up in the yard once before the trip.

### Meeting 1 of 3 · Buddies and the Six Essentials · 40 min
- All-dens option: the Lion and Tiger dens can share this night. Both ranks' Req 1 and Req 3 are worded the same. Each den's leaders run their own pairs.
- Prep: 3–4 weeks before the campout:
  - Print a tic-tac-toe grid for every two pairs, or tape grids on the floor.
  - Pack a sample Six Essentials daypack: a water bottle, a small first-aid kit (bandages and wipes only), a whistle, a flashlight, a hat and sunscreen, and a sealed bag of nut-free trail food.
  - Make six picture cards, one for each essential.
  - Print the family campout note.
- Supplies: sample daypack, 6 picture cards, tic-tac-toe grids, markers, a whistle for the leader, family campout notes
- Tell parents before they leave: Please pack your Lion's Six Essentials at home together and bring the bag to Meeting 2 on [date]. The campout is [date/place] (check), and the family note has everything else.
1. **Buddy Up** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Outdoors, nobody goes anywhere alone. You always have a buddy. Buddies stay together, help each other, and tell a grown-up if something's wrong."
   - How: 1) Pair up the **Lions as buddies**. Partners stay close by. 2) Practice the **buddy check**: when the leader calls "Buddy check!", buddies grab hands and raise them. 3) Call it **three times** at random.
   - Tip: **Odd number?** A group of three works fine.
2. **Buddy Tic-Tac-Toe** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Buddies are a team. You and your buddy play together against another buddy team."
   - How: 1) Each **buddy team** shares one marker. 2) Teams take turns on the grid, and the **buddies agree** on each move. 3) The leader calls **"Buddy check!"** in the middle of the game. 4) Swap opponents and play again.
   - Tip: **Note Req 1** for each Lion who can tell their partner what a buddy does.
3. **Buddy Walk** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Let's walk like we're on a trail. Hold your buddy's hand and freeze when you hear the whistle!"
   - How: 1) Buddies **walk the room** together. 2) One whistle toot means **freeze**. 3) Ask, "**Where's your buddy?**" 4) Do it twice.
   - Tip: **At camp:** a parent takes their own Lion or Tiger; otherwise buddies go together and an adult waits outside.
4. **Six Essentials Show-and-Tell** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "Scouts carry six things every time we go outdoors. Let's find out why!"
   - How: 1) Pull out **one item at a time**. 2) Lions **act out** what it's for: sip the water, point the flashlight, put on the hat, stick on a bandage, and toot the whistle softly. The trail food stays sealed. 3) Put each **picture card** down in a row. 4) Pairs name **all six** together.
   - Tip: **Three toots means help:** Say it with them.
5. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Lions, pick up three things with your grown-up."
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 2) Put a **family campout note** in each partner's hand. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Whistles home:** Only if the pack is giving them out (check).
   - (at home) Req 3: pack the Six Essentials together.

### Meeting 2 of 3 · Dressed for Camp · 40 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before the campout:
  - Check the forecast for the campout weekend and print it as simple pictures: sun, rain, and the night-time low. Fort Yargo in late March usually means cool mornings, mild afternoons and nights that can drop into the 30s (the app's Fort Yargo page).
  - Option A: ask each partner beforehand to come dressed for an assigned season or weather.
  - Option B: bring a pile of clothes: a rain jacket, a winter hat, gloves, a sun hat, shorts, a fleece, wool socks and a swimsuit (as the wrong answer).
  - Make pictures of a tent, a sleeping bag, a campfire circle and a bathroom.
- Supplies: forecast pictures, clothes pile (Option B), weather cards, pictures of camp, a spare Six Essentials checklist
- Tell parents before they leave: Pack for the forecast, and add one extra layer for the night. A warm hat to sleep in helps. The campout is [date/place] (check), and tick checks come afterward.
1. **What Are They Wearing?** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Clothes keep us comfy outdoors. Look at what your grown-up is wearing. What weather is it for?"
   - How: **Option A · Adult Partner Dress Up:** 1) Partners step out **one at a time**. 2) Lions **guess** the season or weather. 3) Ask, "**What would you add?**" / **Option B · Clothes Pile Pick:** 1) A pair draws a **weather card**. 2) The Lion **picks an outfit** from the pile, with the partner's help. 3) The den gives a **thumbs up or down**.
   - Tip: **Layers win:** Several thin layers are easier to add and take off than one thick coat.
2. **Camp Weather Report** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Here's the weather for OUR campout. What should go in your bag?"
   - How: 1) Show the **forecast pictures**. 2) Pairs **talk it over**: rain jacket? warm hat to sleep in? extra socks? 3) Each Lion **names two things** to pack.
   - Tip: **Note Req 2** for each Lion who talked the weather and clothes over with their partner.
3. **Bag Check** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Show us your Six Essentials! Hold each one up when I call it."
   - How: 1) Call out **each essential**. 2) Lions **hold it up**. 3) Partners note **anything missing** on the checklist.
   - Tip: **Note Req 3** for each Lion whose bag is packed. **No bag?** They finish packing at home before the campout.
4. **A Night at Camp** · den · 7 min · Reqs: 4 (set-up)
   - Say: "Let's pretend it's night at camp. Where do you sleep? Where's the bathroom? What are the campfire rules?"
   - How: 1) Show the **tent** and **sleeping bag** pictures: you sleep with your family. 2) Show the **bathroom**: a parent takes their own Lion or Tiger; otherwise buddies go together and an adult waits outside. 3) Show the **campfire circle**: stay outside the ring, walk, and never throw anything into the fire. 4) **Flashlight** under the pillow, whistle in reach.
   - Tip: **Nervous Lions:** Bring a favorite stuffed animal from home.
5. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Clothes back in the pile, bags on your backs!"
   - How: 1) **Clean up**. 2) Remind families of the **campout date**. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Missing items:** Point families to the Camping tab's packing list.
   - (at home) Put the tent up in the yard once.

### Meeting 3 of 3 · The Pack Campout (outing, overnight)
- All-dens option: the whole pack camps together. Each den keeps its own site area and its own den moments, and the rest of the weekend is the pack's program.
- Prep: 3–4 weeks before:
  - The pack's campout lead handles the booking, the BALOO-trained adult, Hazardous Weather training, the SAFE Checklist, and collecting and reviewing health forms, including allergies and EpiPens.
  - The den leader keeps a roster of Lions and partners, a buddy list, and the forecast checked 10, 5, 3 and 1 days out.
  - Make sure a second adult knows how to give any EpiPen in the den.
- Supplies: den roster, buddy list, spare flashlights, spare whistles, sunscreen, bug repellent, tick tool, a water jug, first-aid kit
- Tell parents before they leave: Tick-check everyone tonight and tomorrow. Dry the tent at home. Thank you for camping with us!
1. **Arrival Buddy Check** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Welcome to camp! Find your buddy. Here's our den's area."
   - How: 1) Do a **buddy check**. 2) Walk the **den area boundary**. 3) Show the **bathroom route**: a parent takes their own Lion or Tiger; otherwise buddies go together and an adult waits outside. 4) Point out where the **fire ring** is and say it's off-limits.
   - Tip: **Water and shore:** No one goes near the lake without an adult.
2. **Tent Helpers** · den · 30 min · Reqs: 4 (set-up)
   - Say: "Lions, you're the tent helpers. Hand your grown-up the stakes and unroll your sleeping bag."
   - How: 1) Each family **pitches its own tent**. 2) The Lion **hands pieces** and **unrolls** the bedding. 3) The den leader **walks the sites** and helps where needed.
   - Tip: **Daylight:** Get tents up before dark.
3. **Six Essentials On** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Daypacks on! Water, whistle, flashlight, hat, first aid and trail food."
   - How: 1) Lions **wear their daypacks** for the day's program. 2) Partners do a **quick check**.
   - Tip: **Refill water** at every stop.
4. **Campfire Rules Live** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 4 (set-up)
   - Say: "Before the campfire: we sit outside the ring, we walk, and anything that falls in stays in."
   - How: 1) Walk to the **ring** before it's lit. 2) Show the **line** where Lions stop. 3) S'mores: **grown-ups** hold the long skewers with the Lion (Behavior Around Campfires).
   - Tip: **Allergy-safe s'mores** for anyone who needs them.
5. **Lights-Out Check** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Flashlight by your pillow, whistle in reach, your grown-up right beside you. Good night, Lions!"
   - How: 1) Walk past each **family tent**. 2) Remind everyone of the **three-toot** help signal. 3) Remind everyone of **quiet hours**.
   - Tip: **Headcount** by tent before the leaders turn in.
6. **Morning Headcount & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "You did it. You camped overnight!"
   - How: 1) **Count** every Lion and partner. 2) The site gets a **litter sweep**. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Note Req 4** for each Lion who stayed the night with their partner.

### Meeting 1 of 3 · Buddies and the Six Essentials · 40 min
- All-dens option: Tigers and Lions can share this night. Their Req 1 and Req 3 are worded the same.
- Prep: 3–4 weeks before the campout:
  - Make 8 picture cards of kids outdoors. Some show two kids together, and some show one kid wandering off alone.
  - For the official scavenger hunt, hide two or three sets of the Six Essentials around the room: water bottles, small first-aid kits, whistles, flashlights, hats or sunscreen, and sealed trail-food bags.
  - Print a Six Essentials checklist for each pair and the family campout note.
- Supplies: hidden essentials sets, a bag per pair, picture cards, checklists, crayons, family campout notes
- Tell parents before they leave: Pack your Tiger's Six Essentials at home together and bring the bag to Meeting 2 on [date]. The campout is [date/place] (check).
1. **Back-to-Back Buddies** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Buddies help each other. Sit back to back, link arms, and try to stand up together!"
   - How: 1) Pair the **Tigers as buddies**. Partners spot. 2) Buddies **stand up** together, which is the official activity. 3) Ask, "**Could you do that alone?**" 4) Teach the **buddy check**: grab hands and raise them.
   - Tip: **Soft floor:** Use a carpet or mats.
2. **Buddy or Alone?** · den · 7 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Look at each picture. Are they using the buddy system?"
