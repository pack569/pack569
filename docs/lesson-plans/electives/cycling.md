# Cycling electives, all ranks (DRAFT 2, 40-minute den time)

> **A guide, not the rulebook.** These are ideas to help a den leader run the meeting. They are not a complete or official source. The official requirements on scouting.org and the Guide to Safe Scouting always come first. If something here is unclear or unsettled, use your judgment and check with the Cubmaster.

Researched 2026-09-30 by the cubmaster-program agent, using the format of Let's Camp! (draft 2) and Summertime Fun (draft 2). This is the next elective in the pass, and the first theme: one adventure that every rank has under its own name. Every plan here is written in our own words. Requirements are only summarized, so use the official page for the full text. Anything marked "(check)" still needs confirming. Nothing is in the app yet.

How these plans fit a Pack 569 den night:
- The pack does the opening ceremony together: flag, Pledge, Oath and Law. Each den then splits off for about 40 minutes.
- Den meetings here have no gathering or opening steps. Each one is den steps plus one short den closing. The minutes add up to 35–40 and never go over 40.
- "Reqs: N" means the step does real work on that requirement. "Reqs: N (set-up)" means the step only sets up or reinforces it and does not complete it.
- The guides don't tick requirements. Leaders record progress on the app's Advancement board afterward.
- **Three kinds of meeting:**
  - **Indoor den nights** (40 min) in the usual den room.
  - **Bike nights** (40 min, outing-style). Scouts bring bikes and helmets from home, and the den works outside. Each bike night has a "Where:" line. The place is the den leader's choice, **coordinated with the Cubmaster** (pack rule).
  - **Den rides** (outings, longer than 40 minutes, usually Saturday mornings) for the ride requirement. Every ride and every off-site bike night follows the council outing process (check).
- **Six ranks, one theme.** The official names are On a Roll (Lion), Rolling Tigers (Tiger), **Pedal With the Pack** (Wolf), Bears on Bikes (Bear), Pedal Away (Webelos) and Cycling (AoL). The Wolf name has a capital W on the official page, on the official Cycling pack-meeting page and on the Michigan Crossroads council sheet. The app writes "Pedal with the Pack" (see Open questions). The other five match the app exactly.
- **Bikes, not scooters or skates.** On a Roll is about bicycles. Every official page says a push or balance bike, training wheels, a tricycle or an adaptive bike is fine for a scout who can't ride a two-wheeler yet. No page mentions scooters or skates (check). Electric and hybrid bikes aren't allowed in Scouting activities (every official page), for adults as well as scouts.
- **Where rides happen (pack rule: closed or safe routes only):**
  - No official page at any rank requires riding in traffic. The AoL page's intro mentions "the open road or trail," and Pack 569 chooses the trail.
  - The official ride activities for Lion, Tiger and Bear call for a bike path away from traffic. The Wolf ride activity says to use parks or school playgrounds and to avoid parking lots. The Tiger bike rodeo allows a parking lot only with permission and cones blocking cars.
  - So all **rides** go on paved park paths. **Bike nights** may use a coned-off lot (with permission) or a school blacktop.
  - Greenway road crossings: everyone walks their bikes across at the crosswalk, with an adult standing in it (check each route).
  - The den leader picks the paths from local parks and greenways they know (check the distance, crossings and group rules), coordinated with the Cubmaster. Fort Yargo's paved trails at the pack campout are one option; the app's Fort Yargo packing list already says to bring bikes and helmets.
- **Helmets for every rider, adults too.** The Wolf ride and Webelos ride activities say adults (and den chiefs) wear helmets. The official fit:
  - The helmet sits level and low on the forehead.
  - The side straps form a V under each ear.
  - Only one or two fingers fit under the chin strap.
  - Families bring their own helmets. The pack keeps no loaners (pack rule).
  - No helmet, no ride. That scout does the family ride later.
- **Before every ride, do the ABC Quick Check:** Air in the tires; Brakes; Cranks, Chain and Cogs (every official page, using the PBIC checklist). Riders wear closed-toe shoes (official ride activities), with laces tucked and no wide pant legs near the chain.
- **Paperwork for off-site rides:** the official ride activities ask for an Annual Health and Medical Record (Parts A and B) for every participant, youth and adult. The Tiger and AoL pages ask for an Activity Consent Form for any ride away from the regular meeting place. Pack 569 uses both for every off-site ride and bike night.
- **Supervision on a ride:**
  - The official pack cycling page puts a lead adult in front and a sweep adult at the back, with a way to reach each other (phone or radio, carried by adults only). It also pairs buddies of similar skill.
  - Two registered adults 21 or over on every ride and in every den room, and no one-on-one contact. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
  - Lions and Tigers ride with their adult partner beside them. For Lions and Tigers, an adult partner doesn't count as one of the two unless they are registered as a leader.
  - The official SAFE checklist for cycling suggests a chase vehicle for longer rides. Pack 569 plans one for the Webelos and AoL rides (check). The chase driver never carries a lone scout who isn't their own child. Call the scout's parent, add a second adult, or carry a buddy too.
- **Bikes come from home.** I found no pack loaner bikes (check). Each rank has a "No bike, or missed the ride" path:
  - Every ride requirement also accepts a ride with family (Lion and Tiger: the adult partner or family; Wolf to AoL: den, pack or family). The family tells the den leader the date.
  - A den family may lend a bike, if the owner agrees (check the pack's view).
  - A balance bike, trike or training wheels counts for Lions, Tigers and Wolves.
- **Hand tools.** The Age Appropriate Guidelines (08/24) mark hand tools for Wolf/Bear and older, not for Lions or Tigers (the reader gave me two different column readings, so check).
  - The pack's rule is that tools stay with adults where needed, so here:
    - Lions and Tigers never hold a tool. Adults adjust.
    - Wolves and Bears may turn an Allen key or a quick-release with an adult's hand on the bike.
    - Webelos and AoL use tire levers and lube with an adult watching.
  - An adult checks every bolt and quick-release before anyone rides.
- **Timing, weather and first aid:**
  - **When:** spring (April–May) is best. Evenings stay light past 8 p.m. for bike nights, and the heat hasn't arrived. Early September also works. From mid-October, sunset comes near or during den time (check the den night's start time). The Guide to Safe Scouting says to avoid riding at night.
  - **Heat:** hold rides in the morning, with water every 20 minutes and shade stops. Heat exhaustion looks like pale skin, heavy sweating and dizziness: move the scout to the shade and give water. Heatstroke (hot, flushed skin and confusion) means calling 911.
  - **Sun:** families put sunscreen on at home.
  - **Thunder:** everyone gets off the bikes and into cars or a building.
  - **Scrapes:** carry a first-aid kit with non-latex gloves, water for rinsing, bandages, gauze and a cold pack. An adult cleans and bandages the scrape and tells the parent at pickup.
  - **Head bumps:** any hit to the head means the scout stops riding and the parent is called. A helmet that took a hit is replaced (check).
  - **Serious injury:** call 911.
- **Food:** none is needed. Trail food in the Webelos and AoL essentials stays sealed and gets an allergy check. No den picnics.
- **Devices:** the official Webelos and AoL map activities give each pair a smart device. Pack 569 uses printed maps instead, and the leader's own device checks the distance.
- **Counting twice (pack rule):** a den ride between May 1 and August 31 is an outing. It also counts as one of the three Summertime Fun activities for the rank the scout is moving into (see summertime-fun.md). A bike rodeo and ride is one of the official Summertime Fun pack ideas.
- **Pack option:** the official Cycling pack-meeting page runs four stations (safety, obstacle course, gear and maintenance, races), ends with a pack ride, and maps each station to rank requirements. Pack 569 hasn't scheduled one (see Open questions). Each rank's "Done at a pack meeting:" line says what it would cover.
- **Guest speaker:** the official Tiger options for Reqs 1–4 include a cycling expert, and Bear Req 1 has a biking-expert option. One of the pack's guest-speaker nights with a bike-shop mechanic could serve several dens (check).
- **Sources.** On 2026-09-30 I read, using the r.jina.ai reader:
  - the six official adventure pages and the official Cycling pack-meeting page
  - the activity pages listed under each rank. The reader doesn't show activity links on the adventure pages, so I found each by search and confirmed its rank and requirement on the page itself. Three official activities have no page I could find (Wolf Req 4, and Bear Reqs 1 and 5), and they're marked (check).
  - Guide to Safe Scouting, Sports and Activities (gss08): Bicycle Safety
  - Age Appropriate Guidelines (680-685, 08/24): "Bike—Day Trip" is checked for every rank from Lion up
  - the SAFE checklist for planning for cycling (2025)
  - **Cross-checks:**
    - The Michigan Crossroads Wolf requirement sheet (Oct 2024) matches Pedal With the Pack word for word, capital W included.
    - Boyscouttrail's Pedal Away list matches, except that its Req 1 adds a sentence about how to carry the gear. The official page didn't show that sentence on 2026-09-30 (check). The plan covers carrying anyway.
    - The Michigan Lion sheet didn't load.
  - **The app:** each name sits in the right rank's electives in `ADVENTURES`, and `ADV_ELECTIVE_THEMES` `th:cycling` maps all six. The only difference is the lowercase "with".

## On a Roll (Lion)
- Elective · Meetings: 2 (one den meeting and one den ride) · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/on-a-roll/ · Other sources: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/checking-my-gear-before-i-ride/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/follow-the-dots-to-safety-gear/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/cut-and-paste-bike-rules/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/lets-roll-lions/, https://www.scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/leader-resources/pack-meeting-resources/cycling/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/gss/gss08/, https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/HealthSafety/pdf/680-685.pdf, https://www.scouting.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/SAFE-checklist-for-planning-for-Cycling.docx, https://www.pedbikeinfo.org/bicyclesaferjourney/abcquickcheck.pdf · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: Lions and their adult partners learn what to wear and the rules for riding, then ride together on a path away from traffic. It's about bicycles, not scooters or skates. The official page welcomes balance bikes, training wheels, tricycles and adaptive bikes, so a Lion who can't ride a two-wheeler yet still earns it. Every requirement names the adult partner or family, and under Pack 569's rule any adult partner counts. Partners come to both meetings.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Learn the safety gear to wear when riding a bike. (meeting, with the partner there)
  2. Learn the safety rules for riding a bike. (meeting, with the partner there)
  3. Ride a bike wearing safety gear and following the rules. (den ride with the partner there, or with family)
- Done at the pack opening: nothing.
- Done at a pack meeting: nothing on the standard calendar. If the pack holds the official Cycling pack meeting, its Safety Station covers Reqs 1 and 2. Its pack ride covers Req 3, with the partner riding along.
- Leader's choice for Req 1: Option A · Checking My Gear Before I Ride: pairs fit their own helmets (official). / Option B · Follow the Dots to Safety Gear: the Lion handbook page, with the leader's helmet as the model (official). It's the same step with the same minutes.
- Leader's choice for the ride path: Option A · a short paved loop in a local park, ridden in laps, picked by the den leader (check). / Option B · a family ride at the pack campout on Fort Yargo's paved trails, with the den riding together (check the trail).
- No bike, or missed the ride:
  - A family ride counts, because the official Req 3 accepts the partner or family. Ride on a path away from traffic with helmets on, and tell the den leader the date.
  - A balance bike, trike or training wheels counts.
  - A den family may lend a bike or trike, if the owner agrees (check).
  - The pack ride, if the pack holds one.
- Safety notes:
  - The partner stays beside their Lion at both meetings, walking or riding. No drop-offs.
  - Helmets for every rider, partners too. Closed-toe shoes with laces tied.
  - Lions never hold tools. Adults do the pumping and adjusting.
  - The ride is on a path away from traffic (official Let's Roll Lions). There are no road crossings on the Lion loop.
  - Health forms (Parts A and B) for every rider, and an Activity Consent Form for the ride.
  - Two registered adults 21 or over. No one-on-one. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
- Family ride note (send home at Meeting 1):
  - The den ride is [date/place] (check). Bring your Lion's bike (a balance bike, trike or training wheels is fine), a helmet for your Lion and for you, closed-toe shoes and a filled water bottle.
  - Fill in the health form (Parts A and B) for your Lion and yourself, and sign the Activity Consent Form.
  - Before the ride, check the bike together at home: squeeze the tires, try the brakes, and look at the chain.
  - Put sunscreen on at home.
  - Can't make the ride? A family ride on a path away from traffic counts. Tell the den leader when you've done it.
  - E-bikes can't be used at Scouting activities.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Helmets and Bike Rules · 40 min
- All-dens option: the Lion and Tiger dens can share this night. Lion Reqs 1 and 2 match Tiger Reqs 2 and 3. Each den's leaders run their own pairs.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before the ride:
  - Email families to bring each Lion's helmet and the partner's helmet.
  - Borrow 2–3 spare helmets, and bring disinfectant spray.
  - Get Lion handbooks, or print the official activity pages. The official activities cite handbook pages 51 and 53, so check the pages in your edition.
  - Bring crayons, craft scissors and glue sticks.
  - Make 6 picture cards: sneakers with tied laces, flip-flops, a bright shirt, wide loose pants, bike gloves, and a helmet hanging on the handlebar.
  - Print the family ride note.
- Supplies: helmets, spare helmets, disinfectant, handbooks or printouts, crayons, craft scissors, glue sticks, picture cards, family ride notes
- Tell parents before they leave: The den ride is [date/place] (check). Bring the bike, two helmets, closed-toe shoes and water, and please fill in the health and consent forms.
1. **Helmet Check** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Your helmet protects your brain, the most important part of you. Let's make sure yours fits just right."
   - How: **Option A · Checking My Gear Before I Ride:** 1) Show the **fit** on your own head: level and low on the forehead, straps in a **V** under the ears, and one or two fingers under the chin strap. 2) Pairs **fit** the Lion's helmet, then the partner's. 3) A leader **checks** each Lion. / **Option B · Follow the Dots to Safety Gear:** 1) Show the fit on **your helmet**. 2) Pairs do the **handbook page** together. 3) Each Lion **points** to where the helmet sits on their partner's head.
   - Tip: **Loaners:** Spray borrowed helmets before and after.
2. **Dress the Rider** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "What else keeps a rider safe? Thumbs up or thumbs down!"
   - How: 1) Hold up each **picture card**. 2) Lions give a **thumbs up or down**. 3) Ask, "**Why?**" Flip-flops slip off, and loose pants catch in the chain.
   - Tip: **Note Req 1** for each Lion who can name their riding gear to their partner.
3. **Bike Rules Cut and Paste** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Riders have rules, just like drivers. Let's find them."
   - How: 1) Say the **rules** first: helmet on and buckled, ride with your grown-up, stop and look at corners, stay on the path, keep to the right, and one rider per bike. 2) Pairs **cut and glue** the handbook page (official). 3) Each Lion **tells** their partner one rule.
   - Tip: **Scissors:** Craft scissors only, with the partner helping.
4. **Stop and Go** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Hands on your pretend handlebars! Green means ride, red means stop, and 'Look!' means look both ways."
   - How: 1) Lions **"ride"** around the room at a walk. 2) Call **green, red and look**. 3) Partners walk beside them, just like on the ride.
   - Tip: **Note Req 2** for each Lion who can tell a rule to their partner.
5. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Lions, pick up three scraps with your grown-up."
   - How: 1) **Clean up**. 2) Put a **family ride note** in each partner's hand. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Helmets:** Families take their own home, and the loaners go back in the bin.
   - (at home) Practice buckling the helmet, and do an ABC check on the bike together.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Lion Ride (outing, about 60 min)
- Where: a paved park path away from traffic, on a Saturday morning (Option A or B above; check).
- Prep: 2–3 weeks before:
  - Follow the council outing process (check). Collect health forms (Parts A and B) for every rider and a consent form from each family.
  - Walk the route: flat, wide, with restrooms, water, shade and no road crossings. A loop under a mile is fine, ridden in laps.
  - Check the forecast. Move the date for thunderstorms or high heat.
  - Confirm two registered adults 21 or over, and name a lead adult and a sweep adult.
  - Send reminders a week and a day before (official Let's Roll Lions).
- Supplies: roster, health and consent forms, first-aid kit (non-latex gloves, wipes, bandages, gauze, cold pack), a disinfected spare helmet, pump, water jug, whistle, trash bag
- Tell parents before they leave: Great riding! If today was between May 1 and August 31, it also counts as one of the three for Tiger Summertime Fun.
1. **Arrival & Helmet Check** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Welcome, riders! Helmets on, grown-ups too. Let's check the fit."
   - How: 1) **Count** Lions and partners. 2) Every rider puts on a **helmet**. 3) A leader checks the **V and the fingers** under the strap. 4) **Fill** water bottles.
   - Tip: **No helmet, no ride:** That's what the spare is for.
2. **ABC Quick Check** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "Bikes get a checkup first. A is for air, B is for brakes, C is for chain!"
   - How: 1) Lions **squeeze** the tires. 2) Partners **squeeze the brakes** and roll the bike to see that it stops. 3) Everyone **looks** at the chain. 4) Adults **pump** or fix anything soft or loose.
   - Tip: **Loose bolt?** An adult tightens it. Lions never hold tools.
3. **Lion Ride** · den · 30 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Keep to the right, stop when the lead grown-up stops, and your grown-up stays right beside you."
   - How: 1) The **lead adult** goes first and the **sweep adult** goes last. 2) Pairs ride the **loop**, with the partner beside the Lion. 3) Stop at the **turnaround** and count. 4) Ride **more laps** while the Lions are happy.
   - Tip: **Any length is fine** (official pack page). Walking the bike back is okay.
4. **Water & Rules Stop** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 2 (set-up)
   - Say: "Shade and water! Who remembers a rider rule you used today?"
   - How: 1) Everyone **drinks** in the shade. 2) Each Lion **names a rule** they followed. 3) Check for **scrapes**.
   - Tip: **Scrapes:** An adult wearing gloves rinses and bandages it, and tells the partner.
5. **Headcount & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "You rode like real Scout riders!"
   - How: 1) **Count** everyone. 2) Do a **litter sweep**. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Note Req 3** for each Lion who rode in their gear and followed the rules. Take attendance in the app.
   - (at home) Ride together again on a safe path.

## Rolling Tigers (Tiger)
- Elective · Meetings: 3 (an indoor den meeting, a bike night and a den ride) · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/rolling-tigers/ · Other sources: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/the-abcs-of-bike-gear/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/the-abcs-of-my-bike/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/check-my-bike-gear/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/chalk-it-up-bike-rodeo/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/simon-says-turn-left-turn-right/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/traffic-light-freeze/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/i-want-to-ride-my-bicycle/, https://www.scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/leader-resources/pack-meeting-resources/cycling/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/gss/gss08/, https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/HealthSafety/pdf/680-685.pdf, https://www.pedbikeinfo.org/bicyclesaferjourney/abcquickcheck.pdf · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: Tigers and their adult partners learn the bike ABCs, helmet fit and hand signals at an indoor den night. At a bike night they learn the riding rules on a chalk rodeo course. Then they ride a park path together. Req 5 accepts "starting to learn," so a Tiger on a balance bike or training wheels earns it. Reqs 2–4 say "with your den or adult partner," and the den meetings count because partners are there.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Learn the bike ABCs: air, brakes, chain. (meeting)
  2. Learn the safety gear to wear when riding a bike. (meeting, with the partner there)
  3. Learn the safety rules for riding a bike. (bike night, with the partner there)
  4. Show the bike hand signals. (meeting, with the partner there)
  5. Ride a bike, or begin learning to ride one. (den ride with the partner there, or with family)
- Done at the pack opening: nothing.
- Done at a pack meeting: nothing on the standard calendar. The official Cycling pack meeting covers Reqs 1–3 (Safety Station) and Req 4 (Obstacle Course), and its pack ride covers Req 5. A guest-speaker night with a bike mechanic could also cover Reqs 1–4 through the official guest-speaker options, with partners there (check).
- Leader's choice for Req 1: Option A · The ABC's of Bike Gear: the leader shows example bikes indoors (official). / Option B · a cycling guest speaker, such as a bike-shop mechanic, at the den or at one of the pack's guest-speaker nights (official option). It's the same step with the same minutes.
- Leader's choice for Req 4: Option A · Simon Says – Turn Left, Turn Right (official). / Option B · Traffic Light Freeze (official).
- Leader's choice for the bike night's place: Option A · the pack's meeting-place parking lot: a section at least the size of a basketball court, away from the entrance and driving lanes, closed with cones, with the owner's permission (check). The den goes to the pack opening first, then walks out together. / Option B · a school blacktop or park basketball court, with permission. It's off-site, so collect consent forms, and the den meets there and skips the pack opening.
- No bike, or missed the ride:
  - A family ride counts (the official Req 5 accepts the partner or family). Tell the den leader the date.
  - At the rodeo, a Tiger without a bike walks the course with their partner and names each rule. That still meets Req 3, which is about learning the rules. Borrow a bike for the ride.
  - A den family may lend a bike, if the owner agrees (check).
  - The pack ride, if the pack holds one.
- Safety notes:
  - Partners stay beside their Tiger at every meeting. No drop-offs.
  - Helmets for every rider, partners too, and closed-toe shoes.
  - Tigers never hold tools. They squeeze, spin and pump with the partner, and adults adjust (Age Appropriate Guidelines, check).
  - Rodeo: cones block every car entrance to the course, and an adult stands at the cone line. One rider on the course at a time per lane.
  - Ride: on a path away from traffic (official I Want to Ride My Bicycle).
  - Health forms for every rider, and consent forms for anything off-site.
  - Two registered adults 21 or over. No one-on-one. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
- Family ride note (send home at Meeting 1):
  - Bike night is [date/place]. The den ride is [date/place] (check). Bring your Tiger's bike (a balance bike or training wheels is fine), helmets for both of you, closed-toe shoes and water.
  - Fill in the health form (Parts A and B) for both of you, and sign the consent form.
  - Do the ABC check together at home before each outing.
  - Put sunscreen on at home. E-bikes can't be used.
  - Can't make the ride? A family ride on a path away from traffic counts. Tell the den leader.

### Meeting 1 of 3 · ABCs, Helmets and Hand Signals · 40 min
- All-dens option: Tigers and Lions can share Steps 2 and 3. Lions do their own Meeting 1 steps alongside.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before:
  - Option A: bring two example bikes, one with a coaster brake and one with hand brakes (official), plus a pump with a gauge and a bottle of chain lube. Option B: book the guest and send them the ABC list.
  - Remind families to bring both helmets. Bring 2–3 spares and disinfectant.
  - Bring Tiger handbooks or printouts and pencils.
  - Print the family ride note.
- Supplies: example bikes, pump with gauge, chain lube, helmets, spares, disinfectant, handbooks or printouts, pencils, family ride notes
- Tell parents before they leave: Bike night is [date/place]. Bring the bike and both helmets in the car.
1. **ABCs of Bike Gear** · den · 15 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Every ride starts with the ABCs. A is for air, B is for brakes, and C is for crank, chain and cogs."
   - How: **Option A · The ABC's of Bike Gear:** 1) **A:** find the air number printed on the tire, check it with the gauge, and a Tiger pumps with their partner. 2) **B:** try the **coaster brake** (pedal backward) and squeeze the **hand brake** levers. 3) **C:** find the **crank, chain and cogs**, and look for rust or a saggy chain. 4) Pairs fill in the **handbook page**. / **Option B · Guest Speaker:** 1) The guest shows **A, B and C** on a real bike. 2) Each Tiger **squeezes, spins or pumps** one part. 3) Each Tiger asks **one question**.
   - Tip: **Tools stay with adults.** Tigers look, squeeze and pump.
2. **Helmet Fit** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "A helmet only works if it fits. Level, a V under each ear, and one or two fingers under the strap."
   - How: 1) **Show** the fit (official Check My Bike Gear). 2) Pairs **fit** the Tiger's helmet, then the partner's. 3) Name the **other gear**: closed-toe shoes, bright clothes and gloves.
   - Tip: **Note Req 2** for each Tiger who can show their partner a good fit.
3. **Hand Signal Game** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 4
   - Say: "Riders talk with their arms. Left arm straight out means left. Left arm bent up, or right arm straight out, means right. Left arm bent down means stop."
   - How: 1) Practice the **three signals** standing still. 2) **Option A · Simon Says:** signal only when "Simon says." / **Option B · Traffic Light Freeze:** walk on "green," signal and turn when called, and freeze with the stop signal on "red." 3) Partners **play too**.
   - Tip: **Note Req 4** for each Tiger who shows all three with their partner.
4. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Tigers, pick up three things with your grown-up."
   - How: 1) **Clean up**. 2) Put a **family ride note** in each partner's hand. 3) **Check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Loaner helmets** get sprayed and go back in the bin.
   - (at home) Do an ABC check on your own bike together.

### Meeting 2 of 3 · Chalk It Up Bike Rodeo · 40 min
- Where: Option A · the meeting-place lot, closed with cones (after the pack opening). / Option B · a school blacktop or park court (off-site; meet there). The space needs to be at least basketball-court size, about 94 by 50 feet (official).
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before:
  - Get permission for the space (check). Check that sunset is at least 45 minutes after den time ends.
  - Check the forecast. Rain washes the chalk away, so pick a backup date.

   - How: 1) Leaders **demonstrate** each lock. 2) Each scout **locks and unlocks** all three in turn, timed (official). 3) Compare **times**. 4) Each scout shows how they'd **secure** a bike through the frame and wheel.

   - Say: "One lap. Shift low at the 'hill' cone and high on the straight."

### Meeting 4 of 4 · The 10-Mile Patrol Ride (outing, about 150 min)
- Where: the route the patrol chose at Meeting 1, on a paved greenway or park trail away from traffic, on a Saturday morning (check).
- Prep: 2–3 weeks before:
  - Follow the council outing process (check). Collect health and consent forms (official AOL Bike Ride).
  - Share the route map with every adult (official). Ride the route yourself and note the crossings, water and restrooms.
  - Arrange a chase vehicle (check). The chase driver never carries a lone scout who isn't their own child. Call the scout's parent, add a second adult, or carry a buddy too.
  - Confirm two registered adults, a lead adult and a sweep adult with a way to reach each other.
  - Start by 8:30–9 a.m. in warm months. Check the forecast and the heat.
- Supplies: roster, buddy list, forms, route map, first-aid kit, pump, repair kits, spare tubes, water jug, whistle, trash bag
- Tell parents before they leave: If today fell between May 1 and August 31, it may also count toward Summertime Fun for any scout still earning it (check).
1. **Arrival, Helmets & Buddies** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 7 (set-up)
   - Say: "Patrol, buddies, helmets. Denner, read us the route."
   - How: 1) **Count** everyone. 2) Helmets **on**, adults too. 3) Pair up **buddies** by skill. 4) The denner **reads the route** and the stops.
   - Tip: **Road crossings:** Everyone walks their bike across, with an adult in the crosswalk.
2. **ABC Quick Check & Essentials** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Check your bike, check your pack, check your buddy's kit."
   - How: 1) Do the **ABC check**. 2) Check each scout's **essentials and water**. 3) Each buddy pair confirms its **repair kit**.
   - Tip: **Matches stay home.** Adults carry the first-aid kit.
3. **The Ride** · den · 115 min · Reqs: 7
   - Say: "Ten miles. Keep to the right, signal, call out hazards, and stay with your buddy."
   - How: 1) The **lead adult** goes first and the **sweep adult** last. 2) Scouts take turns **reading the map** at junctions. 3) Take **two water stops**, one at the turnaround. 4) If someone gets a **flat**, their buddy pair fixes it with an adult watching.
   - Tip: **Anyone flagging?** Slow the whole patrol down. The chase vehicle is for real trouble, not a race. The chase driver never carries a lone scout who isn't their own child. Call the scout's parent, add a second adult, or carry a buddy too.
4. **Start, Stop, Continue** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 6 (set-up)

   - How: 1) Everyone **drinks** in the shade. 2) Each scout gives **one start, stop or continue** (official). 3) Check for **scrapes**.
   - Tip: **Scrapes:** An adult wearing gloves cleans and bandages it, and tells the parent.
5. **Headcount & Handoff** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: 7
   - Say: "Ten miles, patrol. That's Cycling!"
   - How: 1) **Count** everyone. 2) Do a **litter sweep**. 3) **Hand off** each scout to their adult by name.
   - Tip: **Note Req 7** for each scout who rode at least 10 miles with a buddy. Take attendance in the app.
