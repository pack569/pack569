# Summertime Fun: elective, all ranks (DRAFT 3, 40-minute den time)

> **A guide, not the rulebook.** These are ideas to help a den leader run the meeting. They are not a complete or official source. The official requirements on scouting.org and the Guide to Safe Scouting always come first. If something here is unclear or unsettled, use your judgment and check with the Cubmaster.

Researched 2026-09-30 by the cubmaster-program agent, using the format of Let's Camp! (draft 2) and Race Time (draft 2). This is the next elective in the pass. Every plan here is in our own words. Requirements are only summarized, so use the official page for the full text. Anything marked "(check)" still needs confirming. Nothing is in the app yet. Draft 2 (2026-09-30) applies Keith's answers to open questions 1, 3, 5 and 7. Draft 3 (2026-09-30) applies his answer to open question 6: the pack never runs a swim, so the Webelos and AoL pool options are replaced with dry official outing ideas.

How these plans fit a Pack 569 den night:
- The pack does the opening ceremony together: flag, Pledge, Oath and Law. Each den then splits off for about 40 minutes.
- Den meetings here have no gathering or opening steps. Each one is den steps plus one short den closing. The minutes add up to 35–40 and never go over 40.
- "Reqs: N" means the step does real work on that requirement. "Reqs: N (set-up)" means the step only sets up or reinforces it and does not complete it.
- The guides don't tick requirements. Leaders record progress on the app's Advancement board afterward.
- **Summertime Fun is not like other electives.** Every rank has one requirement: take part in three Cub Scout activities at any time from May through August. Each of the three can be a den activity, a pack activity or a council activity, in any mix. For AoL the den option is called a "patrol activity." That's the whole adventure. The official activity pages give ideas only (listed under each rank), and "You can choose other activities."
- **Only outings and events count (Keith, 2026-09-30).** A regular den night or pack meeting is not one of the three. Only these count:
  - den summer outings (a patrol outing for AoL)
  - pack summer events that are outings or special events, not the routine pack meeting
  - council camps and events
  - Each rank still gets one 40-minute kickoff den night in May, but it's only for planning and gives no credit. All its steps are (set-up).
  - The other three "meetings" are the three outings. A scout needs three outings or events, and the make-ups listed under each rank give a scout who misses one a path to three.
- **Counting twice is allowed (Keith, 2026-09-30).** One outing may count for Summertime Fun and for another adventure at the same time, such as a den outing that also does work on another adventure, or adventure work done at day camp. The official Summertime Fun pages don't say either way.
- **Which scouts, and which summer.** Summertime Fun belongs to the rank a scout is *moving into*. The Tiger page says it's for the summer after kindergarten, the Wolf page the summer after 1st grade, and so on up to AoL after 4th grade. The Scouting America systems move every scout up a grade on June 1 (the 2024 program FAQ). A council cross-check (manatoc.org) says the same: rising Tigers through rising AoLs. So in **summer 2027**:
  - This year's Lions earn **Tiger** Summertime Fun, and the summer den leader runs their May kickoff.
  - This year's Tigers earn **Wolf**, Wolves earn **Bear**, Bears earn **Webelos**, and Webelos earn **AoL**.
  - This year's AoL scouts cross over to Scouts BSA, so they don't earn it.
- Pack 569 decides in April who leads each rising den over the summer (current or next year's den leader). The guides call that person "the summer den leader."
- **No Lion Summertime Fun.** The official Lion adventure list doesn't have it. The national Summertime Fun page gives the reason: Lions start in the fall of kindergarten, so a Lion has no Cub Scout summer. The app's comment at `ADVENTURES` (~line 7578) says the same.
- **Missed it?** The Wolf, Bear and Webelos pages each say that a scout who learns about it late can earn the next rank's version the following summer, because the requirement is the same. The AoL page says a scout who missed it will be in Scouts BSA the next summer and can go to Scouts BSA resident camp, so AoL has no second chance. The Tiger page has no such line.
- **Look back at summer 2026 (check).** Today's Tigers, Wolves, Bears, Webelos and AoLs had their Summertime Fun summer in May–August 2026, before the app's year began in July. If a scout went to three Cub Scout outings or events then (a pack event, day camp, a den outing; regular meetings don't count), they may already have earned their current rank's Summertime Fun. Den leaders can ask families and record it now (check that Scoutbook Plus still accepts it).
- **How the summer den leader keeps track, without ticks.** Keep a summer log: each outing or event's name, date and who came. Regular den nights and pack meetings don't go on it.
  - Den outings and pack events: take attendance in the app on that event.
  - Council camps and pack events the summer den leader missed: families send a one-line note ("Ava went to day camp, June 1–5").
  - When a scout reaches three, record Summertime Fun on the Advancement board and in Scoutbook Plus. Do it only **after the dens have moved up** in the app (see Open questions: moving dens up clears marks).
- **Pack 569's summer, from the app's standard year (`SEED_ACTIVITIES`):** "Crossover & graduation" in May, "Day camp week" and "Resident camp" in June, "Summertime Fun pack activity" in July, and the "Back-to-school recruiting night / open house" in August. Real 2027 dates are unknown (check). The pack's campouts (Scoutland in October, Fort Yargo in March, Rainey Mountain in April) aren't in the May–August window. The council's summer overnight, Cub Summer Splash, is.
  - **Which pack events count:** the July "Summertime Fun pack activity" counts, as long as it's an outing or special event (check the 2027 plan). Any other pack outing or special event from May to August also counts (check the pack's 2027 calendar).
  - "Day camp week" and "Resident camp" are council camps, so they count as council activities (check which 2027 sessions the pack promotes).
  - The May crossover and the August recruiting night don't count if they're held as regular pack meetings. They count only if the pack holds them as an outing or special event (check).
- **Council summer programs (NEGA, 2026 dates; the 2027 dates aren't posted, check):**
  - **Your district's Cub Scout day camp and twilight camp:** check NEGA's camp pages for the pack's district, and its dates, fees and age rules (check). In 2026, NEGA district day camps cost about $100–130, and a Tiger needed a parent or adult to attend with them.
  - **Scoutland Cub Summer Quest:** Saturday half-day sessions in June (in 2026: June 13, 20 and 27), such as Bug Crawl, Fish Camp and Rocket Academy.
  - **Cub Summer Splash at Camp Rainey Mountain:** July 17–19, 2026, overnight, with lake swimming, archery or BB guns, and meals in the dining hall. It cost $175 per scout and $50 per adult. An adult must attend with a scout who comes as a family. One NEGA page calls it four days and three nights, and the dates show three days (check).
  - The official council activity pages name Cub Scout day camp and resident camp as examples.
- **Don't use the old award.** Before 2024 there was a National Summertime Pack Award with pack-level rules and den participation percentages. The national Summertime Fun page says the adventure replaced it and dropped the percentage rule. Some older council pages still describe the old rules (check any source dated before June 2024).
- **Safety for every summer activity:**
  - **SAFE Checklist:** the official pages ask leaders to use it before every activity.
  - **Outing paperwork:** Collect health forms (Parts A and B) and permission slips, follow the council outing process, and coordinate with the Cubmaster; another adult knows how to use any scout's EpiPen.
  - **Heat** (Heat-Related Illness safety moment): plan outings for the morning. Everyone drinks water often and takes shade breaks.
    - Heat exhaustion looks like pale skin, heavy sweating and strong thirst. Move the scout to a cool spot, give them water and cool them down. Get medical help if they aren't better within 30 minutes.
    - Heatstroke looks like very hot, flushed skin and confusion. It's an emergency: call 911 and cool the scout fast.
    - North Georgia afternoons in June and July are hot and humid, and thunderstorms build after lunch. At the first thunder, everyone goes into hard-topped cars or a solid building.
  - **Sun:** hats and shade. Families put sunscreen on at home before the outing. Scouts reapply their own, and leaders don't apply it to other people's children (pack practice).
  - **Ticks and mosquitoes** (Guide to Safe Scouting, Animal and Insect Hazards): repellent, and a tick-check habit. Families put repellent on before the outing, and adults handle any spray. Stay on trails and out of tall grass. Tick-check at home that night.
  - **Water:** Pack 569 never runs a swim (Keith, 2026-09-30). Scouts swim only at council camps such as Cub Summer Splash, where the camp runs Safe Swim Defense, or with their families on their own. No one goes in any creek, pond or lake on a den or pack outing.
  - **Food:** no cooking at these activities. Keith's cooking rule: meals are cooked at home, at a pack campout, or at a Webelos/AoL den or patrol campout, with adults running the stoves.
    - A sealed, no-cook snack is fine with an allergy check first and hand-washing, using wipes or sanitizer at a park.
  - **Devices:** scouts don't use them. On geocaching outings the leader's phone does the navigating, and scouts use printed clue cards.
  - **Knives:** nothing here needs one. A rising Bear or rising AoL hasn't earned that rank's knife adventure yet this year, so adults handle any knife.
  - **Adults:** two registered adults 21 or over at every den activity, including outings, and no one-on-one contact. For Lions and Tigers, an adult partner doesn't count as one of the two unless they are registered as a leader. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.

  - the official Lion adventure list, which has no Summertime Fun. The Lion URL returns only a maintenance page.

## Summertime Fun (Tiger)
- Elective · Meetings: 4 (a May planning den night with no credit, then three summer outings or events; a scout needs three, and make-ups count) · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/summertime-fun-tiger/ · Other sources: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/den-activity-tiger/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/pack-activity-tiger/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/council-activity-tiger/, https://www.scouting.org/program-updates/summertime-fun/, https://www.scouting.org/program-updates/cub-scout-program-updates-faq-1-3-24/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/gss/gss02/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/gss/gss13/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/safety-moments/heat-related-illness/, https://www.nega-bsa.org/cs-twi-stem-cmp, https://www.nega-bsa.org/SummerCubFun, https://www.nega-bsa.org/cubsplash, https://www.manatoc.org/cub-summer · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: This is for the summer after kindergarten, when a Lion becomes a Tiger. The scouts are this spring's Lions, with their adult partners. The summer den leader holds a planning night at the Lion den's last den night in May, and it gives no credit. The three that count are all summer outings and events: a den outing in June, the pack's July event, and a council camp, with make-ups for a Tiger who misses one. Any three outings or events between May 1 and August 31 earn it. The adult partner comes to every one. The window is that one summer, so a new Tiger who joins in the fall has missed it and earns Wolf Summertime Fun the next summer instead.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Join three Cub Scout activities, any mix, May through August. (den outing, pack summer outing or special event, or council camp or event; with the adult partner. Regular den nights and pack meetings, including the May kickoff, don't count.)
- Done at the pack opening: nothing.
- Done at a pack meeting: nothing. A regular pack meeting doesn't count, even between May and August (Keith, 2026-09-30).
- Done at a pack summer event: one of the three: the pack's July Summertime Fun activity, held as an outing or special event. Any other pack outing or special event from May to August also counts (check the pack's 2027 calendar). The May crossover and the August recruiting night count only if the pack holds them as an outing or special event rather than a regular pack meeting (check). The summer den leader confirms who came.
- Done at a council camp: one of the three: the district day camp or twilight camp, a Scoutland Summer Quest session, or Cub Summer Splash (2027 dates: check). NEGA requires a parent or adult at day camp with every Tiger.
- Leader's choice for the den outing: Option A · Short Day Hike: about a mile on an easy, shady trail (official den example). / Option B · Park Scavenger Hunt: pairs hunt from a picture list (official den example). It's the same slot with the same minutes.
- Make-ups (a Tiger who misses one of the three still has a path; regular den nights and pack meetings don't count):
  - a second den outing in August using the other option, which the summer den leader schedules if any Tiger is short of three (check the date)
  - a second council session, such as another Scoutland Summer Quest Saturday, or Cub Summer Splash on top of day camp (whether a second session counts: see Open question 2, check)
  - any other pack outing or special event from May to August, such as a June or August pack event if the pack adds one (check)
- Safety notes:
  - The adult partner stays with their Tiger at every activity. Partners don't count as one of the two registered adults unless they are registered. No drop-offs. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
  - Heat: morning outings, water every stop, and shade breaks. Watch for pale skin, heavy sweating, dizziness or a headache, and move that Tiger to the shade with water right away.
  - Sun: hats on. Families put sunscreen on at home.
  - Ticks: families put repellent on at home. Stay on the trail, and tick-check at home that night.
  - Water: no one goes in any creek, pond or fountain. Swimming isn't part of the Tiger plan.
  - Snacks: sealed and nut-free unless the den is clear, with an allergy check and hand wipes first.
  - Two registered adults 21 or over at every den activity. No one-on-one. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
- Family summer note (send home at Meeting 1):
  - Your Lion becomes a Tiger this summer. Tiger Summertime Fun means going to **three** Cub Scout outings or events between May 1 and August 31: den outings, pack summer events and council camps. Regular den nights and pack meetings don't count, and neither does tonight's planning night.
  - One outing can count for Summertime Fun and for another adventure at the same time.
  - Our summer: den outing [date/place], pack summer event [date/place], council camps [NEGA list, 2027 dates: check].
  - Missed one? Make-ups: a second den outing [date/place], another council session, or another pack outing or event [date/place] (check).
  - You come with your Tiger to every activity. At council day camp, NEGA requires a parent or adult with every Tiger.
  - If your Tiger goes to a council camp or pack event without the den, text the summer den leader the name and date.
  - Put sunscreen and bug repellent on at home. Send a filled water bottle and a hat. Tick-check that night.
  - Tell the summer den leader about any food allergy.

### Meeting 1 of 4 · Summer Kickoff · 40 min
- Who: the Lion den (the rising Tigers) and their adult partners, at the last den night in May, led by the summer den leader. This is a planning night only and gives no credit.
- All-dens option: the Lion, Tiger, Wolf and Bear dens can hold their kickoffs in one room. Steps 1 and 2 are nearly the same across ranks. Each den keeps its own leaders and its own summer calendar.
- Leader's choice for the date: Option A · the last den night in **May**. / Option B · a late-**April** den night. Either way it's a planning night only and gives no credit, so the Tiger needs three summer outings or events after it.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before:
  - Get the summer dates: the den outing, the pack's July event, the council camps and the make-up dates (2027: check).
  - Print a big May–August calendar poster with those dates marked.
  - Make 6 summer safety picture cards: a sun hat, a water bottle, a shady tree, a tick on a sock, a creek with a grown-up pointing "stay back," and two buddies holding hands.
  - Pack a sample summer bag: a water bottle, a hat, sunscreen, bug spray (closed), a sealed nut-free snack, a whistle and a small first-aid kit, plus mittens and a winter hat as wrong answers.
  - Print the family summer note.
- Supplies: calendar poster, crayons, safety cards, sample summer bag, family summer notes
- Tell parents before they leave: This summer, three Cub Scout outings or events earn Tiger Summertime Fun: den outings, pack summer events and council camps. Tonight was for planning and doesn't count. The den outing is [date/place]. Come with your Tiger to every activity.
1. **Our Summer Map** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "This summer you turn into Tigers! Tigers go on three Scout outings or events before school starts. Let's find them on the calendar."
   - How: 1) Show the **calendar** with the den outing, the pack event and the council camps. 2) Each pair **draws a sun** on the dates they hope to come. 3) Count to **three** together. 4) Point out that **tonight** is just for planning, and the three start with the first outing.
   - Tip: **Planning only:** Tonight doesn't count toward the three. Take attendance in the app as a normal den night.
2. **Sun, Shade or Splash?** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Summer is hot! I'll hold up a picture. Show me what a smart Scout does."
   - How: 1) Hold up each **card**. 2) Pairs **act it out**: put on a hat, take a sip, walk to the shade, take a big step back from the creek with your buddy, and check your socks for ticks. 3) Ask, "**What do you tell your grown-up if you feel dizzy or too hot?**"
   - Tip: **Water rule:** On a Scout outing nobody goes in any water. Tigers swim only at council camp or with their families.
3. **Pack a Summer Bag** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "What goes in your bag for a summer Scout day?"
   - How: 1) Pull items from the **sample bag** one at a time. 2) Tigers-to-be call **"Pack it!" or "Leave it!"** 3) Partners explain that **grown-ups** handle sunscreen and bug spray.
   - Tip: **Snack stays sealed:** No eating tonight.
4. **Summer Buddy Check** · den · 3 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "At every summer outing you have a buddy. Buddy check!"
   - How: 1) **Pair up** the scouts. 2) On "Buddy check!", buddies **grab hands and raise them**. 3) Do it **twice**.
   - Tip: **Odd number?** A group of three works fine.
5. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Pick up three things with your grown-up."
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 2) Put a **family summer note** in each partner's hand. 3) **Check out** each scout with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Hand over:** The summer den leader keeps the attendance list and the summer log. In late August, pass both to whoever leads the Tiger den in the fall, if that's someone else.
   - (at home) Put the summer dates on the family calendar.

### Meeting 2 of 4 · Den Summer Outing (outing, about 80 min)
- Prep: 2–3 weeks before:
  - Pick Option A or B.
  - Choose a local park with shade, water fountains and restrooms, (check trail length and rules).
  - Set a 9 a.m. start. Check the forecast and the heat two days out and again that morning, and move the date if storms or high heat are forecast.
  - Confirm two registered adults 21 or over.
  - Collect health forms (Parts A and B) and permission slips, follow the council outing process, and coordinate with the Cubmaster; another adult knows how to use any scout's EpiPen.
  - Know the nearest urgent care.
  - Option B: make a picture scavenger list: a pinecone, a bird, a shady tree, a bench, something red, a flower, a squirrel and an animal track.
- Supplies: roster, buddy list, water jug and cups, first-aid kit, whistle, trash bag, hand wipes, scavenger lists (Option B)
- Tell parents before they leave: That's one of the three. Tick-check your Tiger tonight. The pack summer event is [date/place].
1. **Arrival Buddy Check** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Welcome, Tigers! Find your buddy and your grown-up."
   - How: 1) **Count** every Tiger and partner. 2) Fill **water bottles** and put **hats** on. 3) Show the **boundary** and the **restrooms**: a parent takes their own Lion or Tiger; otherwise buddies go together and an adult waits outside. 4) Do a **buddy check**.
   - Tip: **Late arrivals:** Text them where the den is.
2. **Summer Outing** · den · 55 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Let's explore! Stay with your buddy and your grown-up."
   - How: **Option A · Short Day Hike:** 1) Walk about **a mile** on an easy trail, with partners beside their Tigers. 2) Stop at **three look spots** and ask what everyone sees, hears and smells. 3) A leader walks **first** and another walks **last**. / **Option B · Park Scavenger Hunt:** 1) Pairs get a **picture list**. 2) They **look and point**, without picking anything. 3) Regroup and **share** the best find.
   - Tip: **Leave it:** Plants, rocks and critters stay where you found them.
3. **Shade & Water Stop** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Everyone into the shade. Three big sips!"
   - How: 1) Sit in the **shade**. 2) Everyone **drinks**. 3) Partners **check** their Tiger's face: red, pale, or sweaty and tired means more rest and water.
   - Tip: **Snack:** Only if planned, sealed, allergy-checked, and after hand wipes.
4. **Litter Sweep & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Tigers leave a place better than they found it!"
   - How: 1) Do a two-minute **litter sweep** with the trash bag. 2) **Count** everyone. 3) **Check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Note Req 1** (one activity) for each Tiger who came, and take attendance in the app.
   - (at home) Tick check tonight.

### Meeting 3 of 4 · Pack Summer Event (outing, about 120 min)
- All-dens option: this is the pack's event. Each den keeps its own group, buddy pairs and headcount inside it.
- Prep:
  - The Cubmaster or activities chair plans the pack's July Summertime Fun activity (the app's standard year) as an outing or special event, not a routine pack meeting, so it counts. The official pack ideas are pack camping, fishing, a bike rodeo and ride, bowling, stargazing with a local astronomy club, and a zoo, aquarium or science museum visit.
  - The pack never runs a swim (Keith, 2026-09-30). Fishing is from the shore or a dock, and no one wades. Boating needs Safety Afloat.
  - The summer den leader brings the roster and knows which partner comes with each Tiger.
- Supplies: roster, buddy list, water, first-aid kit, whistle
- Tell parents before they leave: That's another of the three. Council camp options are on the family note. Tell me if your Tiger goes.
1. **Den Meet-Up** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Tigers, here's our den spot. If you get separated, come back here."
   - How: 1) **Count** Tigers and partners. 2) Show the **den spot**. 3) Do a **buddy check**.
   - Tip: **Name tags** help at a crowded venue.
2. **Pack Program** · den · 100 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Have fun with the whole pack. Stay with your grown-up!"
   - How: 1) The **pack runs** the activity. 2) The den stays **together** where it can. 3) A leader calls a **water stop** every 30 minutes outdoors.
   - Tip: **Heat:** Indoor venues like a bowling alley or museum are good in July.
3. **Headcount & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Great job, Tigers!"
   - How: 1) **Count** everyone. 2) **Check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Note Req 1** (one activity) for each Tiger who came.

### Meeting 4 of 4 · Council Summer Camp (outing, a council camp session)
- The council runs the program. The den steps below are only for a den that attends together. A family that goes on its own texts the summer den leader afterward.
- Prep: in March and April:
  - Share NEGA's summer list: the pack's district day camp and twilight camp (check), Scoutland Summer Quest, and Cub Summer Splash (2027 dates and fees: check).
  - Encourage families to **register the den for the same week**.
  - NEGA requires a parent or adult with every Tiger at day camp.
  - Day camps usually need adult volunteers. Ask the camp director (check).
- Supplies: list of who registered, buddy list
- Tell parents before they leave: Please text me when your Tiger finishes camp so I can log it.
1. **First-Morning Huddle** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "This week we're at camp together! Here's your buddy, and here's where we meet."
   - How: 1) **Count** Tigers and partners. 2) Do a **buddy check**. 3) Water bottles **full**, hats **on**. 4) Meet the camp's **den guide** if there is one.
   - Tip: **Health forms:** Camps collect their own. Families bring them.
2. **Last-Day Pickup** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "You finished camp, Tigers!"
   - How: 1) **Count** everyone. 2) **Check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Note Req 1** (one activity) for each Tiger who attended. A camp week counts as one activity (check).

- Elective · Meetings: 4 (a May planning den night with no credit, then three summer outings or events; a scout needs three, and make-ups count) · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/summertime-fun-webelos/ · Other sources: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/den-activity-webelos/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/pack-activity-webelos/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/council-activity-webelos/, https://www.scouting.org/program-updates/summertime-fun/, https://www.scouting.org/program-updates/cub-scout-program-updates-faq-1-3-24/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/gss/gss02/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/gss/gss13/, https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/safety-moments/heat-related-illness/, https://www.nega-bsa.org/cs-twi-stem-cmp, https://www.nega-bsa.org/SummerCubFun, https://www.nega-bsa.org/cubsplash, https://www.manatoc.org/cub-summer · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: This is for the summer after 3rd grade. The scouts are this spring's Bears. The summer den leader holds a planning night (no credit) at the Bear den's last den night in May. The scouts help plan the den outing, and for the scavenger hunt they write the list themselves. The pack never runs a swim (Keith, 2026-09-30), so both den outing options stay out of the water. The three that count are a den outing, the pack's July event and a council camp, with make-ups for a scout who misses one. Any three outings or events between May 1 and August 31 earn it. A Webelos who misses it can earn the AoL version next summer (official page).
- Requirements (own words):
  1. Join three Cub Scout activities, any mix, May through August. (den outing, pack summer outing or special event, or council camp or event. Regular den nights and pack meetings, including the May kickoff, don't count.)
- Done at the pack opening: nothing.
- Done at a pack meeting: nothing. A regular pack meeting doesn't count, even between May and August (Keith, 2026-09-30).
- Done at a pack summer event: one of the three: the pack's July Summertime Fun activity, held as an outing or special event. Any other pack outing or special event from May to August also counts (check the pack's 2027 calendar). The May crossover and the August recruiting night count only if the pack holds them as an outing or special event rather than a regular pack meeting (check). The summer den leader confirms who came.
- Done at a council camp: one of the three: the district day camp or twilight camp, a Scoutland Summer Quest session, or Cub Summer Splash (2027 dates: check).
- Leader's choice for the den outing: Option A · Day Hike: 2–3 miles on a shady trail (official den example). / Option B · Scavenger Hunt: buddy pairs work a nature list the den wrote at the kickoff, inside a set boundary in a shady park (official den example). It's the same slot with the same minutes.
- Make-ups (a Webelos who misses one of the three still has a path; regular den nights and pack meetings don't count):
  - a second den outing in August using the other option, which the summer den leader schedules if any scout is short of three (check the date)
  - a second council session, such as another Scoutland Summer Quest Saturday, or Cub Summer Splash on top of day camp (whether a second session counts: see Open question 2, check)
  - any other pack outing or special event from May to August, such as a June or August pack event if the pack adds one (check)
- Safety notes:
  - Water: the pack never runs a swim (Keith, 2026-09-30). No one goes in any creek, pond or lake on the outing. Scouts swim only at council camp or with their families.
  - Scavenger hunt (Option B): look and point, don't pick. Stay on paths and inside the boundary, with an adult at each end of it. Stay out of tall grass.
  - Heat: morning outings, water, and shade breaks. Anyone pale, dizzy or with a headache goes to the shade with water and an adult.
  - Thunder: everyone off the trail, into the building or the cars.
  - Ticks: families put repellent on at home for the outing. Tick-check that night.
  - Knives: none needed. Chef's Knife is a kitchen adventure, so no pocketknives.
  - Snacks: sealed, allergy-checked, and after hand-washing.
  - Two registered adults 21 or over at every den activity. No one-on-one. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
- Family summer note (send home at Meeting 1):
  - Your Bear becomes a Webelos this summer. Webelos Summertime Fun means **three** Cub Scout outings or events between May 1 and August 31: den outings, pack summer events and council camps. Regular den nights and pack meetings don't count, and neither does tonight's planning night.
  - One outing can count for Summertime Fun and for another adventure at the same time.
  - Our summer: den outing [date/place], pack summer event [date/place], council camps [NEGA list, 2027 dates: check].
  - Missed one? Make-ups: a second den outing [date/place], another council session, or another pack outing or event [date/place] (check).
  - The den and pack never run a swim. Scouts swim only at council camps such as Cub Summer Splash, where the camp runs it, or with your family.
  - If your Webelos goes to a council camp or pack event without the den, text the summer den leader the name and date.
  - Sunscreen and repellent at home, plus a filled water bottle and a hat. Tick-check that night.
  - Tell the summer den leader about any food allergy.

### Meeting 1 of 4 · Summer Kickoff · 40 min
- Who: the Bear den (the rising Webelos), at the last den night in May, led by the summer den leader. This is a planning night only and gives no credit.
- All-dens option: the Wolf and Bear dens can share steps 1 and 3. The Bear den keeps its own planning step.
- Leader's choice for the date: Option A · the last den night in **May**. / Option B · a late-**April** den night. Either way it's a planning night only and gives no credit.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before:
  - Get the summer dates, including the make-up dates, and mark them on a May–August calendar poster.
  - Print a one-page outing planner: where, when, what to bring, buddy pairs, and a water and shade plan.
  - Option B: print a map of the park and a blank scavenger list with 10 lines.
  - Print the family summer note.
- Supplies: calendar poster, outing planners, pencils, park map and blank scavenger list (Option B), a whistle, family summer notes
- Tell parents before they leave: Three Cub Scout outings or events this summer earn Webelos Summertime Fun: den outings, pack summer events and council camps. Tonight was for planning and doesn't count. The den outing is [date/place].
1. **Our Summer Map** · den · 7 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "This summer you become Webelos! Your job: three Scout outings or events before school starts."
   - How: 1) Show the **calendar**. 2) Each scout **marks** the dates they plan to come. 3) Scouts **count** their three.
   - Tip: **Planning only:** Tonight doesn't count toward the three. Take attendance in the app as a normal den night.
2. **Buddy Check Drill** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "On the trail or in the park, the whistle means 'Buddy check!' Grab your buddy's hand, raise it, and freeze. We'll count you."
   - How: 1) **Pair up**. 2) Scouts **walk** the room, apart from their buddies. 3) On the **whistle**, buddies find each other and raise their hands in **10 seconds**. 4) An adult **counts pairs**. Do it **four times**, faster each time.
   - Tip: **On the outing:** A leader calls this at every stop, and at the regroup spot on a scavenger hunt.
3. **Beat the Heat Plan** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Summer outings in Georgia are hot. What's our den's plan so nobody gets sick?"
   - How: 1) The den names the **warning signs** of getting too hot. 2) It agrees on a **plan**: a morning start, water every stop, shade breaks, and anyone feeling bad tells an adult. 3) It adds the **thunder rule** and a **tick check** at home.
   - Tip: **Heatstroke** is an emergency: very hot skin and confusion mean call 911.
4. **Plan Our Outing** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "You're Webelos now. Help plan it."
   - How: **Option A · Day Hike:** 1) Scouts fill in the **planner**: the route, the start time, what to bring. 2) They pick **buddy pairs**. / **Option B · Scavenger Hunt:** 1) Look at the **park map** together. 2) The den writes its own **list** of 8–10 things to find (look, don't pick). 3) Scouts fill in the **planner**: what to bring (water, hat, pencil) and **buddy pairs**.
   - Tip: **Denner** reads the finished plan back to the den.
5. **Clean-Up & Handoff** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Five things each, then line up with your buddy."
   - How: 1) **Clean up**. 2) Put a **family summer note** in each adult's hand. 3) **Hand off** each scout to their adult by name.
   - Tip: **Hand over:** The summer den leader keeps the plan, the attendance list and the summer log. In late August, pass them to whoever leads the Webelos den in the fall, if that's someone else.
   - (at home) Put the summer dates on the family calendar.

### Meeting 2 of 4 · Den Summer Outing (outing, about 80 min)
- Prep: 2–3 weeks before:
  - Option A: choose a shady 2–3 mile trail with restrooms and water.
  - Option B: choose a shady park with restrooms and water (check park rules). Walk it the week before, set the boundary and a regroup spot, and check that most of the den's list can really be found there. Print a copy of the den's list for each buddy pair.
  - Morning start. Check the forecast and the heat.
  - Confirm two registered adults 21 or over.
  - Collect health forms (Parts A and B) and permission slips, follow the council outing process, and coordinate with the Cubmaster; another adult knows how to use any scout's EpiPen.
- Supplies: roster, buddy list, the den's plan, water jug, first-aid kit, whistle, trash bag, scavenger lists and pencils (Option B)
- Tell parents before they leave: That's one of the three. Tick-check tonight. The pack summer event is [date/place].
1. **Arrival Buddy Check** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Denner, call the buddy check."
   - How: 1) **Count** everyone. 2) Fill **water bottles**. 3) Go over the den's **plan**. 4) Option B: show the **boundary** and the **regroup spot**.
   - Tip: **Restrooms:** Buddies go together, and adults wait outside.
2. **Summer Outing** · den · 55 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Webelos, this is the outing you planned!"
   - How: **Option A · Day Hike:** 1) Hike **2–3 miles**, with scouts taking turns **leading** behind the front adult. 2) Stop to look at **three things** worth seeing. 3) The den follows its **heat plan**. / **Option B · Scavenger Hunt:** 1) Buddy pairs work the den's **list** inside the boundary. 2) They **look and point**, without picking, and mark each find. 3) On the **whistle**, everyone comes to the regroup spot for a **buddy check** and a water sip, then hunts again. 4) At the end, pairs **share** their best find.
   - Tip: **Off at thunder:** Everyone off the trail and to the cars at the first rumble.
3. **Shade & Water Stop** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Water and shade, everyone. How do you feel?"
   - How: 1) **Shade**. 2) **Drink**. 3) Do a **heat check**.
   - Tip: **Snack:** Only if planned, sealed and allergy-checked.
4. **Litter Sweep & Handoff** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Leave it better than we found it."
   - How: 1) Do a **litter sweep**. 2) **Count** everyone. 3) **Hand off** each scout to their adult by name.
   - Tip: **Note Req 1** (one activity) for each Webelos who came, and take attendance in the app.
   - (at home) Tick check tonight.

### Meeting 3 of 4 · Pack Summer Event (outing, about 120 min)
- All-dens option: this is the pack's event. Each den keeps its own group, buddy pairs and headcount.
- Prep: the pack plans its July Summertime Fun activity as an outing or special event, not a routine pack meeting, so it counts. The official ideas are pack camping, fishing, a bike rodeo and ride, bowling, stargazing, and a zoo, aquarium or science museum. The pack never runs a swim (Keith, 2026-09-30). - Supplies: roster, buddy list, water, first-aid kit, whistle
- Tell parents before they leave: That's another of the three. If your Webelos goes to a council camp, text me.
1. **Den Meet-Up** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Webelos, here's our den spot."
   - How: 1) **Count** everyone. 2) Show the **den spot**. 3) Do a **buddy check**.
   - Tip: **Help the Tigers:** Webelos can help a younger den find its spot.
2. **Pack Program** · den · 100 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Have fun with the whole pack. Stay with your buddy!"
   - How: 1) The **pack runs** it. 2) The den stays **together** where it can. 3) Call a **water stop** every 30 minutes outdoors.
   - Tip: **Stargazing night?** Flashlights point at the ground, and the buddy system still applies in the dark.
3. **Headcount & Handoff** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Great job, Webelos!"
   - How: 1) **Count** everyone. 2) **Hand off** each scout to their adult by name.
   - Tip: **Note Req 1** (one activity) for each Webelos who came.

### Meeting 4 of 4 · Council Summer Camp (outing, a council camp session)
- The council runs the program. The den steps are only for a den that attends together. A family that goes on its own texts the summer den leader afterward.
- Prep: in March and April, share NEGA's summer list (2027 dates and fees: check) and encourage the den to register for the same week. Cub Summer Splash is a good den overnight: the council runs the lake swim and the ranges, and the dining hall cooks.

- Tell parents before they leave: Please text me when your Webelos finishes camp.

   - Say: "We're at camp together! Here's your buddy and our meeting spot."
   - How: 1) **Count** everyone. 2) Do a **buddy check**. 3) Water **full**, hats **on**. 4) Meet the **den guide**.
   - Tip: **Swim test:** At a council camp the camp's aquatics staff classify swimmers.
2. **Last-Day Pickup** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "You finished camp, Webelos!"
   - How: 1) **Count** everyone. 2) **Hand off** each scout to their adult by name.
   - Tip: **Note Req 1** (one activity) for each Webelos who attended. A camp week counts as one (check).
