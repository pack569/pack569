# Race Time: elective, all ranks (DRAFT 2, 40-minute den time)

> **A guide, not the rulebook.** These are ideas to help a den leader run the meeting. They are not a complete or official source. The official requirements on scouting.org and the Guide to Safe Scouting always come first. If something here is unclear or unsettled, use your judgment and check with the Cubmaster.

Researched 2026-09-30 by the cubmaster-program agent, using the format of Webelos draft 2, Lion draft 1 and Tiger draft 1. This is the first elective in the pass. Every plan here is in our own words. Requirements are only summarized, so use the official page for the full text. Anything marked "(check)" still needs confirming. Nothing is in the app yet.

How these plans fit a Pack 569 den night:
- The pack does the opening ceremony together: flag, Pledge, Oath and Law. Each den then splits off for about 40 minutes.
- Den meetings here have no gathering or opening steps. Each one is den steps plus one short den closing. The minutes add up to 35–40 and never go over 40.
- No Race Time requirement is done at the pack opening. The race itself is done at the pack's derby, so each rank has a "Done at the pack derby:" line instead of a den step, and the den leader confirms who raced.
- "Reqs: N" means the step does real work on that requirement. "Reqs: N (set-up)" means the step only sets up or reinforces it and does not complete it.
- The guides don't tick requirements. Leaders record progress on the app's Advancement board afterward.
- **Timing.** Every rank does two den meetings. Meeting 1 (kit night) comes 4–6 weeks before the derby. Meeting 2 (pit stop) comes 1–2 weeks before the derby and before check-in, because the sportsmanship requirement has to happen "before the race." The app's standard year puts the Pinewood Derby in January and a Raingutter Regatta in March (`SEED_ACTIVITIES` in `index.html`). The pack's real dates are unknown (check).
- **Cars are built at home with family.** Den meetings cover design, rules, safety, a weight and wheel check, and sportsmanship. Each rank's Meeting 1 sends home a family build note written for that rank.
- **Tools.** No saws, drills or knives are used at any den meeting. Scouts use pencils, scissors, stickers and markers there. The official build-day pages say only adults may use power tools (band saw, scroll saw, Dremel, drill), with eye and ear protection. They also say that from Tiger up, a scout may cut the block with a handsaw. Lions may not use hand tools at all. The Age Appropriate Guidelines (08/24) agree: hand tools from Tiger up, and power tools for adults only. **Pack 569 (pack rule):**
  - At home, a Tiger-to-AoL scout may cut with a handsaw under close adult supervision. Power tools and drilling are for adults only.
  - A scout who has earned their rank's knife adventure this year (Bear Whittling, AoL Knife Safety) may carve their own car at home under adult supervision. Everyone else leaves carving to an adult.
  - Den meetings never use saws, drills or knives.
- **Which races count.** Every official Race Time page names the Pinewood Derby and the Raingutter Regatta, and a Space Derby doesn't count. **Pack 569 runs no Raingutter Regatta (pack rule), so the pack Pinewood Derby is the race for every rank.**
- **Pack build day.** Pack 569 holds a car build day (date: check). Families can do Req 1's building there. Power tools are for adults only, with eye and ear protection. AoL scouts mentor younger scouts there.
- **All-dens options.** Where two ranks' den nights are the same, a line says how to share them. Each rank's section still stands on its own.
- Two registered adults 21 or over in every den room, and no one-on-one contact. For Lions and Tigers, an adult partner doesn't count as one of the two unless they are registered as a leader (https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/yp-faqs/). If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
- On 2026-09-30 I read all six official Race Time pages and the activity pages listed under each rank on scouting.org, using the r.jina.ai reader. I cross-checked Wolf and AoL against the Michigan Crossroads Council's Oct 2024 requirement PDFs, and AoL against Pack 3087. "Race Time" appears in the electives list of all six ranks in the app's `ADVENTURES`, spelled the same way.

## Race Time (Lion)
- Elective · Meetings: 2 (plus the pack derby) · Official page: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-adventures/race-time-lion/ · Other sources: https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/pinewood-derby-car-build-day-lion/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/raingutter-regatta-build-day-lion/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/arrow-of-light-scouts-explain-the-rules/, https://www.scouting.org/cub-scout-activities/good-sportsmanship-for-the-race/, https://www.scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/leader-resources/pack-meeting-resources/pinewood-derby/, https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/HealthSafety/pdf/680-685.pdf · Checked: 2026-09-30
- Summary: Lions and their adult partners build and race a Pinewood Derby car together. The den designs the car and learns the rules at kit night. The family builds at home: the adult cuts and assembles, and the Lion sands, paints and decorates. At the pit-stop night the den adds finishing touches and plans how to be good sports. The Lion races with the partner at the pack derby.
- Requirements (own words):
  1. With your adult partner, put together and decorate a car or boat. (design and finishing touches: meeting, with the partner there; cutting, sanding, painting and assembly: at home)
  2. Learn the race rules for your car or boat. (meeting)
  3. Before the race, plan good sportsmanship with your adult partner. (meeting, with the partner there; any adult partner counts)
  4. Race in a Pinewood Derby or Regatta with your adult partner. (pack derby)
- Done at the pack derby: Req 4. The Lion races with their adult partner there. The den leader confirms each Lion who raced. Req 3 has to happen before this, at Meeting 2 or at home with the partner.
- Leader's choice for Req 2: Option A · The den leader teaches the rules with picture cards (Meeting 1, step 2). / Option B · The Arrow of Light den visits and explains the rules to small groups of Lions and partners, which is an official Lion activity. It is the same step with the same minutes. It also gives the AoLs credit toward their Race Time Req 3, so ask the AoL den leader to book the night.
- Safety notes:
  - **Lions may not use hand tools.** They sand and paint, and adults cut and assemble (official Lion build-day page). That means no hammers or screwdrivers for Lions, even at home.
  - Power tools are for adults only, with eye and ear protection for anyone nearby.
  - No saws, drills or knives at den meetings.
  - Paint: water-based acrylic with a brush. Spray paint and primer are adult jobs, done outdoors.
  - Graphite, weights and glue are adult jobs (family build note below).
  - Stickers and small parts: keep them on the table, and keep a partner at every Lion's elbow.
  - Food: no food at these meetings. If the derby has snacks, flag allergies to the derby chair (check).
  - Two registered adults in the den room. Partners don't count unless they are registered. No drop-offs. If any girl attends, one of the two registered adults is a woman 21 or over.
- Family build note (send with the kit at Meeting 1):
  - This is your Lion's car, built with you. Your Lion's ideas and hands are what matter, not the finish.
  - **Adult jobs:** all cutting (coping saw, handsaw or power saw, with eye protection for anyone nearby), drilling, gluing in weights, putting on wheels and axles, graphite, and any spray paint or primer.
  - **Lion jobs:** drawing the design, sanding (sandpaper wrapped on a block), brush painting with water-based acrylic, and stickers.
  - **Where to work:** in a garage with the door up, outdoors, or at a table covered in newspaper. Sand outside or over a tray. Everyone washes their hands afterward.
  - **Weights:** tungsten or steel are best. If you use lead, only adults touch it, and everyone washes their hands. Never melt lead at home.
  - **Graphite:** an adult puts it on outdoors or over a box. Keep it away from eyes and noses. Follow the pack's rule on when graphite may be added (check).
  - **Glue:** wood glue is fine with an adult. Super glue, hot glue and epoxy are adult-only, used with the windows open.
  - Bring the car to Meeting 2 on [date] for stickers and a weight check. Car check-in is [date/time] (check).

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Lion Kit Night · 40 min
- All-dens option: the Lion and Tiger dens can hold kit night together in one room. The steps are the same, with each den's leaders running their own pairs.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, get the derby date, the check-in time, the pack rules sheet and the kits (one per Lion plus a spare; see Open questions). A leader builds one finished sample car and brings one uncut block. Make 6–8 picture rule cards, for example: a scale (no heavier than the limit, 5 oz on the official build-day page), a car next to a ruler (must fit the pack's size rules, check), kit wheels only, hands off the track, cars roll on their own with no pushing, and every car gets checked in first. Print an actual-size side-view template of the block. The official build-day page points to one on page 55 of the Lion handbook (check). Prop a smooth board on books to make a short ramp. For Option B, confirm the AoL den's visit.
- Supplies: kits, sample car, uncut block, picture rule cards, templates, crayons, pencils, ramp board, books, family build notes
- Tell parents before they leave: Take the kit and the family build note home. You do the cutting, gluing and wheels. Your Lion sands, paints and decorates. Bring the car to Meeting 2 on [date] for stickers and a weight check. The derby is [date] (check), and you race together.
1. **Meet the Race Car** · den · 7 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "This block of wood is going to be your race car! Grown-ups do the cutting and put on the wheels. Lions do the sanding, painting and decorating."
   - How: 1) Pass around the **uncut block** and the **finished sample car**. 2) Show the **wheels and axle nails**. Adults hold them, because they're small and sharp. 3) Each Lion **feels the sandpaper** and the smooth sample car. 4) Ask, "**Which part is your job?**"
   - Tip: **Small parts:** Wheels and nails stay in the kit bag. Only an adult opens it.
2. **Race Rules, Lion Style** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "Races have rules so they're fair for everyone. Let's learn ours."
   - How: **Option A · den leader:** 1) Hold up one **picture card** at a time. 2) Everyone **acts it out**: hands off the track means hands behind your back, and cars roll on their own means making a whoosh sound. 3) Partners **quiz their Lion** with the cards. / **Option B · AoL visit:** 1) The AoLs split the room into **small groups** of Lion-and-partner pairs. 2) Each AoL **explains the rules** with the picture cards. 3) Lions **say one rule back**. The den leader and a second registered adult stay in the room, and the AoLs never take a Lion aside.
   - Tip: **Note Req 2** for each Lion who can show you one or two rules with the cards.
3. **Design My Car** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "What should your car look like? Draw it on the block picture. You're the designer!"
   - How: 1) Each pair gets a **template**. 2) The Lion **draws a shape and colors**, and the partner helps trace a clean line. 3) The partner writes "**cut here**" notes for home. 4) Two or three Lions **show their design**.
   - Tip: **Keep it simple:** A wedge or a gentle slope is easy to cut and fast. The Lion's idea wins, even if it's a hot dog.
4. **Ramp Roll** · den · 4 min · Reqs: 2 (set-up)
   - Say: "Watch the sample car go. Nobody pushes it. It rolls by itself!"
   - How: 1) A leader **lets it go** at the top. 2) Lions **count down** "3, 2, 1, go!" 3) Do it two or three times.
   - Tip: **Hands off:** This is the "cars roll on their own" rule in action.
5. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Lions, pick up three things with your grown-up."
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 2) Put a **kit and family build note** in each partner's hand. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Count kits back:** Write down who took one.
   - (at home) Req 1: the adult cuts and assembles, and the Lion sands and paints.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Pit Stop and Good Sports · 38 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, remind families to bring the car, even if it isn't finished. Bring a digital kitchen scale, the ramp, sticker sheets and number stickers, and water-based markers. Write four role-play cards in your own words from the official activity's themes: a wheel falls off during your race, a car goes off the track, someone adds extra weight that isn't allowed, and winning and losing.
- Supplies: stickers, water-based markers, digital scale, ramp, role-play cards, index cards, paper car cutouts for Lions without a car
- Tell parents before they leave: Please finish the car at home. If the scale said your car is light, an adult adds weight. Check-in is [date/time] and the derby is [date] (check). Race day is together: you sit with your Lion.
1. **Show Your Car** · den · 6 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Hold up your car and tell us one thing YOU did to it."
   - How: 1) Each pair **stands up**. 2) The Lion names **one job**, like "I sanded it" or "I painted the stripes." 3) The den gives a **roar**. 4) Note who has **built with their partner**.
   - Tip: **No car yet?** The Lion decorates a paper car tonight, and the pair finishes the real one at home.
2. **Sticker Pit Stop** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Pit stop! Add your stickers and your number, and then we'll weigh your car."
   - How: 1) Lions add **stickers and marker details**, with the partner helping. 2) An adult **weighs each car** and writes the weight on an index card for the partner. 3) An adult **spins each wheel** and notes any that rub.
   - Tip: **Heavy stickers?** Decorations count toward the weight. An adult fixes any loose wheels at home, not in the den.
3. **Good Sport Pretend** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Being a good sport means being friendly and kind whether you win or lose. Let's practice!"
   - How: 1) Read a **role-play card**. 2) Two pairs **act it out**, for example a Lion whose car lost gives a high five. 3) After every card, ask "**What would a good sport do?**" 4) Each partner and Lion **agree on one promise**, like "We'll cheer for every car."
   - Tip: **Tie it to the Scout Law:** Friendly, Courteous and Kind. The promise made with the partner here is Req 3.
4. **Race Day Walk-Through** · den · 5 min · Reqs: 4 (set-up)
   - Say: "On race day, your car gets checked in, then it races, and we cheer for EVERY car."
   - How: 1) Walk through **check-in**, the **track** and the **cheering section**. 2) Practice **one den cheer**. 3) Remind everyone that the **Lion stays with their partner**.
   - Tip: **Tears happen:** Partners can plan a hug and a "you did your best."
5. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Race cars home safe! Pick up three things first."
   - How: 1) **Clean up**. 2) Each partner gets their car's **weight card**. 3) **Check out** each Lion with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Carry case:** A shoebox with a paper towel keeps wheels safe on the ride home.
   - (at home) Req 1: finish the car, with the adult handling weights and graphite.

The official Tiger page lists paintbrushes, scissors, hammers and screwdrivers, so a Tiger can tap in axle nails with training and an adult's help. The den learns the rules with a matching game, designs the car, and writes a Race Day Promise with partners before the pack derby.

  - **Tiger jobs, with you beside them:** the design, sanding, brush painting with water-based acrylic, decorating, and tapping in axle nails with a small hammer while you hold the car.
  - **Where to work:** a garage with the door up, outdoors, or a covered table. Sand outside. Everyone washes their hands.
  - **Weights:** tungsten or steel are best. With lead, only adults handle it, and everyone washes their hands. Never melt lead.

### Meeting 1 of 2 · Tiger Kit Night · 40 min
- All-dens option: the Tiger and Lion dens can share kit night. The steps are the same, but Tigers play the memory game, while Lions use the picture cards or an AoL visit.
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, get the derby date, the pack rules sheet and the kits (see Open questions). Pick 5–6 kit rules and 5–6 pack rules, as the official Race Memory activity suggests. Write each rule twice on index cards, adding a simple picture so non-readers can play, for example a scale for "5 oz or less" (weight from the official build-day page) and a crossed-out hand for "hands off the track." Print an actual-size side-view template. The official page points to page 45 of the Tiger handbook (check). Bring a sample car and a ramp board.
- Supplies: kits, sample car, memory cards, templates, crayons, pencils, ramp board, books, family build notes
- Tell parents before they leave: The kit and family build note go home with you. You cut and drill. Your Tiger sands, paints and helps tap in the axles. Bring the car to Meeting 2 on [date]. The derby is [date] (check).
1. **Meet the Race Car** · den · 6 min · Reqs: 1 (set-up)
   - Say: "Here's a block, and here's a finished car. What had to happen in between?"
   - How: 1) Pass around the **block** and the **sample car**. 2) Adults show the **wheels, axle nails and weights**. 3) Tigers guess the **steps**: draw, cut, sand, paint, wheels, weigh. 4) Point out which are **grown-up jobs**.
   - Tip: **Nails are sharp:** They stay in the kit bag.
2. **Rule Match** · den · 13 min · Reqs: 2
   - Say: "It's a memory game, but every card is a race rule. Flip two, and if they match, read the rule to your partner!"
   - How: 1) Lay the cards **face down** on the floor. 2) Pairs take turns **flipping two**. 3) On a match, the partner reads it and the **Tiger says what it means**. 4) **Play twice**, as the official activity suggests.
   - Tip: **Note Req 2** for Tigers who can explain two or three rules at the end.
3. **Design My Car** · den · 12 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Draw your car on the block picture. Think about the shape and the colors."
   - How: 1) Tigers **draw a shape** on the template. 2) The partner marks the **cut line** for home. 3) Tigers **color the paint plan**. 4) Volunteers **show** their design.
   - Tip: **Speed tip for later:** Keep the back end thick, because that's where grown-ups usually add weight.
4. **Ramp Roll** · den · 4 min · Reqs: 2 (set-up)
   - Say: "No pushing in a real race. Let's watch gravity do the work."
   - How: 1) Roll the **sample car** down the board. 2) Tigers **count down**. 3) Ask, "**Who pushed it?**" Nobody!
   - Tip: **Keep it short:** Tomorrow's cars go home in bags, not onto the ramp.
5. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Tigers, stack the cards and clear the floor."
   - How: 1) Do a **two-minute clean-up**. 2) Hand the **kit and build note** to each partner. 3) **Check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Kit list:** Note who took a kit.
   - (at home) Req 1: build together.

### Meeting 2 of 2 · Pit Stop and Race Day Promise · 39 min
- Prep: 1–2 weeks before, remind families to bring the car, finished or not. Print the official Race Day Promise handout (linked from the My Race Day Promise activity), or make your own sheet with three blanks: how we'll cheer, how we'll be kind, and what we'll say. Bring a digital scale, stickers and a ramp. Write role-play cards on the official themes: a wheel falls off, a car leaves the track, extra weight that isn't allowed, and winning and losing.
- Supplies: stickers, water-based markers, digital scale, ramp, role-play cards, Race Day Promise sheets, pencils, crayons, index cards
- Tell parents before they leave: Finish the car at home, with the adult adding weight and graphite. Put the Race Day Promise on the fridge. Check-in is [date/time] and the derby is [date] (check), and you sit with your Tiger.
1. **Show Your Car** · den · 6 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Show us your car and tell us one job you did yourself."
   - How: 1) Each pair **shows the car**. 2) The Tiger names a **job**. 3) Note who has **built with their partner**.
   - Tip: **Not finished?** It's fine. Finishing is a family job.
2. **Pit Stop** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 1
   - Say: "Pit crew time! Stickers and numbers on, then weigh-in."
   - How: 1) Tigers **decorate** with the partner. 2) An adult **weighs** each car and writes the weight on a card. 3) An adult **spins each wheel**.
   - Tip: **Loose wheel?** Fix it at home, not here.
3. **Act Like a Good Sport** · den · 8 min · Reqs: 3 (set-up)
   - Say: "What does a good sport do when their car wins? When it loses? When a wheel falls off?"
   - How: 1) Read a **role-play card**. 2) Two pairs **act it out**. 3) The den says what a **good sport** would do. 4) Do all **four cards**.
   - Tip: **Scout Law link:** Honest racers don't add extra weight, because a Scout is Trustworthy.
4. **Race Day Promise** · den · 10 min · Reqs: 3
   - Say: "Now make your own promise with your grown-up: how you'll cheer, how you'll be kind, and what you'll say."
   - How: 1) Pairs **talk it over**. 2) The Tiger **draws or dictates**, and the partner writes. 3) Two or three pairs **read theirs**.
   - Tip: **This is Req 3:** It's done "before the race" with the partner, so note each pair who finishes.
5. **Clean-Up & Check-Out** · closing · 5 min · Reqs: none
   - Say: "Promises in your bag, cars in their box. Let's clean up!"
   - How: 1) **Clean up**. 2) Hand the **weight card and promise** to each partner. 3) **Check out** each Tiger with their partner by name.
   - Tip: **Carry box:** A shoebox protects the wheels.
   - (at home) Req 1: finish the car.
