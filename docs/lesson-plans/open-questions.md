## 1. Safety & Youth Protection

1. **Disclosure steps.** Confirm what the pack does when a scout discloses abuse, and what Georgia requires adults to report. Den leaders need this before they run Safety in Numbers.
   Affects: Wolf (Safety in Numbers).

2. **Two registered adults in every den room.** ANSWERED 2026-09-30: more den leaders are signing up and taking the training. The two-registered-adults-per-den-room rule stays in every guide.

3. **Adult partners who aren't parents (Lions and Tigers).** Does a partner who doesn't live with the scout need an adult application, and how do the restroom rules work for a grandparent? Overnight, the official rules say a parent or guardian, and only a parent or guardian and siblings may share the tent. May a Lion or Tiger camp with a non-parent partner, and where would that pair sleep?
   Suggested: parent or guardian overnight, any partner in the daytime.
   Affects: Lion, Tiger, Let's Camp.

4. **Food allergies and EpiPens.** If a scout carries an EpiPen, at least one other adult must know how to use it. Should that rule go in the pack's standard outing checklist? If the derby has snacks, who checks for allergies?
   Suggested: add it to the checklist.
   Affects: Wolf (Paws on the Path), Race Time.

5. **Firem'n Chit for AoL.** Does the pack offer the Firem'n Chit to AoL scouts before their campout? Until they have it, adults handle all fire.
   Affects: AoL (Outdoor Adventurer).

6. **Knives and hand tools.** Confirm these rules: an AoL scout carries a pocketknife only after earning Knife Safety this year, and matches stay home on rides. Wolves and Bears may turn an Allen key while an adult holds the bike, and Lions and Tigers never hold tools. Will the AoL patrol earn Knife Safety before its campout so scouts can do their own cutting? Also check that Webelos Chef's Knife doesn't count for derby carving.
   Suggested: yes, the same as the Bear Whittling rule.
   Affects: Cycling, AoL, Race Time.

7. **Eye protection when casting.** Everyone in the casting area wears sunglasses or safety glasses that families supply, and a scout without them doesn't cast. Is that OK?
   Suggested: yes, as drafted.
   Affects: Fishing.

8. **Family swims.** The Lion, Tiger and Bear pages say every swim must be supervised by an adult trained in Safe Swim Defense. Will the Advancement Chair accept a family swim at a pool with lifeguards when the parent hasn't taken that training? Should Webelos and AoL, whose pages name no family setting, have a family path at all?
   Affects: Swimming.
